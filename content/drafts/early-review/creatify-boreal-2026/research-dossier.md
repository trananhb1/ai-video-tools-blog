## Primary findings

Creatify describes Boreal as a new LTX-based general video model for text-to-video and image-to-video, strongest on short clips and simple scenes.[5]

The page advertises approximately US$0.01 per generated second and about US$0.05 for a five-second clip, but nearby copy incorrectly refers to Aurora billing and says final figures still require confirmation before launch.[4][5]

Those contradictions make the price provisional rather than verified cost evidence.[4][5]

Image-to-video support makes Boreal relevant to AIVP's exact-product workflow, but AIVP has no evidence that an input product image remains identifiable by shape, labels, colourway and accessories through the first 10 seconds.[5]

Creatify's terms include commercial-use language for compliant Community Assets, but the retrieved text does not resolve Boreal-on-fal output ownership, provider-specific terms, input retention or complete monetized-video rights.[6]
