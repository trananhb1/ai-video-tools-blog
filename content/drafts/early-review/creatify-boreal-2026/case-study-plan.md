# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Creatify Boreal

**Status:** PLAN — access, rights, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Boreal animate rights-cleared reference images of LED face mask ASIN B0D2XTZN8V into a recognizable 9:16 affiliate clip at a measured cost of no more than US$0.10 per usable five-second shot, while preserving exact product identity across three runs?

## 1. Thesis and locked brief

Boreal's strongest AIVP-relevant claim is low-cost image-to-video for short, simple scenes. Freeze one rights-cleared hero image set for Amazon ASIN `B0D2XTZN8V`, the prompt, seed where supported, duration, 9:16 framing and output resolution.

Prompt: “Slow close-up product reveal of the exact supplied white LED face mask on a neutral vanity. Preserve shell shape, eye and mouth cut-outs, controller, cable, colourway and red/blue LED pattern. Gentle camera push-in; no added text, logos, people, accessories or medical claims.”

Control: the current Dreamina reference-image workflow using the same source assets and shot brief.

## 2. Three bounded runs

1. Baseline with the default Boreal image-to-video settings.
2. Best documented identity-preserving settings without paid enhancement or hidden extras.
3. Exact repeat of run 2 to measure identity and motion drift.

Record endpoint/model version, source hashes, exact prompt/settings/seed, queue and render timing, displayed and consumed cost, response metadata, output MP4/hash, retries and acceptance reason.

## 3. Acceptance and stop conditions

**PASS only if all three:** exact mask is visible from frame one and remains distinctive for the full shot by shell, openings, controller/cable and LED pattern; no substitute product or fabricated label; native 9:16 output; usable motion with no severe deformation; ≤60 seconds wall time per five-second shot; measured cost ≤US$0.10 per usable shot; monetized editorial/affiliate use explicitly permitted.

**FAIL:** identity drift in any run, generic mask substitution, invented claim/label/accessory, unusable motion, unavailable vertical output, >60 seconds, >US$0.10 per accepted shot or unclear rights.

**STOP immediately:** any payment or top-up prompt, pay-per-use generation without Tom's explicit cost approval, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature step, unclear input rights, unclear monetized-output rights, or unexpected API permission/egress.

## 4. Evidence package

Source/claim ledger; dated endpoint, pricing and terms snapshots; rights record and source hashes; screenshots of model/settings/displayed cost; complete API request/response with secrets removed; all three outputs including failures and SHA-256; frames at 0:00/mid/final; exact-product comparison sheet; timing/retry/cost ledger; pre/post scorecard.

## 5. Publishable outputs

Evidence-led review/case study only after passing; low-cost video-model comparison module; behind-the-scenes Short; transparent methodology/access/affiliate disclosure. Existing Creatify affiliate CTA may be used only if it routes to the tested product/workflow and tracking is re-verified before publication.

## 6. Vendor boundary and decision

Vendor may provide bounded access, documentation, approved media and factual corrections, but receives no editorial control, pre-publication approval, guaranteed rating, recommendation or placement. Access does not guarantee positive coverage.

`RETEST` — verify a live Boreal endpoint, zero-cost trial allowance and Boreal/fal commercial rights before executing the three runs.
