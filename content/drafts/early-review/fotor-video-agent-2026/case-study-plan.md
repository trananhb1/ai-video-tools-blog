# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Fotor Video Agent

**Status:** PLAN — access, rights, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Fotor Video Agent turn the real Amazon listing and rights-cleared reference images for LED face mask ASIN B0D2XTZN8V into a 30-second vertical affiliate video that preserves exact product identity in the first 10 seconds, exports without manual timeline reconstruction, and costs US$0 across three runs?

## 1. Thesis and locked brief

Fotor's strongest AIVP-relevant claim is product-link/photo-to-video generation with an editable timeline. Freeze the Amazon product URL for ASIN `B0D2XTZN8V`, rights-cleared reference images, price `$49.99`, and script points: red and blue LED modes, at-home skincare use, price callout, and neutral “check current listing details” CTA. Do not add medical-treatment claims.

Output: 30-second 1080×1920, 30fps H.264 MP4, clear English narration, licensed audio, no watermark. The exact mask must be visible by 0:03 and distinctive throughout the first 10 seconds by shell shape, eye/mouth cut-outs, colourway, LED pattern, controller and included accessories. Control: Dreamina/reference-image workflow using the same assets and script.

## 2. Three bounded runs

1. Baseline with default Video Agent product-link workflow.
2. Best documented product-photo/link workflow with frozen prompt and timeline settings.
3. Exact repeat of run 2 to measure identity drift and editability.

Record account/tier, tool/model/version, exact inputs and prompt, generated script/storyboard, settings, start/end and render times, displayed/consumed credits, export path/SHA-256, retries and acceptance reason.

## 3. Acceptance and stop conditions

**PASS only if all three:** exact product appears by 0:03 and remains distinctive through 0:10; no substitute mask or fabricated specification; correct `$49.99` source price clearly time-qualified; 1080×1920 MP4; no watermark; narration and audio commercially cleared; ≤20 minutes total per run; US$0/no paid credits; usable after native timeline edits only.

**FAIL:** generic or substituted product, identity drift, invented medical/specification claim, unreadable product/price, manual rebuild in another editor, export failure, >20 minutes, or any cost.

**STOP:** payment/subscription prompt, paid credits, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature step, unclear source-image rights, unclear complete-output monetization rights, or unexpected integration permission/egress.

## 4. Evidence package

Source ledger; listing snapshot and rights record; input hashes; access/tier and displayed-cost screenshots; prompt/storyboard/timeline exports; all three outputs including failures with SHA-256; QC frames at 0:00/0:03/0:10/mid/final; exact-product comparison sheet; timing/retry/credit ledger; complete video/audio rights confirmation; pre/post scorecard.

## 5. Publishable outputs

Evidence-led review/case study only after passing; product-video buyer-guide module; behind-the-scenes Short; methodology and access disclosure; no affiliate CTA before programme acceptance and live tracking verification.

## 6. Vendor boundary and decision

Vendor may provide documentation, bounded access, media and factual corrections, but receives no editorial control, pre-publication approval, guaranteed rating, recommendation or placement. Access does not guarantee positive coverage.

`RETEST` — obtain bounded zero-cost access and explicit complete-video/audio commercial rights, then execute the three locked runs.
