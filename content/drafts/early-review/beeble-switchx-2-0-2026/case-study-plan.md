# Case Study Plan: Beeble SwitchX 2.0

**Status:** PLAN — cost/access gate active
**Classification:** Monitor only (36/100, confidence C)

## Business question

Can SwitchX 2.0 transform a rights-cleared 10-second LED-mask source clip into three usable 9:16 shots while preserving exact product identity in every first-10-second frame for no more than US$2 per accepted output?

## Locked brief

Use the rights-cleared $49.99 LED face mask asset set (ASIN B0D2XTZN8V) and one fixed 10-second source clip. Replace only the background and relight for red/blue skincare use. Export 1080×1920 H.264. Keep source, mask, prompt, model and seed fixed where supported.

## Three runs

1. Default documented settings.
2. Vendor-recommended mask/tracking settings.
3. Exact repeat of run 2 for consistency.

Record settings, timestamps, queue/render duration, displayed and consumed credits, retries, outputs, SHA-256 and first/mid/final-frame QC for every run, including failures.

## Pass

- Exact silhouette, cut-outs, colourway and red/blue glow remain recognizable through the first 10 seconds.
- Three of three exports have no material mask-edge or geometry defect.
- Each run completes within 5 minutes and costs no more than US$2 per accepted output.
- Monetized use and attribution requirements are confirmed.

## Fail / stop

Fail on product drift, a failed first-10-seconds gate, or unclear commercial rights. Stop immediately at a payment prompt, unapproved paid-credit use, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature request, or unclear input rights.

## Evidence package

Frozen inputs and rights record; screenshots; settings export; all outputs; hashes; frame QC; timing/credit ledger; terms snapshot; pre/post scorecard and control delta.

**Current decision:** RETEST after no-cost access is secured. No affiliate CTA before approval and tracking verification.
