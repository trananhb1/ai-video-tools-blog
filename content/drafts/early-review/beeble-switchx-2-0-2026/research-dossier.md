## Executive decision

Beeble announced SwitchX 2.0 with 4K output, 10-bit colour, clips up to 20 seconds, improved camera tracking and stronger source tracking for fast motion.[1] Stage a docs-only First Look, but do not recommend it for product-affiliate work until three runs prove exact LED-mask identity in the first 10 seconds.
