## Verified Current State

Runway currently displays Free with 125 one-time credits; Standard at US$15 monthly or US$12/month billed annually with 625 monthly credits; Pro at US$35 monthly or US$28/month billed annually with 2,250 credits; and Max at US$95 monthly or US$76/month billed annually with 9,500 credits.[1] The page says Gen-4.5 costs 12 credits per generated second and identifies Max—not Unlimited—as the highest self-serve individual tier.[1]

AIVP's live Runway review still says Gen-4.5 costs 25 credits per second and describes a US$76 Unlimited tier with unlimited Gen-3 Turbo generations.[3] Free, Standard and Pro headline prices remain broadly aligned, but those model-rate and top-tier statements are materially stale.

Runway's terms say it does not claim ownership of user inputs or outputs and does not restrict commercial use of outputs when the user complies with the agreement; the same section grants Runway a broad licence to use inputs and outputs for service and model-improvement purposes.[2] That supports a commercial-rights floor but does not validate output quality, exact-product preservation, reliability or cost per accepted output.
