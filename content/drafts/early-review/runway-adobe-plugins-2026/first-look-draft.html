Runway Plugins for Premiere Pro and After Effects: First Look

Runway announced its Adobe plugin on September 8, 2026. The panel generates images and video, restyles selected footage with Aleph 2, upscales, removes backgrounds and places results back into Premiere Pro or After Effects without the usual browser export and re-import loop.[1][2]

What is actually new?

The differentiation is workflow location, not a new foundation model. Runway says Gen-4.5, Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0 Pro and Veo 3.1 are available for video inside the panel, while selected clips can be re-rendered with Aleph 2 and processed with Ruby.[2]

Adobe separately introduced a Generative Media Tool that selects Adobe Firefly or partner models including Runway directly in Premiere.[6] The announcements overlap, but Runway documents its own installable panel and workspace-credit behavior.

Pricing and access

The installer is free for macOS and Windows, but generation requires a paid Runway plan and consumes the signed-in workspace credits.[1][2] Runway publishes a one-time 125-credit Free plan and recurring credit allocations on paid plans.[3]

Commercial use

Runway says it does not claim ownership of user inputs or outputs and does not restrict commercial use of outputs when the user complies with the agreement.[4] Because the panel exposes several partner models, AIVP still needs written clarification that the same rights treatment applies to every model and plugin workflow tested.

The test that matters

Our planned test uses one rights-cleared LED-mask clip and compares browser generation plus re-import against two identical plugin runs. The plugin passes only if it reduces end-to-end time by at least 25%, uses no extra credits and preserves the exact mask, controller, labels, colourway and red/blue glow throughout the first 10 seconds.
