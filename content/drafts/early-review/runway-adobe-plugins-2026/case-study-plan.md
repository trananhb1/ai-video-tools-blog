# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Runway Plugins for Adobe

**Status:** PLAN — cost, access, rights and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Weak fit on verified evidence; promising workflow specialist pending testing  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can the Runway plugin cut round-trip production time while preserving an exact affiliate product better than the same Runway workflow in a browser?

## 1. Falsifiable thesis

Using identical rights-cleared footage and the same supported model, the Premiere Pro plugin can produce and place an accepted 9:16 product shot at least 25% faster than Runway web export/download/re-import, without changing product identity, increasing consumed credits, or requiring corrective re-editing.

## 2. Locked real AIVP scenario

Use one AIVP-owned 10-second 9:16 clip of the US$49.99 LED face mask, with the controller, labels, mask geometry and red/blue emitters visible. Freeze the source SHA-256, prompt, model/version, duration, aspect ratio, resolution, Premiere project and export preset before Run 1. The control is the same model and prompt through Runway web, followed by download and manual import at the playhead.

The visual hard gate is exact product distinctiveness throughout the first 10 seconds. The plugin cannot receive credit merely for showing a generic LED mask.

## 3. Three bounded runs

1. **Baseline:** browser control using the locked source, model, prompt and settings.
2. **Plugin:** identical generation/edit through the Premiere panel, placed at the locked playhead.
3. **Plugin repeat:** repeat Run 2 unchanged to measure drift and reliability.

Record exact input and source hash, plugin/build and Adobe version, account/workspace, model/settings, start/end time, queue/render/download/import duration, displayed and consumed credits, retries/errors, output path and SHA-256, and first/mid/final-frame QC.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

Pass only if both plugin runs:

- keep the exact mask, controller, labels, colourway and red/blue glow distinctive in the first 10 seconds;
- introduce no material geometry, text, skin or accessory drift;
- land at the correct playhead and remain editable without a repair export;
- use no more credits than the browser control;
- complete at least 25% faster end to end than the browser control; and
- export a valid H.264 MP4 at 1080×1920 with clear rights evidence.

Fail on generic-product substitution, identity drift, wrong placement, broken export, hidden credit surcharge, fewer than two accepted plugin outputs, or failure to beat the locked time threshold.

## 5. Stop conditions

Stop immediately on payment or top-up, consumption outside a vendor-granted free review allowance, password/2FA/identity/payment request, unapproved terms acceptance, unclear input rights, unclear model-specific commercial rights, or a first-run product-identity failure that the identical locked prompt cannot correct without changing the test.

## 6. Evidence package

Retain source assets and rights record, source/settings/output hashes, screenshots of installation/account/model/settings/displayed cost, all three outputs including failures, timing and credit ledger, first/mid/final frame contact sheet, Premiere project/export metadata, terms snapshot, and before/after scorecard.

## 7. Publishable outputs

If the test passes, prepare an evidence-led case study, a Runway review update, an Adobe workflow comparison module and a 30–60 second behind-the-scenes Short. Disclose review access and methodology. Add no affiliate CTA until programme acceptance and tracking are verified.

## 8. Vendor boundary

Runway may provide documentation, bounded access, media and factual corrections, but receives no editorial control, pre-publication approval, guaranteed rating, recommendation or affiliate placement.
