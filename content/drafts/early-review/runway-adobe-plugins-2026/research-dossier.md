## Primary-source finding

Runway announced a free-to-download plugin panel for Premiere Pro and After Effects that can generate images and video, restyle selected footage with Aleph 2, upscale, remove backgrounds and place results back into the active timeline.[1][2] Generation is not free: Runway says it is available on paid plans and consumes the existing workspace credit balance.[1]

Adobe announced its own Generative Media Tool on the same date and named Runway among the selectable partner models, so the two announcements concern the same broader in-timeline AI workflow but document distinct vendor surfaces.[6] This dossier treats the Runway plugin as one deduplicated Runway event, not as a second Adobe launch.

## Rights, cost and product gates

Runway says it does not claim ownership of user inputs or outputs and does not restrict commercial use of outputs subject to compliance with its agreement; users remain responsible for the necessary rights in their content.[4] This is useful general rights evidence, not a substitute for confirming rights for every model exposed through the multi-model plugin.

Runway publishes a one-time 125-credit Free plan, while its paid plans provide recurring credits and model-specific consumption rates.[3] The plugin announcement says generation requires a paid plan and existing credits.[1] Hands-on testing is therefore **cost-blocked** unless Runway grants bounded review credits or Tom approves use of an existing paid allowance.

For product-affiliate use, selected footage must show the exact product before the plugin touches it, and every accepted output must preserve product geometry, colourway, labels, controls and accessories in the first 10 seconds. No recommendation is permitted until three artifact-backed runs pass that gate.
