# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Krea Agent

**Status:** PLAN — access, source rights, output rights, credentials and spend gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Promising/conditional  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Krea Agent take one rights-cleared AIVP product reference photograph and a fixed text brief, autonomously plan and generate six static 1080x1080 social campaign images and one 15-second 9:16 vertical video ad, in under 15 minutes at zero incremental cost on the free/trial allowance, while preserving the exact product identity throughout the video?

## 1. Case-study thesis

Krea Agent delivers tangible creator value only if its automated multi-model orchestration eliminates manual switching across image, video, and editing tools without producing hallucinations, distorted brand assets, or excessive compute burn.

## 2. Locked real-product brief

- **Audience:** Australian creators, video editors, and SMB affiliate marketers.
- **Input:** Rights-cleared 2000x2000 PNG product master (Revlon Plus 2.0 hair dryer brush) and a standardized 60-word promotional prompt brief.
- **Desired Outputs:**
  - 6 distinct static social ad variations (1:1 aspect ratio, consistent brand palette).
  - 1 vertical 9:16 15-second video ad with clear product focal point in the first 3 seconds.
  - Context folder storing intermediate assets and metadata.

## 3. Falsifiable test protocol

1. **Protocol:** Three identical runs executed from fresh sessions at 1-hour intervals.
2. **Execution boundaries:** Free tier / trial compute credits only; zero payment details entered; no paid add-ons consumed.
3. **Pass Criteria:**
   - Run time <= 15 minutes per session.
   - All 6 static images correctly rendered with recognizable product geometry.
   - 15-second video maintains product geometry without shape distortion for at least 10 consecutive seconds.
   - Zero out-of-pocket spend incurred.

## 4. Evidence artifacts to collect

- Prompt brief text and exact timestamps.
- Raw exported image files and vertical MP4 video files.
- Compute unit balance before and after each run.
- Screenshot of the shared context folder and agent generation log.
