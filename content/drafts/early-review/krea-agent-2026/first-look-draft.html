Krea Agent First Look: Creative Production Agent Orchestrating 150+ Models

On September 10, 2026, Krea launched Krea Agent across all subscription tiers, extending autonomous agentic orchestration to its generative media suite.[1] Rather than requiring creators to navigate discrete image, video, and upscaling tools individually, Krea Agent accepts high-level briefs in natural language and coordinates the entire asset production lifecycle.[2] Krea explicitly differentiates this conversational production agent from its earlier node-graph canvas tool.[3]

Multi-Step Creative Orchestration

Krea Agent functions as a workflow director: given a product photo and marketing objective, it plans the necessary generation steps, selects models from Krea's catalog, generates assets, reviews quality, and files deliverables in a persistent context library.[2][6] Default generation models include Nano Banana Pro for reference-following, ChatGPT Image 2 for text-heavy layouts, Krea 2 for exploration, Seedance 2.5 for video, and Topaz for upscaling.[2][6] Orchestration is handled by frontier language models, with selectable effort levels controlling the depth of automated planning and review.[6][7]

Availability and Compute Pricing

The agent is accessible at krea.ai/agent on all plans, including the Free tier.[1][2] Generations consume standard compute units rather than a separate agent surcharge.[7] Subscription tiers include Basic, Pro, Max, and Enterprise plans with scaled compute allocations and model selection privileges.[7][8] Launch-week free credits expired on September 15, 2026, returning usage to regular plan quotas.[1][7] Commercial generation rights are included on paid subscription plans under standard terms, while media and partnership inquiries route to the published press contact.[8][12][13]

AIVP Assessment & Limitations

While multi-model agentic pipelines represent an ambitious step forward from standalone generators, key operational factors remain unverified: multi-run consistency, product retention during video rendering, and latency under multi-step workflows. Because testing requires an authenticated session and consumes compute units, controlled evaluation is held pending credential and spend authorization. A structured three-run case study is planned under the Early Review Desk protocol.
