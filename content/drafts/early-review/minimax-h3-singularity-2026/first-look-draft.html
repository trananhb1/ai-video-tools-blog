Minimax-h3_Singularity First Look: Promising H3 Fine-Tune, Serious Fidelity Warning

This community H3 derivative expands ComfyUI workflows, but an early reference test reported exact-object drift and the license chain needs clarification.

What launched

The model card presents a community fine-tuned H3 fusion supporting text-, image-, reference- and video-to-video workflows in ComfyUI, with a recommended four-step acceleration LoRA.[9]

Not an official MiniMax release

MiniMax’s official H3 is a 33B model with local 768p generation and API-assisted context and 2K stages.[11] The Singularity derivative must be evaluated and attributed separately.

Product-specificity red flag

A user reported that a custom truck reference was overwritten before animation; the author linked that behavior to aggressive early diffusion and suggested a LoRA mitigation.[22] That blocks any current product-affiliate recommendation.

Rights warning

The derivative card displays Apache-2.0 while official H3 points to a MiniMax community license.[9][11] A monetised recommendation requires written clarification of the complete derivative, base, LoRA and output-rights chain.
