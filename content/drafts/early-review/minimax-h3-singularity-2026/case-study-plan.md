# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Minimax-h3_Singularity

**Status:** PLAN — rights, compute and author-contact gates unresolved  
**Confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can this community H3 derivative beat official MiniMax H3 on fast exact-product reference video without overwriting the product?

## Falsifiable thesis
With identical rights-cleared inputs and fixed seed/prompt, Minimax-h3_Singularity plus its recommended four-step LoRA produces at least two acceptable 9:16 product clips out of three, preserves the exact product in the first 10 seconds better than official H3, and stays within US$2 effective compute cost per accepted clip.

## Locked scenario and control
Use AIVP-owned footage/images of a distinctive product with labels, colourway, controls and accessories. Control is the official MiniMax H3 Ref2V workflow at matched duration/resolution. Lock prompt, seed, input hashes and QC rubric before setup.

## Three bounded runs
1. Derivative at documented default settings.
2. Derivative with recommended four-step LoRA at a pre-locked strength.
3. Repeat the better derivative setting; run the official-H3 control with the same inputs separately.

Record license snapshots, input rights/hashes, exact files/commits/model versions, ComfyUI graph, seeds/settings, hardware, start/end and render times, energy/host cost estimate, all outputs/failures, frame contact sheets, product-identity QC and score delta.

## Evidence Package
Retain rights confirmation, source assets and hashes, exact tool/model version and settings, all three outputs including failures, timing and cost logs, first/mid/final frame QC, commercial-rights evidence, and the score delta versus control.

## Pass/fail and stops
Pass only if exact product is distinctive in the first 10 seconds, no defining feature is overwritten, at least two derivative outputs pass, and the derivative beats the control on predeclared identity and usable-output metrics. Fail on reference overwrite, fewer than two accepted outputs, unsupported license chain, unstable graph or cost above ceiling.

Stop on gated-license acceptance, paid compute, account/credentials, unapproved download execution, unclear dataset/input/output rights, or hardware/resource risk.

## Deliverables
Plan a reproducible case-study article, clearly labelled community-model First Look, open-model buyer-guide module and behind-the-scenes Short. Include hashes/methodology/disclosures. No affiliate CTA unless a programme is accepted and tracking verified.

`RETEST` — resolve the derivative/base license chain and author contact before allocating compute.
