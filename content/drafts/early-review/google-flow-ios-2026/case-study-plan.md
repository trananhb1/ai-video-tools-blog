# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Google Flow for iOS

**Status:** PLAN — free-credit, rights, device and publication gates apply
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only pending mobile testing
**Evidence confidence:** C
**Business question:** Can Google Flow on iPhone produce three usable eight-second vertical product hooks from the same LED-mask reference while preserving exact identity, using no more than 30 free credits and six minutes median elapsed time?

## 1. Case-study thesis

The iPhone app is production-useful only if camera-roll intake and background generation reduce mobile friction without weakening exact-product identity or silently multiplying credit usage.

## 2. Real AIVP scenario

- **Audience:** Australian creators and SMBs.
- **Input:** rights-cleared hero image of Amazon product B0D2XTZN8V, AIVP's $49.99 LED face mask.
- **Output:** three eight-second 9:16 clips exported to the iPhone; no text or narration.
- **Control:** identical source, prompt, model and duration through Flow desktop.
- **Product gate:** exact mask shape, eye/mouth openings, colourway and red/blue LED pattern remain distinctive for the full clip.

## 3. Locked brief and runs

**Prompt:** `Create an eight-second vertical close-up product hook. Preserve the exact face-mask shape, eye and mouth openings, controls and colourway from the supplied reference. Begin with red LEDs illuminated, transition to blue LEDs, slow push-in camera, clean neutral background, no text, no extra products, no redesign.`

Freeze app version 1.552.0, Veo 3.1 Lite, source image, prompt, eight-second duration and 9:16 if available.

1. **Run 1 — Baseline:** default documented mobile workflow.
2. **Run 2 — Repeatability:** identical inputs and settings.
3. **Run 3 — Repeatability:** identical inputs and settings.

Before each generation, capture the displayed generation count and debit. Record timestamps, queue/render duration, credits, retries, export path, SHA-256 and QC.

## 4. Strength criteria tested

Product specificity, output quality, control/consistency, workflow strength, speed/reliability, cost per accepted clip, export behavior and commercial rights.

## 5. Acceptance and stop conditions

**Pass:** 3/3 exports; product recognizable throughout; 9:16 available; no material deformation; total debit at most 30 free credits; median input-to-download at most six minutes; commercial monetized use explicitly confirmed.
**Fail:** any identity failure, fewer than 3 exports, total debit over 30 credits, median over six minutes, or desktop control materially outperforms mobile.
**Stop immediately:** displayed request creates multiple outputs or exceeds the remaining 30-credit test ceiling; purchase/subscription prompt; paid-credit use; password/2FA/identity/payment/signature step; unclear input rights; unclear commercial-use rights; or new terms acceptance.

## 6. Evidence package

App/version and account-tier screenshots; source-rights record; exact settings; pre/post credit balances; all outputs including failures; first/mid/final frame QC; timestamps; SHA-256 hashes; iOS versus desktop workflow notes; terms snapshot; pre/post scorecard.

## 7. Publishable deliverables

If passed: update the existing Flow review with a tested iPhone section, add a mobile workflow comparison, and produce a behind-the-scenes Short. No affiliate CTA without programme acceptance and verified tracking.

## 8. Vendor collaboration boundary

Google may provide documentation, bounded access, media and factual corrections. It receives no editorial control, pre-publication approval, guaranteed rating, recommendation or affiliate placement.

## 9. Decision

`RETEST` — execute on a physical iPhone with free credits only; otherwise publish only the primary-source availability fact after editorial review.
