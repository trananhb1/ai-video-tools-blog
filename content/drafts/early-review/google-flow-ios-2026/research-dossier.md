## Verified Current State

Google announced mobile Flow apps on May 19, when Android was beta and iOS was still “coming soon.”[3]

Apple now lists Google Flow for Australian iPhones, and its API dates release 1.552.0 to September 9.[1][2]

The app listing describes camera-roll/live-camera input, a library synced with desktop, background generations with notifications, and download/share.[2]

The live Flow page confirms the broader desktop studio remains available alongside the app.[6]

Google documents 50 free daily credits for unsubscribed users and prices Veo 3.1 Lite at 10 credits per generation, Fast at 20, and Quality at 100; a request can create multiple generations.[4]

The supported-region page includes Australia but warns that feature availability may differ by country.[5]

AIVP's live Google Flow review describes the web product and free allowance, but does not mention the released iPhone app.[8]

Google's general terms say Google will not claim ownership of original generated content, but they do not provide a Flow-specific, explicit commercial-use grant; monetized-output rights remain a publication gate.[7]
