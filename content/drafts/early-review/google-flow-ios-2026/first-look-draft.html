Google Flow is now available on iPhone in Australia

Apple's Australian App Store lists Google Flow as a free iPhone app requiring iOS 16 or later.[1][2] This completes the iOS launch that Google described as “coming soon” in May.[3]

The app supports camera-roll and live-camera inputs, project and asset sync with desktop, background generations with notifications, and local download/sharing.[2] Google lists Australia as a supported mobile region while warning that features can vary by country.[5]

Free credits are not the same as three guaranteed requests

Google documents 50 free daily credits for unsubscribed users and 10 credits for a Veo 3.1 Lite generation. It also warns that one request may create multiple generations, so the visible debit must be checked before testing.[4]
