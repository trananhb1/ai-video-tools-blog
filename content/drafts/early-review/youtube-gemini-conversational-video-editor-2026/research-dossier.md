## Search strategy and primary-source verification

The event was surfaced on September 24, 2026 via Google News launch radar referencing YouTube's Gemini editor tools for Shorts and Create.[unverified]
Primary source research verified the underlying event as the annual **Made on YouTube 2026** platform announcements published September 23, 2026 (`2026-09-23T14:30:00+00:00`) on the official YouTube Blog by Aparna Pappu, Vice President, YouTube.[1]
The official announcement, titled "New tools to power your creation journey from start to finish", confirms that YouTube is integrating Gemini into a new conversational editing assistant for both **YouTube Shorts** and the **YouTube Create app**.[1]

According to YouTube's primary release:
- The conversational editing assistant acts as a creative partner that handles tedious editing tasks, allowing creators to focus on storytelling.[1]
- Creators can start with suggested prompts or input their own custom prompts to generate a video's first draft.[1]
- Creators continue chatting with the assistant to receive personalized suggestions, reorder frames and clips, trim pauses, and sync music to the beat.[1]
- The tool introduces a bidirectional workflow: creators can seamlessly toggle back and forth between conversational AI guidance and manual timeline editing, making iterative tweaks.[1]
- This update builds on YouTube's earlier integration of Gemini Omni intelligence into Shorts and the YouTube Create app, which YouTube reports was used daily by hundreds of thousands of channels in August 2026 alone.[1]

Storefront verification confirms that the standalone **YouTube Create: Video editor** base mobile application is published by Google LLC on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store (`com.google.android.apps.youtube.producer`).[2][3]
Apple App Store inspection (via the Australian storefront API) confirms version `0.144.420` was released on September 24, 2026 at `02:36:23Z` at zero cost (`Price: 0.0`), confirming base app availability in Australia.[2]
However, storefront listings document the existing base video editing features and "Edit with AI" camera-roll drafting, but do not specifically confirm that the newly announced conversational editing assistant is already activated or universally rolled out to Australian creator accounts.[2][3]
Furthermore, YouTube's general Terms of Service do not explicitly grant commercial rights specifically to conversational-editor outputs or off-platform use; rights remain unverified pending contractual clarification.[4]
