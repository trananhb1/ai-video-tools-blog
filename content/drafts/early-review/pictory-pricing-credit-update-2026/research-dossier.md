## What is verified now

Pictory's live annual view lists Starter at US$25/month billed annually with 2,400 video minutes and 1,200 AI credits per year; Professional at US$35/month billed annually with 7,200 minutes and 12,000 AI credits; and Team at US$119/month billed annually with 21,600 minutes and 28,800 AI credits.[1]

The page describes those credits as usable for generated images, video and avatar video, but its equivalents are maxima and not evidence of accepted-output cost for a product-specific workflow.[1]

The same pricing route says annual pricing saves up to 40%; that is annual-versus-monthly framing, not evidence of an additional 40% affiliate coupon.[1]

AIVP's current review already states the US$35 Professional annual price, but separately claims an extra 20% affiliate discount to US$336/year. The live pricing/referral routes reviewed in this run did not substantiate that extra discount.[1][2]

## Rights and workflow limitation
