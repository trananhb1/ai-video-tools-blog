## 1. Thesis and locked brief

## 2. Three bounded runs

3. Exact repeat of run 2 to measure identity drift and credit consistency.

Record account/tier, model/version, prompt, source hashes, settings, start/end/render time, displayed and consumed credits, output path/SHA-256, retries and acceptance reason.

## 3. Acceptance and stop conditions

**PASS only if all three:** exact product appears by 0:03 and remains distinctive through 0:10; no substitute mask or fabricated specification; 1080×1920 MP4; no watermark; narration/audio commercially cleared under current terms; ≤15 minutes per run; zero incremental cost; displayed consumption does not exceed the documented annual-plan equivalent.[1][3]

**STOP:** payment/subscription prompt, consumption outside an already approved no-cost allowance, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature step, unclear source rights, unclear complete-output monetization rights, or changed terms requiring acceptance.
