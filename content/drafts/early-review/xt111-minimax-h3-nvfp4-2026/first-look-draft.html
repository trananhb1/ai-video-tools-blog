MiniMax H3 NVFP4 for ComfyUI: First Look

The xt111 repository packages 12.5GB NVFP4 variants for reference-image and first/last-frame MiniMax H3 workflows. It is a deployment optimization, not a new foundation model.[1][2]

Why it is interesting

The reference build is documented at 11.9GB DiT VRAM, but native NVFP4 requires Blackwell hardware. The author also retracts an earlier quality assurance and says the current single-pass builds have not been re-benchmarked.[1]

Early verdict

Monitor only, confidence C, verified floor 34/100. Reference inputs and a detailed ComfyUI route are useful; exact-product fidelity, current quality, reliability, cost and licence suitability remain unverified.

This is not a product recommendation. Three artifact-backed exact-product runs are required.
