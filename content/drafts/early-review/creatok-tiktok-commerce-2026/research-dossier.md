## Primary findings

CreatOK positions itself specifically for TikTok ecommerce. Official pages document product-image-to-video generation, first/end-frame controls, multiple video models, vertical output, watermark-free export and commercial-use claims.[1]

The official agent page and GitHub repository document API-key access through the `@creatok/cli` package for analysis, generation, avatar creation and publishing.[3][4] This makes automation materially stronger on paper than a browser-only wrapper, but the package has not been security-audited or run by AIVP.[4]

Pricing begins at US$10/month and the Basic estimate is about four Seedance 2 videos; counts vary by model and settings.[2] The homepage says “Get Started Free,” but the retrieved pricing page does not define complimentary generation credits, so three no-cost video runs are not assumed.[1][2]

## Rights and access limits

The product page says generated videos are commercially licensed and watermark-free.[1] A complete rights decision still requires the operative terms, model-provider pass-through restrictions, input-rights rules and confirmation for included music, avatars and reference-derived outputs. The terms route was indexed but could not be fully extracted in this run.[6]

No account was created: the free allowance is unclear, and authentication/API-key creation could require user account selection. No paid credits or subscription were used.
