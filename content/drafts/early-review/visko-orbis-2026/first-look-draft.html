Visko Orbis 1.0 First Look: Continuous Video Needs an Identity Test

Orbis is a real-time, prompt-steerable video/world model that claims persistent state and long-form generation. For AIVP, the useful test is whether it preserves one exact product when a live instruction changes only one attribute.

What is available

Visko’s models page exposes text and image-reference demos, while the technical report describes prompt switching, image-to-video and long-running 4K/24fps output in its reported serving configuration.[2][5] The dedicated Orbis page still says “Coming soon,” so this is a demo, not a production-ready workflow.[3]

Why it may matter

Continuous steering could reduce regeneration waste if locked subjects and scenes actually survive changes. Vendor benchmarks are not enough to establish that outcome.

Verdict

Monitor only, confidence C, verified floor 17/100. The planned test uses the exact AIVP LED mask for 30 seconds and changes only its light mode at second 10 across three identical runs.

AI Video Picks has not generated an Orbis output, has no affiliate relationship, and will not publish generated media until rights and privacy gates are resolved.
