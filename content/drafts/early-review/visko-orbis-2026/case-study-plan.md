# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Visko Orbis 1.0

**Status:** PLAN — access, privacy, rights, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Orbis preserve the exact identity of AIVP’s rights-cleared red/blue LED face mask for 30 continuous seconds while a mid-stream instruction changes only the lighting mode, at zero cost and without visual drift?

## 1. Case-study thesis

Orbis matters to AIVP only if continuous generation and prompt steering preserve a specific product rather than producing a merely plausible mask. The test targets identity persistence and controlled change, not generic cinematic quality.

## 2. Real AIVP scenario and locked brief

- **Audience:** Australian creators and SMB product marketers.
- **Input:** rights-cleared AIVP reference images of the $49.99 red/blue LED face mask used in the existing portfolio; preserve exact shell shape, eye/mouth cut-outs, strap position, control layout and colourway.
- **Initial prompt:** “Create a clean at-home skincare product demonstration. Keep the referenced LED face mask centered and recognizable. A person wears it in a neutral bathroom. Start in red-light mode.”
- **Locked intervention at 10 seconds:** “Change only the LEDs from red to blue. Keep the same mask, person, room, camera and framing.”
- **Output:** a saved 30-second 9:16 or highest available vertical capture/export, with exact available resolution and codec recorded rather than assumed.
- **Control:** run the same references and scene/intervention through the current Google Flow reference workflow; measure the baseline during testing.
- **Product gate:** exact product must be visually distinctive in the first 10 seconds and remain the same physical product after intervention.

## 3. Three bounded runs

1. **Baseline/default:** public demo defaults, frozen reference and prompts.
2. **Best documented workflow:** vendor-recommended image-reference and steering settings, no paid extras.
3. **Consistency repeat:** repeat run 2 unchanged to measure identity and scene drift.

Record input hashes, model/build, all settings, start/end and intervention timestamps, displayed/consumed credits, output/capture path and SHA-256, errors, retries and acceptance reason.

## 4. Pass/fail criteria

**PASS only if all three runs:** keep exact mask geometry and controls recognizable during seconds 0–10; change LEDs from red to blue within 2 seconds after the intervention while changing no other locked element; preserve product identity through second 30; provide a reusable capture/export of at least 720×1280; consume US$0/no paid credits; and have written monetized-publication rights.

**FAIL:** wrong/generic mask, geometry drift, altered person/room/camera after intervention, product absent in first 10 seconds, response latency >2 seconds, unusable export/capture, non-zero cost, or any failed run.

**STOP immediately:** payment/credit prompt, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature requirement, unclear reference rights, missing privacy policy for uploads, unclear monetized-output rights, or terms beyond ordinary no-cost service use.

## 5. Evidence package

- source ledger and archived terms/privacy pages;
- rights record and SHA-256 for all reference images;
- access/plan/model/settings/cost screenshots;
- all three complete captures/exports including failures and hashes;
- frames at 0, 5, 9, 10, 12, 20 and 30 seconds;
- product-geometry checklist and intervention-delta QC;
- timing, latency, retry and cost log;
- before/after scorecard.

## 6. Publishable deliverables if passed

- evidence-led case-study/review article;
- reference-consistency module in the image-to-video buyer guide;
- Short showing the locked mid-stream intervention;
- transparent methodology and disclosure;
- no affiliate CTA until programme approval and tracking are verified.

## 7. Vendor collaboration boundary

Vendor may provide documentation, bounded access, media and factual corrections. Vendor receives no editorial control, pre-publication approval, guaranteed rating, recommendation or placement. Access does not guarantee positive coverage.

## 8. Current decision

`RETEST` — wait for consistent public access, complete privacy/commercial terms and a compliant vendor contact, then run three zero-cost bounded trials.
