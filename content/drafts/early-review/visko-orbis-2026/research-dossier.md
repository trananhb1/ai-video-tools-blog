## Primary-source findings and contradiction

The technical report claims long-form text-to-video, image-to-video and video continuation, prompt switching during generation, 4K output at 24fps, and hour-scale rollouts in its reported serving configuration.[5] These are vendor-authored benchmark and system claims, not independent AIVP output evidence.

Access language is inconsistent: the homepage and models page offer Try Orbis/demo routes, while the dedicated `/orbis` page says “Coming soon.”[1][2][3] The desk therefore treats current access as a limited public demo, not a generally available production product or API.

No official pricing, credit limits, export specification or API documentation was found on the retrieved primary surfaces.[1][2][3]

The same surfaces did not establish Australian availability, an affiliate programme or independently verified output.[1][2][4]

## Rights, cost and testing gate

Visko’s terms make users responsible for outputs they publish, distribute or commercialize, but the retrieved excerpt does not establish a clear assignment of output ownership or an explicit monetized YouTube/editorial license.[4] The privacy page is still a “Coming soon” placeholder, creating a separate data-handling concern for uploaded product references.[3][4]

No account, demo generation, upload or paid credit was used by AIVP during this desk tick. Testing remains blocked until the demo exposes zero-cost limits without a password/2FA/payment/signature escalation and written commercial-publication rights are obtained.
