# InVideo Agent Two — Case Study Plan

**Decision:** Can Agent Two maintain an exact product and locked brand brief across a 30-second hero video plus two platform variants with fewer manual corrections than the current workflow?

## Real scenario
Use one rights-cleared distinctive product, approved product facts, a locked 30-second script and 9:16 hero format. Ask Agent Two to storyboard, generate/edit, then adapt the approved result for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. Compare against the current reference-led generation plus manual editing workflow.

## Three bounded runs
1. Default Agent Two workflow with locked product references and script.
2. Controlled workflow with explicit composition, character, product and claim constraints.
3. Repeat the better setup unchanged to measure cross-shot consistency.

## Pass
- Exact product is distinctive throughout the first 10 seconds.
- Shape, colour, controls, labels and accessories do not drift.
- No invented claims or prices.
- At least two of three hero outputs are usable.
- Platform variants preserve approved content and framing.
- Setup under 30 minutes, winning render under five minutes, and effective cost no more than US$2 per accepted output.
- Commercial rights for every underlying model used are documented.

## Stop/fail
Stop on payment/top-up, unapproved account access, OAuth, publishing permission or unclear rights. Fail on product drift, context loss, hidden model costs, fewer than two usable outputs or cross-platform factual changes.

## Evidence
Save source rights and hashes, Agent Two/model versions, storyboard, prompts/settings, credit estimates/consumption, timing, every output including failures, contact sheets, export metadata and score delta.

## Deliverables
- “We Tested InVideo Agent Two on a Real Product Campaign”
- refreshed InVideo review
- Agent Two versus Creatify/Dreamina workflow comparison
- behind-the-scenes Short
- existing affiliate CTA only after current Impact tracking is reverified

`RETEST` — obtain bounded Agent Two credits and current commercial/model terms.
