InVideo Agent Two and Agentic Editor First Look

InVideo’s September release combines an AI assistant with a browser-based multitrack editor and supports editable project export.[1]

Current verdict: useful workflow potential, but exact-product consistency, accepted-output cost, reliability, and per-model commercial rights are not verified. Verified floor 40/100, confidence C.

Product affiliate gate

Do not recommend it for affiliate video until three controlled runs preserve the exact product in the first 10 seconds. Stock or generated B-roll cannot substitute for rights-cleared hero-product footage.

Pricing and affiliate context

Credits vary by model and do not roll over.[2] InVideo publishes an affiliate programme with a 120-day tracking window and commissions up to 50%, but AIVP must reverify current tracking before adding a CTA.[4]
