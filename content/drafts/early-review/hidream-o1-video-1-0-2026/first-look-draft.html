HiDream Unveils HiDream-O1-Video-1.0 (HD-V1)

HiDream.ai launched HiDream-O1-Video-1.0 (HD-V1) on September 15, 2026, positioning the foundation model for physics-guided video generation.[1] News syndication surfaced the launch across regional reporting channels.[5]

Claims of physical consistency and native audio

Press reporting states that HD-V1 supports text, image, and video inputs to generate 5 to 20 seconds of 1080p video with co-generated audio.[1] Launch coverage highlights a fourth-place rank on Artificial Analysis's Image to Video Leaderboard (With Audio) and eighth place on Arena.ai's blind test.[1] The release also reports a simultaneous C+ funding round.[1] These statements reflect vendor claims and press summaries rather than independent AIVP test outcomes.[1]

The callable single-image-to-video API

Official HiHarness developer documentation specifies that HiDream-O1-Video-1.0 is exposed via an asynchronous single-image-to-video API.[2] The endpoint accepts one reference image via public URL or Base64 encoding and preserves the aspect ratio of the input image.[2] Users may request dynamic duration or a fixed 10-second clip.[2] The API workflow uses asynchronous task submission and status polling until the output is ready.[2]

Pricing, rights, and workflow limitations

The HiDream developer portal highlights enterprise MaaS solutions but provides no public credit table or self-serve per-second debit breakdown for HD-V1.[3] The consumer showcase platform offers no dedicated affiliate signup route or explicit terms of service for monetized commercial output rights.[4] Generation latency, queue behavior, and Australian network routing remain unverified.[2][3]
