## Primary findings

Pixparkle is primarily an image workflow, but it documents image-to-video from the same chat context using reference assets and Veo 3.1 Lite.[1] This could reduce handoffs for product-video keyframes, but the site does not specify video duration, resolution, aspect ratios, credit cost or identity fidelity.[1]

The free tier advertises ten credits and commercial use without a card.[1] Published paid plans describe image economics, not cost per video, so the free allowance may not support three video runs.[1]

Terms say users own generated content subject to legal limitations, pass responsibility to users, and grant Pixparkle a broad operational/marketing licence.[2] Privacy documentation says prompts, uploads and outputs are stored and sent to third-party providers through Replicate.[3]

## Strength

**Verified floor 35/100; potential ceiling 100; evidence coverage 35%; confidence C; Monitor only.** The floor reflects reference input, conversational workflow and commercial-use language.[1][2]

Video output quality, exact-product fidelity, control during animation, render reliability, video credit cost and automation remain unresolved. The terms prohibit automated tools or bots from accessing the service, and no public API was verified.[2]
