# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Pixparkle

**Status:** PLAN — access, rights, spend and publication gates apply
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only
**Evidence confidence:** C
**Business question:** Can Pixparkle preserve the exact identity of LED face mask ASIN B0D2XTZN8V when turning one locked product keyframe into a 5–10 second vertical video, at zero cost and without leaving its conversational workflow?

## 1. Thesis and locked brief

Use rights-cleared mask references to create one fixed 1080×1920 hero frame, then animate a slow push-in while preserving shell shape, eye/mouth cut-outs, colourway, LED layout, controller and accessories. No generated text, price, medical claim or substitute product. Control: the existing Dreamina reference-image-to-video route using the identical hero frame and motion brief.

## 2. Three bounded runs

1. Baseline image-to-video with default settings.
2. Best documented same-chat reference workflow with frozen model and prompt.
3. Exact repeat of run 2 to measure identity drift.

Record account/tier, model/version, source and output hashes, all prompts/settings, start/end and render duration, displayed/consumed credits, errors/retries and acceptance reason.

## 3. Acceptance and stop conditions

**PASS only if all three:** exact product is distinctive from frame one through second 10; no geometry, LED, controller or accessory substitution; 9:16 export at least 1080×1920; no watermark; commercial-use rights cover the complete clip; ≤5 minutes per run; total US$0; no external rebuild.

**FAIL:** identity drift, generic mask, deformation, unavailable 9:16/1080 export, watermark, >5 minutes, or any cost.

**STOP:** fewer than enough free credits for all three runs, payment/top-up prompt, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature step, unclear displayed credit cost, unclear source or output rights, or any attempt to automate contrary to the terms.

## 4. Evidence package

Source ledger; input rights record and hashes; account/free-credit screenshots; all prompts/settings; all outputs including failures with SHA-256; frame QC at 0:00/0:03/0:05/final; identity comparison sheet; timing/retry/credit ledger; terms snapshot; pre/post scorecard.

## 5. Publishable outputs and decision

Evidence-led review only after passing; image-to-video buyer-guide module; behind-the-scenes Short; transparent access/methodology disclosure; no affiliate CTA without accepted programme and verified tracking. Vendor receives no editorial control or guaranteed coverage. Access does not guarantee positive coverage.

`RETEST` — first verify video credit cost and a compliant product contact, then run only if ten free credits cover all three outputs.
