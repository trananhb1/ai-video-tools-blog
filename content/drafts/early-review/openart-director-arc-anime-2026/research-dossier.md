## Verified Current State

OpenArt launched Director on June 23 as a conversational system for complete videos up to five minutes, with consistent characters, objects, audio and product assets; those launch statements remain vendor claims until tested by AIVP.[3]

Physion's current Anime 1.0 leaderboard independently reports OpenArt first overall at 77.8/100, first in Animetic Language at 68.6 and first in Production Quality at 84.5; Anime Agent 1.0 leads Narrative Coherence, 82.9 to OpenArt's 82.3.[2]

The leaderboard reports OpenArt at US$21.30 per benchmark video. Runway, MiniMax and Flova scored lower but cost less, so OpenArt is not the benchmark's cheapest route.[2]

OpenArt says the anime evaluation used 100 prompts, 800 videos and 19 metrics, and says Director led all eight human-preference metrics.[1] The independent leaderboard confirms the 19-metric structure and score table, but its extracted page does not expose annotation instructions, raw outputs, statistical uncertainty or reproducible run settings.[2]

Current pricing lists Starter at US$14 monthly, Plus at US$34, Pro at US$56 and Wonder at US$240. The page says plan video estimates exclude Director, so those estimates cannot be used to calculate Director cost per accepted output.[4]

OpenArt's terms grant commercial use only at Plus and above and prohibit automated access, bots or scripts except through official interfaces.[5] OpenArt separately publishes an official OAuth MCP/CLI route for image and video generation, reference uploads, model discovery and credit tracking; any future integration must use that official route and retain a manual cost gate.[6]

The affiliate programme is open to applications and its terms state a default 20% commission for 12 months, NET-15 processing, Tolt administration, and a valid PayPal or Wise account requirement.[7][9]
