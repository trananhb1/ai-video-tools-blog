OpenArt Director ranks first on Physion's anime-video-agent benchmark

Physion Labs' Anime 1.0 leaderboard reports OpenArt first overall at 77.8/100, ahead of Anime Agent 1.0 at 75.6. OpenArt also leads Animetic Language and Production Quality, while Anime Agent leads Narrative Coherence.[2]

OpenArt says the evaluation covered 100 prompts, 800 videos and 19 metrics, with Director leading all eight human-preference measures.[1] That is meaningful independent evidence for anime story production—but it is not proof that Director preserves a specific affiliate product.

Quality leadership did not mean lowest cost

Physion reports US$21.30 per OpenArt benchmark video. Runway, MiniMax and Flova scored lower but cost less.[2] OpenArt's plan estimates explicitly exclude Director, so AIVP cannot infer a dependable Director cost from “videos per month” marketing figures.[4]

Commercial use starts above Starter

OpenArt's current terms allow commercial output use on Plus and higher tiers, not Starter. Current monthly prices are US$14 Starter, US$34 Plus, US$56 Pro and US$240 Wonder.[4][5]
