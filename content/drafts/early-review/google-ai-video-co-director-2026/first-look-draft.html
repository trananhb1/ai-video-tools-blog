Google AI Video Co-Director: First Look

Internal noindex draft; not publication-ready.

Google Research groups four systems around multi-shot coherence: Co-Director selects creative direction, CANVAS tracks visual state, A2RD extends generation, and VQQA critiques outputs.[1]

What is available?

A public GoogleCloudPlatform repository includes an Ads Co-Director implementation and CLI.[4] That is stronger than a concept announcement, but it is not a simple hosted creator product. Setup, AU access, quotas, cost and reliability remain unverified.
