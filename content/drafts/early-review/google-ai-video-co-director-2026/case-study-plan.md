# Case Study Plan: Google AI Video Co-Director

## Business question

Can the public Co-Director workflow turn one rights-cleared real affiliate-product reference pack into a coherent 60-second vertical story while preserving exact identity, for no more than US$10 per accepted video and 20 minutes end to end?

## Status

**PLANNED — NOT RUN.** Blocked until complimentary credits or Tom's approval for a bounded paid allowance, explicit generated-output commercial terms, and written source-asset rights exist.[unverified]

## Locked brief

- One real AIVP affiliate product selected only after written asset rights.
- Identical front, side and in-use references; fixed 120-word script; fixed logo and continuity sheet.
- 60-second 1080x1920 H.264 MP4, English narration, eight beats.
- Three verifiable attributes only; no unsupported performance claim.
- Disclosure: "AI-generated illustrative demonstration; not physical product testing."
- Pinned repository commit, model IDs, four MAB iterations and recorded seeds.
- Control: current Google Flow references-to-video workflow using identical inputs.

## Success criteria

1. Exact identity is distinctive in the first 10 seconds and at least six of eight beats.
2. Logo, colourway and defining geometry do not materially drift.
3. Narration matches the script and invents no specification.
4. Vertical export is valid and usable without structural re-editing.
5. Elapsed time <=20 minutes and metered cost <=US$10 per accepted run.
6. Rights and watermark requirements are documented before generation.
7. At least two of three identical runs pass all gates.

## Stop conditions

Do not start without asset rights, output permission, complimentary credits or Tom's prior spend approval. Fail a run if identity misses the first-10-seconds gate or a claim is invented. Stop after two failures or if either of the first two runs exceeds US$10.

## Three bounded runs

Run 1 pending gates; Run 2 pending Run 1 review; Run 3 pending Run 2 review. Inputs, model IDs and parameters stay identical.

## Evidence package

Rights records; source pack; commit SHA; dependency lock; settings; timestamps; all outputs including failures; provider usage; SHA-256 hashes; first/10-second/mid/final QC frames; identity/continuity/narration/export checklist; control delta; commercial-rights and SynthID record.

## Decision

PASS requires two of three runs to pass every gate. CONDITIONAL permits one non-critical time/editing miss if identity and rights pass. FAIL follows identity, rights, claims, cost-stop or two-run failure.
