# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Marvis AI Studio

**Status:** PLAN — rights, access, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Marvis produce three coherent five-second 1344×768 native-audio B-roll clips for an AIVP narrative article, each within 25 minutes and at zero incremental cost, without failed jobs or manual repair?[1][2][3]

## 1. Case-study thesis

Test Marvis only for short narrative B-roll. The current text-only workflow cannot satisfy exact-ASIN product affiliate work and will not be tested or presented as such.[2]

## 2. Real AIVP scenario

Use a rights-cleared AIVP script module about exporting AI-video projects before a service shutdown. Freeze one non-branded scene prompt describing a creator archiving video files at a desk; do not use vendor trademarks or personal likenesses.

Output: five-second 1344×768, 24fps clip with native stereo ambience, downloadable without watermark. Control: the same prompt and duration through the existing no-cost Google Flow route, only if no paid credits are consumed.

## 3. Test matrix

1. Baseline with the frozen prompt, 1344×768 and five seconds.
2. Exact repeat with identical settings.
3. Exact repeat to measure queue variance, coherence and audio consistency.

Record prompt/settings, service timestamp, queue depth, task ID, start/end/render duration, displayed/consumed credit, output path/SHA-256, errors/retries and acceptance reason.

## 4. Acceptance and stop conditions

**PASS only if all three:** complete in ≤25 minutes each; no failed or stuck job; output is five seconds at documented resolution/24fps; motion is coherent; audio has no clipping or unrelated speech; no watermark; no manual repair; zero incremental cost; Australia-specific monetized-output rights are documented before publication.[2][3][5]

**FAIL:** any two failures, median total time above 25 minutes, broken/duplicated anatomy that makes the clip unusable, corrupted audio, undocumented watermark, or required post-repair.

**STOP immediately:** payment/tip/redemption prompt, quota beyond the stated free allowance, credentials/2FA/identity/payment/signature, queue estimate above 60 minutes, unclear input rights, or unresolved Australia-specific H3 monetized-output rights.[1][5]

## 5. Evidence package

Save the source ledger, service/model/version screenshots, exact prompt/settings JSON, queue/task responses, all MP4s including failures, ffprobe metadata, QC frames, timing/failure log, SHA-256 hashes, terms/licence snapshot and scorecard delta.

## 6. Publishable deliverables

If rights and all three runs pass: publish an evidence-led case-study module focused on low-cost narrative B-roll and disclose the single-home-GPU architecture. Do not add an affiliate CTA unless a programme is accepted and tracking is verified. Do not recommend Marvis for product affiliate while references remain unavailable.

## 7. Vendor boundary and decision

Vendor may provide documentation, bounded access and factual corrections, but receives no editorial approval, guaranteed rating, recommendation or placement. Access does not guarantee positive coverage.
