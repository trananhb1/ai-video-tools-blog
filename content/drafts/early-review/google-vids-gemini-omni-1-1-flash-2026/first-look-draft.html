Google Vids adds Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash with 1080p, duration control, and scene extension

Google announced the integration of Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash into Google Vids on September 23, 2026.[1] The update introduces native 1080p Full HD video generation and upscaling, exact duration controls, and scene extension with character and audio consistency directly inside the timeline video editor.[1][2]

What is documented

Official Google documentation confirms that users across Google Workspace editions and personal Google accounts can now generate AI video clips at 24 fps in 720p or 1080p resolution, in both 16:9 widescreen and 9:16 vertical aspect ratios.[1][2] The Ingredients feature accepts text prompts along with up to 7 uploaded reference images and avatar references with synchronized voice.[2] The new scene extension feature lengthens scenes while maintaining consistent visual context, lighting, character appearance, and environmental elements.[1] Personal Google accounts receive 50 AI video credits per month at no cost, resetting on the first of each month.[3]

What remains unverified

Google’s documentation does not establish actual generation speeds, server queue delays, failure rates, or prompt adherence under stress.[unverified] Crucially, independent laboratory testing is required to verify whether commercial product details survive 1080p generation without hallucination, and whether scene extension maintains continuity across the transition seam without visual jumps or lighting flicker.[unverified] Commercial use terms for personal accounts versus Workspace business accounts require contractual verification.[unverified]
