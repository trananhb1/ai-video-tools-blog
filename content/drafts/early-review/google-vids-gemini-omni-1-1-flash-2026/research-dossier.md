## Search strategy and primary-source verification

The update was announced on September 23, 2026 on the official Google Workspace Updates blog as "Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash now in Vids with improved extension quality, 1080p, and duration control".[1]
The official announcement confirms users now have access to Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash directly within the Google Vids web application.[1]
According to Google's primary release, Omni 1.1 delivers higher quality video extension with significant improvements in character and audio consistency.[1]
The update introduces full 1080p HD AI video generation, as well as timeline upscaling of existing AI clips to 1080p.[1]
Users can set exact clip durations to match storytelling and voiceovers precisely, and extend scenes with smooth visual and environmental transitions.[1]
Rollout across Rapid Release and Scheduled Release domains commenced on September 23, 2026 with a 1–3 day full visibility timeline.[1]
Availability includes Google Workspace Business (Starter, Standard, Plus), Enterprise, Education Plus, Nonprofits, Individual, and Consumer accounts (personal Google accounts), as well as Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.[1][3]

Supporting documentation from Google Docs Editors Help provides explicit operational specifications for AI video clip generation.[2]
Generated video clips operate at 24 frames per second in 720p or 1080p resolution, supporting 16:9 landscape and 9:16 portrait aspect ratios.[2]
The interface accepts text prompts combined with "Ingredients" supporting up to 7 uploaded reference images and avatar references with synchronized voice.[2]
Google Docs Editors Help confirms that personal Google accounts receive 50 AI video credits per month, resetting at 12:00 AM PT on the first day of each calendar month.[3]
Gemini in Google Vids is available globally across all regions where Google Workspace is available, including Australia.[3]

## Pricing, rights, and operational limits

Personal Google accounts receive 50 AI video credits per month, with monthly limits resetting at 12:00 AM PT on the first day of each month.[3]
Google Workspace Business, Enterprise, and Education tiers include access, while users with AI Expanded Access add-ons receive higher limits.[1][3]
Video specifications are strictly documented: 24 fps frame rate, 720p or 1080p resolution, 16:9 landscape or 9:16 portrait aspect ratio, with up to 7 reference images under Ingredients.[2]
Personal Google accounts have access to preset AI avatars and may experience restrictions on advanced AI video editing.[3]
Commercial terms require distinction between business Workspace accounts (standard business ownership) and personal Google accounts.[unverified]
