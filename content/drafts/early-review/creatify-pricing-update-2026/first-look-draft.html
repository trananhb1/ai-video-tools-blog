Creatify Pricing Update: Starter $39, Pro $99

Creatify's current pricing page lists Starter at US$39 per month for 100 credits and Pro at US$99 per month for 300 credits. It advertises annual savings up to 50%.[1]

The free plan remains 10 monthly credits with watermarked exports, enough for roughly two short videos under Creatify's published help guidance.[1][2][3]

Why this matters

These figures materially exceed the US$33 Starter and US$49 Pro values still present in AIVP's Creatify review and UGC comparisons. Those pages need a factual correction before readers make a buying decision.

Credit math is not one universal rate

Creatify's app FAQ says AI Video Ads, Avatar Videos and AI Shorts use five credits per 15 seconds. Its API billing documentation lists URL-to-Video and AI Avatar at five credits per 30 seconds.[3][5]

Until a controlled run identifies the exact workflow and observed charge, we should not publish one universal “cost per video” figure.

Product and commercial fit

The FAQ says Creatify accepts Amazon product links plus uploaded product images and videos, and that users own their creatives and may use them on any channel.[3]

That is promising for affiliate production, but it does not prove the exact product stays recognizable in the first 10 seconds. Three locked LED-mask runs remain the hard gate.

Affiliate status

Creatify's official programme page advertises 25% recurring commission.[4] AIVP already has verified active tracking, but the historical `$49 off first month` audience offer must be reverified before it appears beside the new plan prices.

Early verdict

This pricing event has a 43/100 verified floor and confidence C: Weak fit pending a current-plan test. The immediate editorial action is factual correction, not a new quality recommendation.
