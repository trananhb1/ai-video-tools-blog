## 4. Acceptance and stop conditions

**PASS only if all three:** mask appears by 0:03 and remains distinctive through 0:10; no substitute product or invented specification; 1080×1920 MP4; no watermark; no external rebuild; ≤10 credits and ≤15 minutes per run; complete output is monetizable under current terms.[1][3]

**FAIL:** identity drift, generic stock substitute, unsupported medical/specification claim, >10 credits, >15 minutes, manual rebuild, export failure or inconsistent credit charging.

**STOP immediately:** a paid-plan requirement, consumption beyond a confirmed complimentary/free allowance, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature, unclear source rights, changed terms, or fewer than 30 no-cost credits available. The published free allocation is only 10 credits, so the full three-run plan is currently COST_BLOCKED absent bounded review credits.[2][3]
