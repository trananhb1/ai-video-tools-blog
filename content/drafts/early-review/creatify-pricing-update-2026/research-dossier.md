## What changed for AIVP

Creatify now lists Starter at US$39/month for 100 credits and Pro at US$99/month for 300 credits, with annual savings advertised up to 50%. The free plan remains 10 monthly credits and watermarked exports.[1][2]

AIVP's staged repository still states Starter at US$33/month and Pro at US$49/month in the Creatify review, UGC buyer guide and Arcads/Creatify/MakeUGC comparison. Those values should be treated as stale pending a publication-gate edit.

The official FAQ says AI Video Ads, Avatar Videos and AI Shorts use five credits per 15 seconds, whereas the API billing page says URL-to-Video and AI Avatar use five credits per 30 seconds. The documents address different product surfaces but do not make that distinction clear enough to calculate one universal per-video cost.[3][5]

The FAQ says users own their creatives, may use them on any channel without limitations and receive royalty-free stock footage. It also says Amazon product links and uploaded product images/video are supported.[3]
