# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Creatify Boreal Public Launch

**Status:** PLAN — access, rights, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Weak fit pending testing  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Boreal turn the exact rights-cleared LED face mask ASIN B0D2XTZN8V into three usable five-second vertical clips while preserving product identity, rendering in real time, and costing no more than US$0.10 per usable output?

## 1. Locked brief

Use one rights-cleared hero image of ASIN B0D2XTZN8V, hashed before upload. Output: five seconds, native 9:16, highest free-tier resolution, no audio requirement. Prompt: “Slow product reveal of the exact supplied white LED face mask on a neutral vanity. Preserve shell shape, eye and mouth openings, controller, cable, colourway and red/blue LED layout. Gentle camera push-in. No people, text, added logo, substitute object, medical claim or accessory.”

Control: Google Flow Veo 3.1 Fast reference-to-video using the same image and visual brief, without spending beyond an approved allowance.

## 2. Three bounded runs

1. Default Boreal image-to-video settings.
2. Best documented identity-preserving settings.
3. Exact repeat of run 2 to measure drift.

Freeze model/version, image, prompt, duration, ratio, resolution and seed where supported. Record setup, queue/render/total time, displayed and actual debit, retries, response metadata, output and SHA-256.

## 3. Pass, fail and stop

**PASS:** 3/3 exports; exact mask recognizable from frame one through frame five seconds; no shape, opening, controller, cable, colourway, label or LED-pattern substitution; native 9:16; no severe warping; median render at most five seconds; no more than US$0.10 per usable output; written monetized-use permission.

**FAIL:** any identity failure, fewer than three usable exports, median over five seconds, cost above threshold, unavailable vertical output, or re-editing required to hide defects.

**STOP immediately:** payment/top-up/subscription prompt; paid-credit debit beyond an approved allowance; password/2FA/identity/payment/signature step; unclear input rights; unclear monetized-output rights; or a predicted total above US$0.30.

## 4. Evidence package

Save rights record and source hash; model/settings screenshots; prompt and request/response with secrets removed; before/after balance; all outputs including failures and hashes; first/mid/final frames; product comparison sheet; timing/retry/cost ledger; terms snapshot; and pre/post scorecard.

## 5. Publishable outputs

A Review or case-study article only after the test passes; a low-cost model comparison; a workflow Short; methodology and AI-illustration disclosure. Use the existing Creatify affiliate CTA only after Boreal-route attribution is verified.

## 6. Decision

RETEST — first obtain bounded zero-cost access, an exact Boreal debit table, and written rights confirmation.
