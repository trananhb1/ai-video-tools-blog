## What is verified—and what is not

Creatify says Boreal is built on LTX-2.5, generates text-to-video and image-to-video, costs about US$0.01 per generated second, and rendered a five-second clip in about five seconds in its measurements.[1][2]

The launch release reports a vendor-run blind comparison where Boreal beat the untouched base in 25 decisive cases versus 6, with 9 ties across 40 cases. This is useful publisher evidence but not an independent benchmark or an AIVP-controlled run.[1]

Creatify also claims the model preserves product labels, creator identity, and app-interface details better than its base. Exact ASIN preservation, 9:16 behavior, retries, billed cost, and three-run consistency remain unverified.[1]

The Model Playground advertises a free plan with 10 monthly credits and watermarked exports, but the public page does not state Boreal’s exact free-tier credit debit.[3]

Creatify’s API billing page describes subscription-gated API access at US$99/month and does not expose a Boreal line item, so the relationship between one-cent-per-second launch pricing, fal pricing, Playground credits, and direct API credits remains unresolved.[6]

Creatify’s terms include commercial-use language for compliant Community Assets, but the retrieved language does not clearly establish ownership and monetized-use rights for a private Boreal output routed through every named provider.[4]
