Creatify Boreal launches with one-cent video claims

Creatify publicly launched Boreal on September 15 as an LTX-2.5-derived text- and image-to-video model for advertising.[1] The company advertises roughly US$0.01 per output second and real-time rendering, but those are vendor measurements rather than AIVP-tested results.[1][2]

The useful claim is product fidelity

Creatify says its post-training improved product-label, creator-identity and interface retention over the base model.[1] That is directly relevant to product ads, but it does not establish that Boreal preserves a specific ASIN across repeated vertical runs.

The price needs route-by-route verification

Boreal is said to be live through Creatify Labs API, Model Playground, AdFlow and fal.[1] Model Playground advertises 10 monthly free credits with watermarked export, while the separate API billing page requires a paid API subscription and does not list Boreal’s debit.[3][6]
