## Search strategy and event deduplication

The new Technology Org headline was resolved against Synthesia primary sources rather than treated as a same-day launch.[5] Synthesia officially announced Dubbing 2.0 on July 15, 2026, describing better lip sync, natural voices, translation, and editing; therefore the September 23 item is secondary resurfacing of an older underlying release, not a new September launch.[1]

Current Synthesia documentation confirms that Dubbing accepts external MP4, WebM, or MOV files and public YouTube links, preserves the speaker voice, offers optional lip sync, supports bulk uploads, and exposes a Dubbing API for larger catalogs.[2] The public translator page offers the first minute free and requires the uploader to hold all necessary source rights.[3]

The current updates index shows later dubbing improvements on August 21 and September 3, but no September 23 launch entry.[4]
