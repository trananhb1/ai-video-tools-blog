Jianying Hub First Look: CapCut's New End-to-End AI Workspace

Draft status: Staged, not published. Australian availability, exact price, credit use and commercial rights are unresolved.

What launched

A September 20 launch introduced Jianying Hub, an infinite-canvas and multitrack workspace that can begin with a sentence, reference video or document, build a script, call image/video/audio generation services and continue into detailed editing.

Why it matters

The differentiated claim is workflow consolidation rather than a new base video model: planning, generation, agent assistance, reusable Skills and editing are presented in one environment. Xiaoying also documents a product-image-to-marketing-material path, which could be relevant to creator and SMB product videos.
