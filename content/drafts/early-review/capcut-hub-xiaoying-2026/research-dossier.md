## Search strategy and correction

The deterministic monitor retained the same Google News URL but normalized the publisher suffix from `eu.36kr.com` to `36Kr`.[5] The prior September 21 assessment treated the item as commentary about established CapCut AI features. A correction search found a dated Xinhua account of Jianying's September 20 AI creation launch, which describes a new Hub workspace, creator agent, mobile assistant and AI Ultra subscription.[1]

This is a correction to the event classification, not a second event. The existing state record is updated in place and the earlier below-threshold decision is superseded.[1][5]

## Primary and supporting evidence

Xinhua reports that Jianying launched **Jianying Hub**, built from an infinite canvas plus multitrack editor. The documented input path starts from a sentence, reference video or document, develops a script, calls ByteDance image/video/audio creation services, and passes the assets into a multitrack editor.[1]

The same launch account says Jianying Assistant supports conversational planning/editing, repetitive editing tasks and custom Skills; the mobile assistant Xiaoying can accept a product image and plan marketing assets.[1]

Jianying's official site currently positions the product as an all-in-one AI creation partner and lists text-to-video assembly, marketing videos, AI music, avatars, AI voice and desktop editing. It publishes a business-cooperation email at `jianying.business@bytedance.com`.[2]

CapCut's current terms warn that versions and service availability differ by jurisdiction and device. They do not establish that Jianying Hub is available to Australian users.[3]
