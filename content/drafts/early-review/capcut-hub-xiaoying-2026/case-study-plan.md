# Case Study Plan: Jianying Hub Exact-Product Vertical Video

## Business question

Can Jianying Hub turn a rights-cleared reference image and owned product footage for the Revlon One-Step hair dryer brush into a recognizable 30-second 9:16 affiliate-video draft in no more than eight minutes and at zero incremental cost, while keeping the exact product distinctive in the first 10 seconds?

## Production prerequisite

Testing is blocked until the reference image and footage are either Tom-owned captures or covered by explicit supplier permission. Vendor web images are not authorized by default. Australian access, no-cost credits and Hub-specific monetized-output rights must also be confirmed before any run.

## Locked brief

- **Product:** Revlon One-Step hair dryer brush
- **Inputs:** one rights-cleared front/three-quarter product image, one rights-cleared 8-second owned product clip, the fixed 78-word script in `locked-script.txt`, and AIVP brand colors
- **Output:** H.264 MP4, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 30 seconds, English narration, burned-in captions
- **Prompt:** "Create a concise vertical product explainer. Show the supplied exact black-and-red brush in the first shot and preserve its shape, barrel, controls and color. Use only supplied product media for product claims. Add deterministic price and disclosure text in post, not generated imagery."
- **Model/settings:** freeze the exact Hub model, aspect ratio, seed if available, voice, template, Skill set and export settings before Run 1; reuse without changes for Runs 2 and 3
- **Control:** current AIVP Google Flow reference-video workflow using the same cleared assets and script
- **Cost ceiling:** US$0 incremental cost; use only a verified free allowance or vendor-granted review credits

## Success criteria

Each of three identical runs must:

1. Show the exact black-and-red brush clearly within the first 10 seconds.
2. Preserve recognizable barrel shape, controls and color without adding a competing product.
3. Export 1080×1920 H.264 with intelligible narration and readable captions.
4. Finish from locked input import to export in no more than eight minutes.
5. Require no external re-edit beyond deterministic price, disclosure and CTA overlays.
6. Consume no paid credits and produce a verifiable zero-cost receipt/credit delta.
7. Be permitted for monetized YouTube and affiliate editorial use under written terms.

**Pass rule:** all hard gates pass in all three runs. Report median time and accepted-output cost; do not average away a failed identity run.

## Failure and stop conditions

- Identity is not distinctive in the first 10 seconds: mark the run failed; stop after a second identity failure.
- A billing prompt or paid-credit requirement appears: stop immediately and ask Tom before spend.
- Rights or geographic access cannot be verified: do not start Run 1.
- Commercial use is prohibited or ambiguous after vendor response: stop publication and recommendation.
- Two timeouts or corrupt exports: stop the pilot.

## Evidence package

- Rights record for every input
- Tool build/version, region and account tier
- Full prompt, script, model, seed and settings export
- Input screenshot and dated output preview
- All three MP4s, including failures
- SHA-256 hashes for inputs, settings and outputs
- Setup, queue, render and total elapsed seconds
- Credits before/after and cost per accepted output
- Frame QC at first frame, 10 seconds, midpoint and final frame
- Identity checklist: barrel, controls, black/red color, no substitute product
- Commercial-rights and watermark confirmation
- Control output and score delta

## Publishable outputs if passed

- Evidence-led case study with method, all failures and limitations
- Updated strength scorecard
- AIVP buyer-guide module only if exact-product and rights gates pass
- Behind-the-scenes Short labelled as an AI-generated illustrative demonstration
- Affiliate CTA only after programme acceptance and tracking verification
