FastH3 V2 GGUF First Look: Smaller Local Weights, but Text-Only

Rebel AI has published a 19.84GB Q4_K_M GGUF conversion of FastVideo’s 35B FastH3 8-Step V2 transformer for ComfyUI-GGUF.[1][2]

What changed

The converter says it reconstructed the full official BF16 transformer—not the pruned ComfyUI checkpoint—and retained higher-precision projection and conditioning tensors where appropriate.[1] The upstream checkpoint produces synchronized video and audio in eight transformer forwards.[3]

The catch

The GGUF is only the transformer: creators still need the Qwen3-VL encoder and both MiniMax H3 VAEs.[1] FastVideo also says V2 did not distil FL2VA or Ref2VA, so this release cannot ingest an exact product reference and currently fails AIVP’s affiliate-product hard gate.[3]

Rights

The inherited MiniMax licence covers Australia and says MiniMax claims no rights over outputs, but territorial, attribution, disclosure, acceptable-use and revenue-threshold conditions apply.[5]

Verdict

Monitor only: 21/100 verified, confidence C. The lower-storage package is useful for local experimentation, not an evidence-backed product-video recommendation. Wait for reference-conditioned support and a controlled no-cost run.
