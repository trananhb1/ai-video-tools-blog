# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Pika Product Ad

**Status:** PLAN — supplier rights, access, terms, and zero-spend gates apply
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only (30/100 verified floor)
**Evidence confidence:** C
**Business question:** Can Pika Product Ad turn one rights-cleared exact-product image and locked brief into a usable 15-second vertical affiliate ad in three repeatable runs while preserving product identity in the first 10 seconds, with no unapproved spend?

## 1. Case-study thesis

Pika's strongest AIVP-relevant claim is product-image-plus-brief to finished ad.[1][2] The falsifiable claim is that this workflow can preserve the exact product well enough for product-affiliate content, not merely make a generic category ad.

## 2. Real AIVP scenario and rights gate

- **Product:** Revlon One-Step Volumiser Plus 2.0 used in AIVP's existing affiliate-video workflow.
- **Source input:** one AIVP-captured photograph or supplier-authorized hero image; written rights record required before upload.
- **Brief:** "Create a 15-second product ad for Australian creators. Show the exact black/red oval brush, its handle controls and bristle/barrel shape. Hook in the first 2 seconds, one benefit-focused demonstration, and a neutral final CTA. Do not invent performance test results, price, reviews, logos, packaging or accessories."
- **Target output:** 9:16, 1080×1920 if the app supports it; otherwise record a hard workflow failure because the live page currently presents 16:9.[2]
- **Control:** the same image and script through the established Google Flow reference-to-video workflow, using existing approved allowance only; do not consume credits solely to create a new control if no approved allowance remains.

## 3. Three bounded runs

1. **Baseline:** Product Ad defaults, locked brief and source image.
2. **Best documented workflow:** same inputs, using any vendor-published template/recommended settings supplied in writing.
3. **Consistency repeat:** exact Run 2 settings and inputs with a new generation.

For every run record account/plan, app/model/version, full prompt and settings, displayed cost, balance before/after, queue/render/total seconds, retries, output path, SHA-256, and acceptance reason. Preserve failures.

## 4. Pass criteria

- Exact black/red product, oval barrel/bristles and handle controls are visually distinctive by 2 seconds and remain recognizable through the first 10 seconds in all three runs.
- No material geometry, colourway, logo, control, accessory, packaging, price, review or performance fabrication.
- 9:16 output at 1080×1920, 15 seconds, H.264 MP4, no watermark.
- At least 2/3 outputs usable without visual re-editing beyond deterministic captions/CTA.
- Median elapsed time ≤10 minutes; no more than one retry across three runs.
- Commercial-use/no-watermark rights remain explicit for the tested plan.[7][8]
- AIVP-paid cost is US$0 unless Tom separately approves spend; complimentary evaluation credits may be used only within their written bounds.

## 5. Fail and stop conditions

**Fail:** product not distinctive in the first 10 seconds; only 16:9 export; fewer than 2/3 accepted outputs; invented product details; watermark; ambiguous monetized-use rights; or median time above 10 minutes.

**Stop immediately:** no written source-asset rights; payment/subscription prompt; unapproved paid-credit consumption; password, 2FA, identity, payment, signature or binding terms requiring Tom; displayed run cost exceeds the written complimentary allowance; or product identity fails in two runs.

## 6. Evidence package

Rights record; dated input/settings/cost screenshots; exact prompt/settings export; all three MP4s including failures; SHA-256 hashes; credit and timing ledger; 0s/2s/5s/10s/final frame contact sheet; product-identity QC; terms/pricing snapshot; control comparison; pre/post scorecards.

## 7. Publishable outputs if the case study passes

Evidence-led case study or review update, buyer-guide module, and a behind-the-scenes Short. Label generated product footage as an AI-generated illustrative demonstration, not physical performance proof. Add no affiliate CTA until Pika.art acceptance and live tracking are verified.

## 8. Vendor boundary

Vendor access, credits, documentation or affiliate eligibility does not guarantee coverage, a positive verdict or ranking. Pika receives no editorial control.

## 9. Decision

`RETEST` — first obtain rights-cleared input, a compliant vendor contact, and bounded no-cost access/credits.
