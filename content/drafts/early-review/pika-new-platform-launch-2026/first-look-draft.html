Pika Product Ad and Video Studio: First Look

Pika has surfaced a product-focused set of creative apps. Product Ad accepts a product image and brief and presents a 15-second, 16:9 workflow; Video Studio accepts referenced characters, products, settings and styles for multi-shot video creation.[1][2][3]

What changed

Camera Director offers alternate angles from an uploaded clip, while Relight Media is presented as changing the colour, intensity and direction of video lighting.[4][5][6] These are workflow claims from Pika, not AIVP test results.

Why product creators should care

The Product Ad workflow is unusually close to the real affiliate-video job: begin with the actual product rather than generic stock footage. That does not prove identity preservation. A recommendation requires three controlled runs and the product must remain distinctive in the first 10 seconds.

Pricing, rights and limitations

Pika's official pricing page lists a free plan and paid plans, and states commercial use plus watermark-free downloads on the listed plans.[7] It does not show the credit cost of these new apps. The Product Ad page currently shows 16:9, which may not satisfy a Shorts-first workflow.[2] Australian access and app-specific API support are also unverified.

Early assessment

Verified floor: 30/100; potential ceiling: 100; evidence coverage: 30%; confidence C; classification: Monitor only. No affiliate CTA is included because Pika.art programme acceptance and tracking have not been verified.

Planned test

A rights-cleared exact-product image will be used for three identical Product Ad runs. The test will measure first-10-second product identity, vertical export, accepted-output rate, time, credits and commercial usability. No paid generation will occur without Tom's approval.
