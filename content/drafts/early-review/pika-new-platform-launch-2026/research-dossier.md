## What is documented

### Product Ad

Pika says the app takes a product image and brief and returns a market-ready video; the live page currently shows a 15-second, 16:9 configuration.[1][2] This is directly relevant to product-affiliate creative, but neither the source pages nor vendor demos prove exact product identity survives generation.

### Video Studio

The live page says users can attach up to 50 characters, products, settings, and styles, then reference them in a prompt. It describes outputs from short clips to three-minute multi-shot videos with sound.[3] The visible configuration showed 720p, 16:9, audio on, and reference-upload options, but those defaults do not prove all plan/model combinations.

### Camera Director and Relight Media

Camera Director accepts an existing clip and offers alternate angles while claiming to maintain the original scene; Pika's official post additionally claims preservation of motion and performance.[4][5] Pika's Relight Media post says users can alter light colour, intensity, and direction.[6] Neither workflow has been independently tested by AIVP.

### Price, rights, and integration

The official pricing page lists a free plan and paid annual-billing equivalents of US$8, US$28, and US$76 per month, with commercial use and watermark-free downloads stated for every listed plan.[7] It does not publish per-run credit costs for these new apps, so accepted-output cost remains unresolved. Pika's developer site verifies a separate API Club and 100+ model operations, but it does not establish API endpoints for the new creative apps.[9]
