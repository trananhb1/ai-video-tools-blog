# Case Study Plan: Ankon AI Whiteboard Video

**Status:** PLAN — credit gate active
**Classification:** Monitor only (35/100, confidence C)

## Business question

Can Ankon turn one rights-cleared AIVP educational article into three consistent 60-second whiteboard explainers that are accurate, mobile-readable and monetizable without manual scene rebuilding?

## Locked brief

Use a fixed 150-word excerpt from AIVP's “What Is Text-to-Video AI?” guide and no third-party images. Generate a 60-second 9:16 English explainer with burned captions, SRT and metadata. Freeze script, voice, orientation and render mode.

## Three runs

1. Default. 2. Best documented workflow. 3. Exact repeat. Save settings, timing, credits, retries, MP4/SRT/metadata, SHA-256 and visual QC for every run.

## Pass

Three accurate videos; readable synchronized captions; complete artifacts within 10 minutes each; no scene rebuilding; at most US$2 per accepted output; monetized rights confirmed.

## Fail / stop

Fail on fabrication, unreadable/broken output, insufficient free allowance or unclear upstream rights. Stop at any paid spend, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature, or unclear input rights.

## Evidence and decision

Save source rights, screenshots, all outputs/failures, hashes, QC, timing/credit log, terms and scorecard delta. **Current decision: RETEST** after one additional no-cost review credit is secured.
