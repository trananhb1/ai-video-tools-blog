# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Yoroll H3 Superfast / YoLive

**Status:** PLAN — rights and access unresolved  
**Confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Yoroll produce a reliable live branching buyer-education experience from one locked AIVP brief without product drift or unsafe factual improvisation?

## Falsifiable thesis
Yoroll can run a five-minute, three-choice interactive “Which creator video workflow fits?” experience in three repeat sessions, keep one rights-cleared featured product distinctive in the first 10 seconds, preserve locked pricing/fact statements, respond within the predeclared latency ceiling, and provide monetisable export or replay rights.

## Scenario and control
Use AIVP-owned product footage and a locked three-branch script. The control is a conventional pre-rendered branching page using the same clips and decisions. No audience PII, voice clone, public room, production account, or publishing connection is allowed.

## Three bounded runs
1. Default documented creation path with the locked brief.
2. Best vendor-documented consistency/reference workflow.
3. Unchanged repeat with scripted audience votes to measure latency and drift.

Save inputs and rights, script hash, model/version/settings, screenshots, all outputs/failures, start/response/render timing, credits/cost, replay/export hashes, frame QC and score delta.

## Pass/fail and stops
Pass if all three branches complete in all runs, median response stays under the locked vendor-supported threshold, no locked fact changes, exact product is distinctive in the first 10 seconds, and two of three runs are editorially usable. Fail on broken state, hallucinated facts, product drift, moderation bypass, missing replay/export, or fewer than two usable runs.

Stop immediately on payment, account creation, credentials/2FA, social OAuth, public publishing, terms acceptance, unclear input/output rights, or unbounded credit use.

## Deliverables
Plan an interactive-video case study, Yoroll First Look, interactive-video buyer-guide module, and behind-the-scenes Short. Include methodology/disclosure; affiliate CTA only after programme acceptance and tracking verification.

`RETEST` — first obtain written commercial rights, pricing, bounded access and a supported latency target.
