## Decision

YoLive is a live interactive-video surface powered by Yoroll; the reachable page exposes an active “live Director” and audience voting state.[7] LinearGame's official product site describes Yoroll as a prompt-, photo-, and clip-driven platform for branching cinematic experiences and describes Roll-01 as producing interactive video at 24 fps.[20][21] This is differentiated from linear text-to-video, but H3 Superfast speed, reliability, pricing, rights, exports and Australian availability remain unverified.

**Strength:** verified floor 23/100; potential ceiling 100/100; evidence coverage 23%; confidence C; Monitor only.

## Product and audience fit

The strongest plausible AIVP use case is an interactive choose-the-next-tool demo or branching buyer-education story, not a direct product-affiliate Short. Reference clips could support a specific product, but no controlled evidence establishes identity preservation in the first 10 seconds. Product-affiliate recommendation is blocked.

## Rights and commercial gate

Yoroll's privacy policy says uploaded text, images, video and audio are processed for AI generation and are not used to train foundational/general models without explicit notice and consent; users remain responsible for third-party rights.[8] That is a data-processing statement, not an explicit grant of monetised output rights. Publication beyond a clearly labelled First Look is blocked until output and media-reuse rights are documented.

## Outreach status

A separate release-distribution page lists `support@yoroll.ai`, but it is not an official-domain source. No address was guessed and no contact form was submitted. Status: HELD — contact proof fails validator.
