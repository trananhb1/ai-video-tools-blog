# Case Study Plan: HeyGen current pricing, credits and affiliate programme

## Business Question
Can HeyGen’s current free allowance produce three recognizable 30-second product-avatar videos for the LED mask without consuming paid credits?

## Real AIVP Use Case
Amazon B0D2XTZN8V LED face mask UGC-style affiliate Short

## Locked Brief
Create one finished video using rights-cleared AIVP inputs. Output settings: 9:16, 1080×1920 target, 30 seconds, identical script/product image/avatar/voice. Freeze the source image/script, model, voice, avatar, seed where supported, and all export settings across three runs.

## Control
Use AIVP's last accepted workflow for the same job. Record total elapsed time, render time, edit time, credit use, accepted-output cost and first-10-second product/disclosure QC.

## Success Criteria
- Exact LED mask recognizable in first 10 seconds
- Three of three free renders complete
- No paid credits consumed
- Lip-sync and narration usable without re-editing
- Commercial use confirmed

## Failure / Stop Conditions
- Product identity drifts
- Any payment or paid-credit prompt
- Free allowance cannot support three runs
- Rights ambiguity

## Three Identical Runs
- Run 1: NOT STARTED
- Run 2: NOT STARTED
- Run 3: NOT STARTED

## Evidence Package
- Rights-cleared source asset and frozen script
- Tool/model/version and full settings export
- Input/output screenshots and all three MP4s, including failures
- Timing and credit ledger per run
- SHA-256 hashes of source, settings and outputs
- First, midpoint and final-frame QC
- Commercial-use and Content Credentials checks
- Score delta against control

## Cost Gate
US$0 authorized for this plan. Stop before any paid-credit consumption, subscription or payment prompt. Complimentary vendor credits may be used only after their terms and balance are recorded.

## Outcome
NOT RUN. Hand off to vendor-tool-automation only after no-cost access, rights and account prerequisites are confirmed.
