Alaya-EVOKE-Turbo: First Look at the Control-Focused World Model

Alaya Lab released EVOKE-Turbo on September 11 as a controllability-optimized checkpoint for camera-motion following and scene/subject instruction adherence. It uses the same 14B, three-step, CFG-free 384 × 640 at 24fps architecture as EVOKE.[1][2]

What changed

The vendor reports higher WBench Navigation, Consistency and Physical scores than base EVOKE, but lower Video Quality and Setting scores. The card warns that Turbo's selected seed and prompts differ from the base comparison, so these numbers are not an independent or directly controlled benchmark.[1]

Why AIVP has not tested it

The FP32 Turbo weights are about 57.25GB. Required selected base components and ViGeo bring the minimum download near 79.7GiB before code and caches, exceeding this host's free storage; this host also has no detected NVIDIA GPU.[2][8][9]

Commercial-use blocker

Although Turbo is tagged Apache-2.0, its required ViGeo checkpoint is CC BY-NC 4.0. AIVP therefore does not treat this workflow as cleared for monetized affiliate or AdSense output.[1][5]

Early verdict

Monitor only, confidence C, verified floor 19/100. The control-focused release is technically interesting, but no AIVP output exists, product identity is unverified, creator economics are unknown and commercial rights fail the current publication gate.

This is not a recommendation. Hands-on testing is planned only after rights and no-cost compatible compute are documented.
