# Fetra AI — Case Study Plan

**Decision:** Can Fetra turn one rights-cleared product page and asset set into a usable three-channel campaign without inventing product details or regulated claims?

## Real scenario
Use a rights-cleared distinctive consumer product with verified listing facts and source images. Lock one brief and create a 30-second 9:16 product video, an Instagram post and a buyer-intent article brief. Compare the video against the current Dreamina/Creatify reference-led workflow.

## Three bounded runs
1. Product URL ingestion with default AI Director workflow.
2. Same brief with uploaded rights-cleared references and explicit claim constraints.
3. Repeat the better workflow unchanged to measure brand/product consistency.

## Pass
- Exact product remains distinctive in the first 10 seconds.
- No invented price, medical/performance claim, feature or endorsement.
- At least two video runs are visually usable.
- All three channel outputs remain factually aligned.
- Rights for monetized outputs are explicit.
- Setup under 30 minutes and effective cost no more than US$2 per accepted video.

## Stop/fail
Stop on social OAuth, publishing permission, payment, contract acceptance or unclear rights. Do not connect production accounts. Fail on product drift, hallucinated claims, pricing mismatch, fewer than two usable videos or hidden publishing actions.

## Evidence
Save product-fact ledger, input-rights record, prompts/settings/model, screenshots, all outputs and failures, frame QC, timing, credits/cost, export metadata and scorecard delta.

## Deliverables
- “Fetra AI Product-to-Campaign Case Study”
- Fetra review/first-look page
- Fetra versus Creatify/Dreamina workflow module
- behind-the-scenes Short
- affiliate CTA only after programme approval and live tracking verification

`RETEST` — obtain bounded credits and written output-rights clarification.
