# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan - Higgsfield GPT-6 Astra Paid Ads Bundle

**Status:** PLAN - rights, authentication, and spend gates apply  
**Classification:** Monitor only  
**Confidence:** C

## Business question
Can the Paid Ads bundle turn one rights-cleared product reference and fixed brief into three complete 15-second 9:16 ad variants in under 15 minutes per run, with exact product identity in the first 10 seconds, for no more than US per accepted output?

## Locked brief
- Product: Revlon One-Step Volumiser Plus 2.0; AIVP-held or supplier-authorized clean reference only.
- Input: one rights-cleared 15-second base ad, no third-party music.
- Instruction: create one complete 15-second 9:16 variant with a new first-three-second hook; preserve product geometry, red/black colourway, controls, remaining shot order, voiceover, and CTA.
- Output: H.264 MP4, 1080x1920, 30 fps, no watermark, monetization permitted.

## Three-run protocol
Run three fresh sessions with identical inputs/settings. Record balance before/after, setup/generation/elapsed time, retries, and every output including failures.

## Pass criteria
- Product distinctive throughout first 10 seconds in all runs; no material geometry, colour, bristle/barrel, or control changes.
- Only opening hook changes; remainder materially unchanged.
- Each run <=15 minutes and <=1 retry.
- Cost <=US per accepted output and <=US total.
- Written monetized-use and no-watermark permission.

## Stop conditions
Stop for password, 2FA, identity/payment request, paid credits, product-identity failure, projected cost over US, or unresolved rights.

## Evidence package
Rights record; prompts/settings; dated screenshots; all MP4s/failures; SHA-256 hashes; timing/credit ledger; frame QC at 0s/3s/10s/final; rights confirmation; control comparison.
