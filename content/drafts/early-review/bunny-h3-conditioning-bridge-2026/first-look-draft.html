BUNNY H3 Conditioning Bridge: First Look at Action-Logic Control

BUNNY H3 is a new ComfyUI conditioning adapter for MiniMax H3, not a new foundation model. Its stated focus is keeping character, action, target, object and scene-state relationships coherent in complex movement.[1][3]

What is actually documented

The public package contains a roughly 130MB safetensors adapter, an example workflow and three comparison videos. The node sits after H3 text conditioning and exposes alpha, magnitude-matching and bypass controls.[1][2][4]

The publisher says roughly 60% of its test cases improved to some degree, 20% showed no obvious difference and 10% regressed—but explicitly labels these approximate observations rather than a formal benchmark. AIVP has not verified them.[1]

The biggest unknown

The adapter does not document Ref2VA support. That matters for affiliate work because MiniMax H3's reference mode can accept product images, while the earlier project credited by BUNNY explicitly says its v1 does not support reference-conditioned generation.[5][9]

Rights and access

The model metadata points to MiniMax's community licence. Australia is not among its excluded territories, and MiniMax says it claims no rights over outputs, but the candidate's GitHub tree does not visibly include a licence or NOTICE file. Package compliance needs resolution before use in monetized production.[2][3][6]

Early verdict

Monitor only, confidence C, verified floor 17/100. The action-role idea is differentiated and the node is inspectable, but there is no independent benchmark, no AIVP run, no documented exact-product route and no practical hardware path on the current host. The planned test compares Bridge OFF versus ON across three paired LED-mask runs.
