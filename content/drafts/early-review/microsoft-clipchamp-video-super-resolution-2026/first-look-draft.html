Clipchamp adds local AI video upscaling on Windows 11

Clipchamp published Video Super Resolution on September 21, 2026. The Windows desktop feature creates a higher-resolution copy locally and offers targets up to 4K where the source is lower resolution.[1][2]

What is documented

Users can select one or more timeline clips, queue processing, preview the result and restore the original. Availability depends on supported Windows video-upscaling hardware, and the feature is not available in Clipchamp’s browser app.[1][2]

What this does not prove

Clipchamp warns that results vary and missing source detail cannot be recovered.[1][2] Documentation does not establish how well labels, controls and product geometry survive three repeated exports on our fixture.
