# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Impractical AI Motion Design

**Status:** PLAN — access, rights, security, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Impractical turn a rights-cleared recording of AI Video Picks’ live Tool Matcher into an accurate, on-brand 12-second 1080p launch video in under 25 minutes at zero cost, without hallucinating or replacing the real interface?

## 1. Case-study thesis

Impractical’s strongest differentiated claim is not physical-product generation; it is agent-directed motion design around a real software interface. The claim passes only if the exported video preserves AIVP’s actual UI and brand while materially reducing manual editing.

## 2. Real AIVP scenario and locked brief

- **Audience:** Australian creators and SMBs choosing AI-video tools.
- **Input:** a new rights-cleared 12-second screen recording of `https://aivideopicks.com/landing/recommend.html`, AIVP logo, existing brand colours/fonts, and this frozen copy: “Find your AI video tool. Answer a few questions. Get a practical match.”
- **Output:** 12-second 1920×1080, 30fps, H.264 MP4, no watermark, with the real Tool Matcher visible in the opening three seconds and a final `Try the Tool Matcher` card.
- **Control:** recreate the same locked brief with the current manual screen-recording plus editor workflow; measure baseline during the test rather than inventing it now.
- **Identity gate:** the AIVP logo, page structure, button labels and colour palette must remain recognizably accurate throughout every screen-based shot.
- **Settings:** same template/director choice, input files, copy and export settings across all three consistency runs; record any unavoidable random seed limitation.

## 3. Three bounded runs

1. **Baseline/default:** use the documented default workflow and frozen brief.
2. **Best documented workflow:** apply only vendor-published guidance, same assets and output specification.
3. **Consistency repeat:** repeat run 2 unchanged to quantify visual/copy drift.

Record exact inputs, version, settings, timestamps, queue/render duration, displayed and consumed credits, output path and SHA-256, errors/retries, and acceptance reason for every run.

## 4. Pass/fail criteria

**PASS only if all three runs:** export a playable 1920×1080 MP4; show the real Tool Matcher within 3 seconds; preserve the AIVP logo, UI labels and palette without fabricated claims; contain no watermark; complete in ≤25 minutes each; consume US$0 and no paid credits; require no corrective re-edit beyond selecting a documented take; and have written confirmation that AIVP may monetize and publish the outputs.

**FAIL:** any run substitutes a hallucinated interface, materially alters the logo or CTA, contains unreadable critical copy, exceeds 25 minutes, requires re-editing, fails export, or consumes money/paid credits.

**STOP immediately:** payment prompt, non-zero credit charge, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature step, package audit failure, unexpected network/file access, unclear input rights, unclear monetized-output rights, or terms beyond ordinary no-cost service use.

## 5. Evidence package

- source and claim ledger;
- original screen recording, logo and rights record;
- npm tarball hash, dependency lock and security-audit report before setup;
- screenshots of account/plan, model, settings and displayed cost;
- all three MP4 outputs, including failures, with SHA-256;
- first-frame, 3-second, midpoint and final-frame QC sheets;
- exact-copy OCR comparison and brand-colour/UI fidelity notes;
- timing, retry and credit/cost log;
- terms snapshot and written commercial-publication confirmation;
- before/after scorecard.

- transparent access, testing and affiliate disclosure;
