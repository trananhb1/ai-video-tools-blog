Narrative AI Video Editor First Look: Conversational Cuts on an Editable Timeline

Narrative presents a browser editor where creators upload footage, describe the edit in normal language, and receive a cut on a timeline that remains manually editable.[1] Unlike a generic text-to-video generator, its current pitch is reducing rough-cut work while preserving access to the source timeline.

What the vendor documents

  • Rough cuts, supercuts and vertical reels from uploaded footage, refined in chat.[1]
  • Transcript, captions, model modes, aspect-ratio controls and turn-by-turn version restore.[1]
  • 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5 output framing.[1]
  • US$20, US$40 and US$100 monthly plans with 2,000, 4,000 and 10,000 AI credits respectively; all advertise a three-day trial and 4K no-watermark rendering.[1]

Why AIVP is interested

A source-footage editor can preserve a real product more credibly than stock-only or text-only generation. For affiliate work, however, that remains a hard test: the exact product must stay distinctive throughout the first 10 seconds, and Narrative must not replace it with generic B-roll.

What remains unknown

AI Video Picks has not verified edit quality, repeatability, actual render time, per-operation credit burn, trial card requirements, export codecs, Australia-specific billing, current API availability or monetized-output rights. The older YC launch described editing and clipping APIs as forthcoming rather than live.[2]

Narrative's terms say uploaded material stays with the user and grant service-processing permission to produce the edit, but the retrieved language does not explicitly confirm monetized commercial rights for exported outputs.[4]

Planned test

We will ask Narrative to make the same 21-second 9:16 cut from one frozen, rights-cleared AIVP Revlon Plus 2.0 product-video asset set three times. A pass requires zero incremental cost, completion within 10 minutes per run, valid watermark-free export, exact narration, source-footage-only edits and the exact black/red brush visible and distinctive throughout seconds 0–10.

Early verdict

Weak fit pending testing — 50/100 verified floor, 100/100 potential ceiling, 50% evidence coverage, confidence C. The workflow is differentiated and relevant, but quality, consistency, credit economics and publication rights are unresolved. This is not a recommendation or review.
