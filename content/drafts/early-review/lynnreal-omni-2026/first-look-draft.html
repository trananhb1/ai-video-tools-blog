LynnReal-Omni First Look: Broad Video Control, Heavy Beta Requirements

LynnReal-Omni combines text/image/reference generation, pose and structural control, editing, repair and streaming routes in one early-beta model family.[1]

What is genuinely different

The project documents Standard four-step and Flash three-step models, reference-image workflows, native audio-video generation and ComfyUI support.[1][4] That breadth is meaningful, but it remains publisher evidence rather than an AIVP production result.

The AIVP hard stop

Public packages are ungated, yet Hugging Face reports approximately 279.6 GB storage for Standard and 117.5 GB for Flash.[3][4] This host has no NVIDIA GPU and insufficient free storage. No run was attempted.

Can it preserve a real affiliate product?

Reference inputs are documented, but no controlled run proves that a Revlon brush or any exact ASIN remains visually distinctive in the first 10 seconds. Product-affiliate suitability is therefore unresolved, not passed.

Rights

The MiniMax H3 community licence includes Australia but excludes several territories and imposes disclosure, attribution and acceptable-use duties. It does not provide supplier permission for product imagery.[6]

Verdict

Monitor only: 34/100 verified, confidence C. Wait for a secure no-cost hosted route and supplier-cleared inputs, then run three locked exact-product tests.
