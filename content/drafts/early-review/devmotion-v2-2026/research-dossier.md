## Primary findings

DevMotion says a product URL can supply logos, colours and screenshots, after which its agent creates script, scenes, mockups, animation, voiceover and music on an editable timeline.[2]

First-party docs describe plain-HTML projects, hosted OAuth MCP, REST rendering, webhooks and layer-level tools.[5][6]

v2's differentiator is visual self-inspection: render frames, inspect them and repair faults rather than trust a successful tool response.[1][2]

This is a vendor claim, not AIVP outcome evidence. Terms preserve users' content rights, and the homepage says exports may be used commercially.[2][4]
