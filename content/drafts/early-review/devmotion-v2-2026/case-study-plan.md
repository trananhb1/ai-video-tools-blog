# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — DevMotion v2

**Status:** PLAN — access, rights, security, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can DevMotion v2 turn the live AIVP Tool Matcher into an accurate 15-second vertical launch video, catch and repair its own layout faults, and deliver a monetizable MP4 in under 20 minutes at zero cost without replacing the real interface?

## 1. Thesis and locked brief

The differentiator is visual self-inspection over editable motion graphics. Use a rights-cleared 15-second recording of `https://aivideopicks.com/landing/recommend.html`, AIVP logo, URL, colours, and frozen copy: “Find the right AI video tool. Answer a few questions. Get a practical match.” Output: 15-second 1080×1920, 30fps H.264 MP4, clear English narration, licensed music, no watermark, real Tool Matcher visible by 0:03.

Control: current manual screen-recording/editor workflow with identical assets. Identity gate: preserve logo, page layout, labels, buttons and colours; no fabricated results, prices or claims. Lock model, dimensions, duration, FPS, assets and prompt.

## 2. Three bounded runs

1. Baseline/default agent behavior.
2. Vendor-documented product-URL/reference workflow.
3. Exact repeat of run 2 for consistency.

Record version, inputs/settings, pre/post inspection state, faults/patches, times, queue/render duration, displayed/consumed credits, output path/SHA-256, retries and acceptance reason.

## 3. Acceptance and stop conditions

**PASS only if all three:** real Tool Matcher by 0:03; accurate logo/UI/palette; playable 1080×1920 MP4; no watermark or invented claims; ≤20 minutes; US$0/no paid credits; no manual repair after agent inspection; monetized-output rights explicit.

**FAIL:** hallucinated/replaced UI, altered brand/CTA, unreadable critical text after inspection, export failure, >20 minutes, manual repair, or any cost.

**STOP:** payment/subscription prompt, paid credits, password/2FA/identity/payment/signature step, unclear rights, unexpected MCP permission/egress, or behavior outside zero-cost cap.

## 4. Evidence package

Source ledger; input/rights record; OAuth/MCP audit; plan/model/settings screenshots; self-detected fault/patch screenshots; all outputs including failures with SHA-256; QC at 0:00/0:03/mid/final; OCR/UI comparison; timing/retry/cost ledger; terms/free-export clarification; scorecard update.

## 5. Publishable outputs

Case study/review only after passing; buyer-guide module; behind-the-scenes Short; full methodology/access disclosure; no affiliate CTA before acceptance and tracking verification.

## 6. Boundary and decision

Vendor may provide documents, bounded access, media and factual corrections, but no editorial control, approval, guaranteed rating, recommendation or placement. Access does not guarantee positive coverage.

`RETEST` — obtain compliant contact, bounded no-cost MP4 access and export clarification, then audit OAuth/MCP before testing.
