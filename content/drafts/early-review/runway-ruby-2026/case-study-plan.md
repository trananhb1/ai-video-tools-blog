# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Runway Ruby

**Status:** PLAN — access, rights, spend and publication gates apply  
**Confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Ruby deliver materially useful HDR masters without changing the identity or colour of a filmed affiliate product?

## Falsifiable thesis
Ruby converts one rights-cleared SDR product master into HDR10 and ProRes/ACES output with no material product-colour or label drift, no clipping of defining highlights, under five minutes per 10-second run, and no more than US$2 effective cost per accepted output.

## Locked scenario and control
Use one AIVP-owned 9:16 ten-second LED-mask clip with calibrated colour chart, visible controller, labels, red/blue emitters and retained SDR master. Control is a manual colour-managed SDR-to-HDR conversion in the current editor. Do not use listing media without written permission.

## Three bounded runs
1. Ruby HDR10 at documented defaults.
2. Ruby ProRes 4444 or ACEScg using the same source.
3. Repeat Run 1 unchanged for consistency.

Record source rights and SHA-256, model/version, settings, start/end and queue/render times, displayed/consumed credits, every output including failures, waveform/vectorscope captures, frame QC, export metadata and post-test score delta.

## Evidence Package
Retain rights confirmation, source assets and hashes, exact tool/model version and settings, all three outputs including failures, timing and cost logs, first/mid/final frame QC, commercial-rights evidence, and the score delta versus control.

## Pass/fail
Pass only if the exact product is distinctive in the first 10 seconds; labels, geometry and controller remain unchanged; measured colour patches stay within the locked tolerance selected before access; no illegal clipping appears; two formats import correctly; and at least two runs are accepted. Fail on identity/colour drift, broken metadata, fewer than two accepted outputs, or cost above ceiling.

Stop immediately on payment/top-up, credential or 2FA step, unclear input/output rights, terms acceptance, or unavailable cost preview.

## Publishable package
Plan a Ruby case-study article, a Runway first-look/review update, an HDR buyer-guide module, and a behind-the-scenes Short. Include method and disclosures. Add an affiliate CTA only after programme acceptance and tracking verification.

`RETEST` — secure bounded access and lock the numeric colour tolerance before generation.
