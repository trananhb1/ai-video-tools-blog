# Case Study Plan: Can Synthesia’s New Stack Preserve a Real Product and Its Provenance?

## Business Question
Can Gemini Omni Flash plus Synthesia’s new preset and Content Credentials workflow produce a usable 30-second vertical product explainer while preserving exact product identity and verifiable AI provenance?

## Locked Brief
Use AIVP-owned or vendor-cleared images and footage of the same red/blue LED face mask from the prior specificity pilot. Produce a 30-second 9:16 explainer with the exact mask, controller, colourway, openings, and emitter pattern visible in the first 10 seconds. Use only approved factual claims. Output must retain C2PA provenance and include the preselected visible AI label.

## Control
Current reference-led image/video workflow plus manual provenance/disclosure checklist, using identical assets, script, duration, and QC rules.

## Three Bounded Runs
1. Gemini Omni Flash with the locked product reference and script-derived prompt.
2. Best available avatar-plus-space preset with the exact product source integrated as hero evidence.
3. Repeat the stronger route unchanged to measure consistency.

## Success Criteria
- Exact product is visually distinctive throughout the first 10 seconds.
- No geometry, controller, label, colourway, opening, or emitter-pattern drift.
- At least two of three outputs are usable without corrective regeneration.
- C2PA provenance survives download and re-import; the visible label is present as locked.
- No invented medical, performance, endorsement, or price claim.
- Setup under 30 minutes; winning render under five minutes.
- Effective cost no more than US per accepted output and no paid credits consumed without approval.

## Failure and Stop Conditions
Fail on product drift, missing provenance, missing disclosure label, factual invention, fewer than two usable outputs, or unmeasurable model selection. Stop on payment, top-up, paid-credit consumption beyond an approved allowance, credential or 2FA request, new terms acceptance, or unclear source/output rights.

## Evidence Package
Save rights records, source hashes, account region and plan, model/version, prompt and preset settings, credit estimate and consumption, all outputs including failures, render timings, first/mid/final frame QC, C2PA inspection, visible-label screenshot, and pre/post score delta.

## Publishable Outputs
Case-study article, First Look update, Synthesia review module, compliance workflow note, and behind-the-scenes Short. Add a release-specific affiliate CTA only after the current tracking link is verified.
