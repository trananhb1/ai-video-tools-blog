T8star VDN-MiniMax-H3 ComfyUI Bundle: First Look

The bundle packages an 82–83GB MiniMax H3/OpenVDN model set for ComfyUI, with text, first/last-frame, single/multiple-image, video and audio reference workflows.[1][2][4]

Why it is interesting

The publisher reports 9/9 successful mechanical compatibility runs for the pruned bundle on a 16GB RTX 4060 Ti, but explicitly says this is not universal quality or memory proof.[1] Reference inputs make it more relevant to exact-product video than generic text-only generation.

Why AIVP has not tested it

The current host has 74GB free storage and no detected NVIDIA GPU, so the model will not fit and cannot be meaningfully rendered here. No paid cloud compute is authorized.

License constraint

Australia is allowed, but the MiniMax H3 agreement excludes the EU, UK, South Korea and United States and restricts use and outputs outside the applicable territory.[3] That is a major constraint for globally distributed web and YouTube content.

Early verdict

Weak fit, confidence C, verified floor 43/100. The workflow is technically differentiated; output quality, product fidelity, creator-hardware speed and practical global publication rights remain unresolved.
