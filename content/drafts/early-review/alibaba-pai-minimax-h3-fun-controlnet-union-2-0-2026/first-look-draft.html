Alibaba PAI MiniMax-H3 Fun ControlNet Union 2.0: First Look

Alibaba PAI published a 13.59 GB control branch for MiniMax-H3 on September 22, 2026.[1][2] Compared with v1, it expands from five to eight control conditions, doubles control injection from five to ten transformer blocks, and changes the inpainting mask recipe to post-normalization.[1][3]

What it can do on paper

The same checkpoint supports Canny, Depth, HED, MLSD, Pose, Scribble, Layout and Gray video control plus inpainting. The documented path uses the VideoX-Fun Python project and a specific v2 configuration.[1][5]

Why this is not a review

We have not rendered an output. Product identity, output quality, consistency, speed, reliability and real compute cost are unknown. The documented stack requires large base-model assets and offloading even on one 80 GB GPU.[1]

Rights and AU fit

Australia is not among the licence exclusions, but commercial use carries attribution, territorial, disclosure and revenue-threshold conditions. MiniMax says it claims no rights over generated outputs.[4]

Preliminary verdict

Weak fit, 34/100 verified floor, confidence C. Structural control is differentiated, but local hardware friction and untested product fidelity prevent a recommendation.
