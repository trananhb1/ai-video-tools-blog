## Findings

Kling says a presenter image and sales script create a presenter video. Adding product images/information triggers presenter-only, presenter-with-product and close-up shots.[1] Controls include speed, aspect ratio, resolution, voice and music.[1]

The quick start documents API keys, usage reporting and a trial package, but login uses a mobile number or Kuaishou QR. Trial price, Video Commerce allowance, AU eligibility and three-run capacity are unstated.[2]

Global API packages start at US$700 for 5,000 units and expire after 180 days. Detailed pricing lists general models, avatars and lip sync but no Video Commerce tariff.[3][4]

Paid-service terms allow paid members lawful commercial output use and derivatives, excluding competing products, but do not clearly extend that grant to this API/Agent feature.[5] Users bear input-media, likeness and trademark rights.[5] Kling says source/generated media may be used for model optimisation after encryption and de-identification unless authorization is withdrawn.[6]

The workflow accepts product images and claims close-ups, but no AIVP artifact proves preservation of ASIN B0D2XTZN8V’s silhouette, openings, controller, straps, colourway and LED pattern in the first ten seconds.[1]
