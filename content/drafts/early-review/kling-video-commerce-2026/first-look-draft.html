Kling Video Commerce First Look

Kling’s September 7 update introduces API and Agent routes. A presenter image and sales script create a presenter video; product images and information are claimed to trigger presenter-only, presenter-with-product and close-up shots.[1]

Why it matters

The product inputs and close-ups make exact-product preservation testable. Controls include speech speed, aspect ratio, resolution, voice and music.[1]

Unknowns

Kling documents a trial package and API keys, but phone/Kuaishou authentication is required and Video Commerce’s free allowance is unstated.[2]

API packages start at US$700; the detailed table does not list a Video Commerce rate.[3][4]

Paid consumer terms allow commercial output use subject to lawful inputs, but API/Agent-specific rights remain unclear.[5]

Early verdict

40/100 verified floor; 100 ceiling; 40% coverage; confidence C; Weak fit pending testing. Exact ASIN fidelity, quality, consistency, speed, AU access and cost are unverified. No recommendation or affiliate CTA is included.
