# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Kling Video Commerce

**Status:** PLAN — authentication, rights, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Weak fit pending testing  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can Kling Video Commerce turn one presenter image, a locked script, product information and rights-cleared images of ASIN B0D2XTZN8V into three usable 30-second 9:16 affiliate videos while preserving the exact mask in the first ten seconds for no more than US$2 per accepted output?[1]

## Thesis and locked scenario

The falsifiable claim is that Kling’s automatic presenter-only, presenter-with-product and close-up sequencing preserves a real product for affiliate conversion.[1]

- **Audience:** Australian skincare buyers and solo creators.
- **Inputs:** rights-cleared adult presenter image plus likeness consent; three exact-product images; locked facts; 75-word English script; disclosure asset.
- **Output:** 30-second 1080×1920 H.264 MP4, clear voice and captions.
- **Control:** identical assets through Google Flow References → Veo 3.1 Fast, only within an approved no-cost allowance.

**Locked brief:** “Create a 30-second 9:16 presenter-led video for B0D2XTZN8V. Show the exact mask by second two. Include presenter-only, presenter-with-product and close-up shots. Preserve silhouette, openings, controller, straps, colourway and red/blue LED pattern. Use only supplied facts. No medical promise, invented testimonial, unsupported discount, competitor logo or generated price typography.”

## Three bounded runs

1. Agent Market defaults.
2. Vendor-recommended 9:16, 1080p, fixed voice/speed settings.[1]
3. Exact repeat of run 2 for drift.

Record input hashes/rights, route/version, settings, task ID, timing, displayed/final units, retries, output SHA-256, frame QC at 0/2/5/10/15/30 seconds, factual deviations and acceptance.[1][2]

## Acceptance and stop conditions

**PASS only if all three:** ≤10 minutes each; ≤US$2 per accepted output inside written complimentary access; 30±2 seconds at 1080×1920; exact product by second two and distinctive through second ten; all three shot types; no product substitution; clear narration; no visual replacement beyond deterministic captions/disclosure.[1]

**FAIL:** any identity failure; two unusable outputs; median over ten minutes; missing shot type; factual/medical invention; product-obscuring distortion; watermark; or cost over US$2.

**STOP:** payment/package prompt, unapproved credits, or phone/QR/password/2FA/identity/payment/signature requiring Tom.[2][3]

Also stop for unclear input/output rights or auto-publish permission.[5][6]

## Evidence package and deliverables

Save ledger, media/consents, hashes, locked script/facts, access/cost screenshots, settings/task IDs, all outputs, ffprobe metadata, contact sheets, QC/timing/error/cost logs, terms snapshots and before/after scorecards.

If all gates pass: evidence-led review, Kling-versus-Creatify module and behind-the-scenes Short. No affiliate CTA before acceptance and live tracking verification.

Vendor may provide documentation, complimentary access, media and factual corrections, but receives no editorial control, approval, guaranteed rating, recommendation or placement. Access does not guarantee positive coverage.
