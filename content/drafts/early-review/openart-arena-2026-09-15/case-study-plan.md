# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — OpenArt Arena

**Status:** PLAN — access, rights, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Weak fit  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Does OpenArt Arena’s Ads ranking correctly identify the model that best preserves one exact affiliate product under AIVP’s locked brief at an acceptable cost?

## 1. Locked AIVP scenario

- Product: rights-cleared LED face mask ASIN B0D2XTZN8V.
- Source: one frozen hero image, hashed before use.
- Output: one five-second 9:16 image-to-video clip per model, no generated typography or medical claim.
- Prompt: “Animate the exact supplied white LED mask on a neutral vanity with a slow push-in. Preserve shell shape, openings, controller, cable, colourway and LED layout. No redesign, replacement, text, person or accessory.”
- Test models: the top three models on Arena Ads v1.0 at test lock time.
- Control: Google Flow Veo 3.1 Fast reference-to-video with the same source and prompt.

## 2. Three bounded runs

Run the identical brief once through each of the top three Ads models using the same available resolution, duration and closest equivalent settings. This tests Arena’s routing prediction, not within-model repeatability. If the ranking predicts a winner, a later dedicated three-repeat model study is required before recommendation.

Record board snapshot/version, model/provider version, settings, queue/render/total time, predicted and actual debit, retries, output/hash, and first/mid/final-frame QC.

## 3. Pass, fail and stop

**PASS:** all three exports complete; the Arena leader wins or ties on a blinded product-identity rubric; the leader preserves the exact mask from frame one through second five; native 9:16; no major warping; cost no more than US$2 per usable clip; complete monetized-use rights confirmed.

**FAIL:** board leader scores materially below another model; product identity fails; fewer than three comparable outputs; hidden model/setting mismatch; or cost exceeds the ceiling.

**STOP immediately:** any unapproved paid-credit use, payment/top-up/subscription prompt, credential/2FA/identity/payment/signature step, unclear input or output rights, or predicted total above US$6.

## 4. Evidence package

Save Arena and methodology snapshots; prompt/model/setting lock; input rights and hash; before/after balances; all outputs including failures and hashes; blind-scoring sheet; first/mid/final frames; timing/retry/cost ledger; provider terms; and pre/post scorecard.

## 5. Publishable outputs

Publish an Arena methodology First Look or validation case study only after evidence review. Do not promote a model merely because it leads Arena; affiliate CTA requires separate programme acceptance and tracking verification.

## 6. Decision

RETEST — secure zero-cost bounded access to all three routed models and verify provider-specific rights before running.
