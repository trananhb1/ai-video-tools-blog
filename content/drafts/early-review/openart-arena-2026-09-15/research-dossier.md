## Primary findings

OpenArt Arena publishes separate video boards for overall, ads, film, animation, motion design, video editing and lip sync.[1]

At retrieval, Seedance 2.5 led the overall and ads boards; Wan 3.0 led video editing by one point over Seedance 2.5. The page displays 95% confidence-interval controls, so a one-point ordering should not be treated as a meaningful quality gap without inspecting the interval.[1]

The official methodology says prompts come from real creation use cases, each model receives the same brief, one output per model enters by a fixed rule, model identities are hidden, and left/right placement is randomized.[2]

The methodology also says standardized cost is planned rather than included in current quality scores.[2]

VentureBeat reports that OpenArt had not supplied final completed-judge count, total pairwise judgments or prompts per launch benchmark, and notes OpenArt’s commercial interest because it sells access to many evaluated models.[3]

Those omissions do not invalidate the board, but they prevent AIVP from treating it as a production oracle. AIVP must test the exact product brief, rights, cost and route itself.[2][3]
