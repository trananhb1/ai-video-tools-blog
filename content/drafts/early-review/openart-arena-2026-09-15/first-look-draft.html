OpenArt Arena ranks AI video models by creative job

OpenArt Arena launched public boards for overall video, ads, film, animation, motion design, editing and lip sync.[1][3] That job-level split is more useful than one universal score, but it is still a vendor-operated benchmark.

What the launch boards say

At retrieval, Seedance 2.5 led overall video and video ads, while Wan 3.0 led video editing by one point.[1] The page exposes 95% confidence intervals, so one-point differences should not be read as definitive separation.

Methodology strengths and gaps

OpenArt says models receive the same brief, one output enters by a fixed rule, identities are hidden and pair placement is randomized.[2] Standardized cost is not yet included in the quality score.[2] VentureBeat reports missing launch totals for completed judges, votes and prompts per board, and notes OpenArt’s commercial relationship with many ranked models.[3]

Verdict

Weak fit: 44/100 verified, confidence C. Arena is a useful shortlist, not proof that its leader preserves a specific affiliate product or offers the best cost-adjusted workflow. AIVP’s planned test runs the same LED-mask brief through the top three Ads models before using the board in buying advice.
