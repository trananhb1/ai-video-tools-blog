FastWan2.2 MLX Q8 First Look: Lower-Memory Video Generation on Apple Silicon?

Draft status: noindex, not published, no affiliate relationship.

What changed

This community package retains the upstream FastWan2.2 transformer and VAE but replaces the text encoder with an 8-bit version. Its author reports a 6.0 GB resident encoder and complete generations on a 32 GB M2 Max.[1]

Why it could matter

The package is public, ungated and labelled Apache-2.0. The reported five-second 832x480 generation took 311 seconds, which could make local Apple-Silicon experiments practical without a cloud API.[1][2]

Why we are not recommending it

  • The compatible LocalLab runtime is not publicly linked.
  • The model card explicitly directs stock mlx-video users to the unquantized source repository.
  • We have no AIVP render, repeated-run reliability evidence or exact-product fidelity result.
  • Generated-output and source-asset rights still need production review.

Our controlled three-run test is planned pending an auditable compatible loader and suitable Apple-Silicon host. Until then this is a launch brief, not a review.
