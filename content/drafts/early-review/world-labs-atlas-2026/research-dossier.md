## Launch materiality

## Primary-source findings

The official page says Atlas accepts spatial context and precise camera geometry rather than relying only on text camera instructions, can reconstruct scenes to explicit 3D outputs, and will power future versions of Marble.[1] It also says Atlas is entering early access with selected partners, not that it is generally available.[1]

The claims are vendor evidence, not AIVP output evidence. No public Atlas pricing, self-serve Atlas API model, generation latency, failure rate, Australia-specific access statement, or commercial terms for Atlas early access were found.

## Rights and cost gate

World Labs’ general terms grant free-account outputs only a personal, non-commercial license, while paid-account/API users receive commercial output rights subject to the terms.[3] The same terms state that pre-GA information may be confidential.[3] These general provisions do not establish that an Atlas early-access partner may publish test outputs or disclose results. Marble’s free plan exists, but commercial rights are listed for Pro and higher workflows; Marble is not Atlas.[4]

**Result:** no generation, application form submission, or account setup was attempted. Atlas testing is blocked until World Labs provides written permission for monetized publication and a bounded no-cost route that does not require payment, personal signature, confidential-review restrictions, or unapproved credits.

## Contact and outreach decision

World Labs publishes `hello@worldlabs.ai` on an older official research page and `support@worldlabs.ai` in its current documentation.[3][5] Neither is a published press/partnerships/affiliate contact. The standing policy requires affiliate questions to go to an official affiliate contact and forbids contact forms, so no message was sent and the early-access form was not submitted.
