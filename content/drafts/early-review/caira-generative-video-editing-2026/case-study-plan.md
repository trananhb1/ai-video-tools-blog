# Caira Generative Video Editing — Case Study Plan

**Decision:** Can Caira turn rights-cleared camera footage into four useful generative edits while preserving the exact filmed product and scene?

## Real scenario
Film or obtain vendor-cleared footage of one distinctive creator product in a real room. Use the same source clip for Caira Studio Relight, Reframe, Camera Motion and Set Extension. Compare against the untouched source plus the closest available manual edit.

## Three bounded runs
1. Relight and reframe the locked clip for 9:16.
2. Add one camera-motion or set-extension treatment without changing the product.
3. Repeat the strongest workflow unchanged to measure consistency.

## Pass
- Exact product shape, colour, controls, labels and accessories remain stable.
- Product is distinctive in the first 10 seconds.
- At least two of three outputs are usable.
- Original remains unchanged and generated files are clearly separated.
- Rights for monetized use, supplied assets and model outputs are documented.
- Setup under 30 minutes and effective cost no more than US$2 per accepted clip, excluding hardware purchase.

## Stop/fail
Stop on deposit/payment, account terms, shipping/customs cost, credentials or unclear rights. Fail on product drift, region block, fewer than two usable outputs or unmeasurable generation cost.

## Evidence
Save source rights, input hashes, app/model/version, settings, credit display, timing, all outputs including failures, frame contact sheets, SynthID/Content Credentials evidence, and scorecard delta.

## Deliverables
- “Caira Generative Video Editing Tested on a Real Creator Product” case study
- Caira first look/review update
- Camera-to-social workflow comparison
- 30–60 second behind-the-scenes Short
- Affiliate/creator CTA only after written programme and tracking verification

`RETEST` — obtain bounded beta access or a vendor-supplied remote test package.
