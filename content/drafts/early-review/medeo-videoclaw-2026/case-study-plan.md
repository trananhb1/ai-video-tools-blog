# AI Video Picks Case-Study Plan — Medeo VideoClaw

**Status:** PLAN — access, rights, spend and publication gates apply  
**Candidate classification:** Monitor only  
**Evidence confidence:** C  
**Business question:** Can VideoClaw turn one frozen social-trend brief and rights-cleared images of the Amazon B0D2XTZN8V LED face mask into three usable 30-second 9:16 affiliate videos while preserving the exact product in the first ten seconds, in no more than 15 minutes per run and at zero incremental cost?[1][5][7]

## 1. Case-study thesis

VideoClaw's strongest claimed differentiator is the connection from trend discovery to production and post-publish learning.[1][3][4] The test will isolate the pre-publish half of that claim: whether a frozen trend signal can become a product-specific affiliate video without breaking identity or requiring a multi-tool rebuild.

## 2. Real AIVP scenario and locked brief

- **Audience:** Australian skincare buyers and budget-conscious creators.
- **Product:** LED face mask, Amazon ASIN B0D2XTZN8V, described in the existing AIVP portfolio as a US$49.99 test product.
- **Inputs:** one frozen, date-stamped trend packet supplied identically to all runs; three rights-cleared manufacturer-authorized or AIVP-created product reference images; one fixed 75-word script; fixed logo/disclosure assets. Do not run until image reuse and transformation rights are recorded.
- **Output:** 30-second 1080×1920 H.264 MP4, English narration, captions and one disclosure-safe call to action.
- **Control:** the same inputs through AIVP's established Dreamina/reference-image product workflow, only within an already approved no-cost allowance.

**Locked brief:** “Create a 30-second 9:16 product video for the B0D2XTZN8V LED face mask. Open on the supplied exact mask within two seconds. Preserve its silhouette, eye/mouth openings, red-and-blue LED appearance, controls and accessories. Use only the supplied factual script and frozen trend packet. Friendly, practical tone for Australian viewers. No medical claims, invented testimonials, unsupported discounts or competitor logos. Export a captioned H.264 MP4 with no watermark.”

## 3. Three bounded runs

1. **Baseline:** VideoClaw/Medeo default documented workflow with the locked packet and assets.
2. **Best documented workflow:** vendor-recommended settings supplied in writing, with no paid extra.
3. **Consistency repeat:** exact repeat of run 2 to measure product drift, timing and failures.

Record exact inputs, trend sources selected, generated research/script changes, model/version, settings, connected services, task IDs, start/end and queue/render duration, displayed and consumed credits, output path and SHA-256, errors/retries, first-10-second contact sheet, factual deviations and acceptance reason.

## 4. Acceptance and stop conditions

**PASS only if all three runs:** finish in ≤15 minutes each; cost US$0; export 30±2 seconds at 1080×1920 without a watermark; show the exact product by second two and keep it distinctive through second ten; preserve all locked product traits with no substituted model; use no unsupported health, price or trend claim; produce clear English narration/captions; require no external re-edit beyond file naming.[5][7]

**FAIL:** any two unusable outputs, product identity failure in any run, median elapsed time above 15 minutes, factual invention, broken audio/captions, watermark, or manual reconstruction outside Medeo.

**STOP immediately:** payment or upgrade prompt; any paid-credit consumption; request for password, 2FA, identity, payment details or signature; unapproved social-channel publishing; unclear input/media rights; output licence conflict; or permission request broader than read-only trend research.[5][6]

Do not connect AIVP social accounts during this case study. Publishing/analytics automation is outside scope and requires a separate security and account-permission audit.[4]

## 5. Evidence package

Save the citation ledger; frozen trend packet and source URLs; input-rights record; all reference assets; prompt/script/settings export; screenshots of access, model, permissions and displayed cost; network/API record where permitted; all outputs including failures; ffprobe metadata; first-ten-second contact sheets; timing/credit/error log; SHA-256 hashes; terms/media-rights snapshot; and before/after scorecards.

## 6. Publishable deliverables

If all runs and rights gates pass: publish an evidence-led case-study module, a First Look upgraded to Review language, a buyer-guide comparison block and a 30–60-second behind-the-scenes Short. Add no affiliate CTA unless a programme is accepted and tracking is live-verified.

## 7. Vendor boundary and decision

Vendor may provide documentation, bounded complimentary access, media and factual corrections. Vendor receives no editorial control, pre-publication approval, guaranteed rating, recommendation, affiliate placement or campaign commitment. Access does not guarantee positive coverage.
