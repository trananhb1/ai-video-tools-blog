## Primary findings

Medeo announced VideoClaw on September 10, 2026 as an agent that identifies trends, researches opportunities and carries ideas into scripts, visuals, voiceovers, music, scenes and finished social video.[1]

Official feature pages describe keyword, hashtag, creator and competitor monitoring; viral-structure analysis; production packs containing scripts, storyboards, sources and prompts; multi-platform scheduling; comment analysis; and a performance-to-next-video feedback loop.[2][3][4]

The launch release says the product is available now, while Medeo's pricing page lists a US$0 plan with chat-based creation, generative features, an editor and 1 GB storage. The page does not state whether VideoClaw trend monitoring, channel connections or three complete test renders are included free, and watermark removal is shown as a paid-plan capability.[1][5]

The published API supports media import by URL, recipe discovery, video creation, rendering and status polling using an API key. This is strong integration evidence, but the documentation does not establish that VideoClaw's trend-discovery and publishing functions are exposed through the same API.[7]

## Product specificity, rights and commercial limits

Medeo can import media by URL through its API and its broader workflow accepts user media, but no AIVP artifact proves that VideoClaw preserves an exact ASIN's shape, colourway, controls and accessories in the first ten seconds. Product-affiliate suitability remains unverified.[7]

The May 7 terms grant users a non-exclusive, worldwide, revocable licence to use, reproduce, display and distribute complete Medeo outputs for lawful purposes. Medeo-provided elements may only be used in final combined outputs and may not be extracted or isolated.[6]

Those terms are explicit enough to justify limited rights points, but their revocability, third-party model/media dependencies and absence of a launch-specific media-reproduction grant block publication until clarified.[6]
