Film Studio Skills First Look

Film Studio Skills packages seven agent instructions for script breakdown, reference locking, asset passports, static stress tests and generation-ready shot prompts.[1][2]

It is a preproduction workflow, not a video generator. Its strongest idea is enforcing persistent product and character descriptors before generation. Its biggest blockers are the absence of a tracked license, untested repeatability, and a setup step that edits project instruction files.

AIVP verdict

Promising workflow candidate, not recommended yet. Verified floor 38/100, confidence C. Keep it sandboxed until licensing is clear and three controlled planning runs pass the exact-product gate.
