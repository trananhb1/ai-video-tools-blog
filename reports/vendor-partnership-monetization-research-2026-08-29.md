## 3. Ethical Editorial Model (no pay-to-rank)

**The single most important rule:** ranking order on aivideopicks.com is determined by evidence in the article, not by commission rate. A vendor paying 50% cannot buy position 1 over a vendor paying 25% on the same listicle. The publish-gate (`strategy/aivp-revenue-publish-gate`) is the existing enforcement mechanism — every price, ranking, and "as of" claim must be backed by a fresh public source ≤90 days old, or the article blocks. **No new exception is being proposed here.**

**The lanes (visual model):**

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Editorial lane (ranked by evidence + testing, no $$)         |
|  - Reviews, comparisons, "best of"                            |
|  - Subject to publish-gate                                    |
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Disclosure lane (commercial, disclosed)                      |
|  - Affiliate links with rel="nofollow sponsored"              |
|  - "FTC disclosure" banner present on every page              |
|  - Coupon/deal page (ai-video-deals-coupons-2026.html)        |
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Sponsored lane (separated)                                   |
|  - If/when introduced: clearly marked "Sponsored by [Vendor]" |
|    with a distinct visual style; never inserted into the      |
|    editorial ranking; published only after human approval.    |
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
```

**Per-article commercial-integrity gate (unchanged):**
- Every price, tier, coupon, ranking, and "as of" marker must trace to a fresh public source retrieved in the publish-gate run.
- Affiliate rel="nofollow sponsored" enforcement is a hard deploy block.
- "Sora 2 sunset" article is a good test case: the existing `how-to-export-sora-videos-before-shutdown.html` already cites the dates, but should be re-verified ≤90 days.

**Conflict-of-interest policy (proposed addition to `CLAUDE.md`):**
- A vendor that sponsors an article may not appear in the "top 3" of any "best of" listicle on the same site for 12 months from the sponsored date.
- Editorial "best of" lists are reviewed quarterly by Tom and cannot be reordered in exchange for compensation.
- Sample products, free credits, or review-tier accounts received from a vendor are disclosed on the article page (alongside the FTC disclosure), with a single sentence naming the consideration.

**Why this matters now:** the **HeyGen 50% Aug 1 – Oct 31, 2026 promo** + **Pictory 40%-first-payment-only** + **Synthesia 25%** are all reasons a vendor could pressure for ranking position. The pre-existing publish-gate already blocks "unsupported ranking claims" (`FAIL_UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM`); this report does not propose any change to that block. The proposed addition above strengthens the *sponsorship-vs-ranking* separation, which the publish-gate does not currently police.
