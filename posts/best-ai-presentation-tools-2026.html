How We Ranked These AI Presentation Tools

Every tool was scored on five equally weighted criteria (20% each):

  1. Design Quality (20%): Layout polish, typography, visual consistency, and how professional the output looks without manual adjustments.
  2. AI Capability (20%): How well the AI understands prompts, generates relevant content, structures slides logically, and handles edge cases (data-heavy slides, minimal prompts).
  3. Speed (20%): Time from prompt to a presentable deck. We measured wall-clock time for each of the 3 test briefs.
  4. Collaboration (20%): Sharing options, team features, export formats, embed capability, and viewer analytics.
  5. Pricing Value (20%): Output quality per dollar at the entry-level paid plan, weighted by free tier generosity.

All tools were tested on paid plans (except Google Slides, which is free). We paid for every subscription ourselves. Full methodology at our editorial methodology page.
