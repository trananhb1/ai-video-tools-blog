How to Stack Savings

Getting the best price on AI video tools is not just about finding a coupon code. Here are five strategies that can save you 30% to 60% compared to paying full monthly rates.

1. Always Choose Annual Billing

Every AI video tool charges less per month on annual plans. The savings typically range from 20% to 40%. A tool that costs $30 per month drops to $18–$24 per month with annual billing. Only commit to annual once you have tested the tool and know it fits your workflow.

2. Start on the Free Tier, Then Upgrade Strategically

Never pay for a tool you have not tested. Use the free tier or trial first. Once you are sure you need paid features, watch for seasonal promotions — Black Friday, New Year, and back-to-school periods often bring the deepest discounts of the year.

3. Ask About Education and Nonprofit Discounts

If you are a student, teacher, or part of a nonprofit organization, many tools offer 25% to 50% off standard pricing. Synthesia, HeyGen, and Descript all have education programs. Contact their sales teams with proof of eligibility.

4. Combine Coupon Codes with Annual Plans

Some coupon codes work on top of annual billing discounts. For example, using the Zebracat code 6YAZM9V5 on an annual plan stacks the 15% coupon discount with the annual billing savings, giving you the lowest possible price.

5. Use Multiple Free Tools Together

You do not have to choose just one tool. Use HeyGen (free tier) for AI avatar videos, Fliki (5 min/month) for text-to-video clips, and Submagic (3 videos/month) for caption styling — all for free. Combining free tiers across tools lets you produce a variety of content without paying anything.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these AI video tool coupon codes verified?

Yes. Every coupon code and deal on this page is verified by our team before publishing. We test each code at checkout to confirm it applies the stated discount. We update this page regularly and remove any expired codes. If you find a code that does not work, please contact us so we can investigate.

Can I get a refund if I do not like an AI video tool?

Most AI video tools offer refund windows ranging from 7 to 30 days. HeyGen and Synthesia offer 7-day refund policies on paid plans. Pictory and Fliki typically provide 14-day money-back guarantees. Always check the specific refund terms on the tool's pricing page before purchasing, and start with a free tier or trial whenever possible to test before committing.

Do AI video tools offer student or education discounts?

Runway has the most transparent program: verified students and educators get 25% off via SheerID. ElevenLabs offers a 50% student discount on the Creator plan via SheerID verification. Synthesia and HeyGen will discuss education and nonprofit pricing if you contact sales directly. Descript does not run a public student program but participates in FounderPass, which gives eligible startups a 50% discount on annual plans. Always verify eligibility directly with the tool's sales team.

Is annual billing always cheaper than monthly?

Yes, in almost every case. Annual billing typically saves 20% to 40% compared to paying monthly. For example, a tool that charges $30 per month might drop to $20 per month when billed annually. The trade-off is committing upfront for 12 months. We recommend testing a tool on its free tier or monthly plan first, then switching to annual once you are confident it fits your workflow.

Can I stack multiple discounts or coupons on one AI video tool?

Generally, no. Most AI video tools only accept one coupon or discount code per purchase. However, you can often combine strategies: use a coupon code on top of annual billing savings, or sign up during a seasonal sale for extra savings. Some tools also offer additional credits or features during Black Friday or end-of-year promotions that stack with your existing plan.
