What Is Kommodo?

Kommodo is an AI screen recorder that does something Loom and most other screen capture tools do not: when you finish recording, Kommodo automatically generates a formatted, step-by-step SOP with screenshots alongside your video. You get both the walkthrough video and the written guide from a single recording session.

The use case is tightly focused: process documentation. Software onboarding, IT support playbooks, customer success walkthroughs, internal how-to guides. Any workflow you can demonstrate on a screen, Kommodo captures and documents at the same time.

It runs as a Chrome extension. You hit record, do the thing you normally do, stop recording, and Kommodo hands you a shareable link with the video, transcript, and SOP all attached. No editing required.

Key Features

AI SOP Generation

The core feature. As you record, Kommodo tracks your mouse clicks, scroll events, and screen state. After recording, the AI converts these into a numbered step guide with annotated screenshots. The result is a Confluence-style document that non-technical readers can follow without watching the video. This replaces the most tedious part of process documentation: writing it up after the fact.

AI Transcription

Every recording gets a full text transcript automatically. No setup, no integration required. On paid plans, transcripts feed into a searchable library, so your team can find any procedure by searching for keywords — "how to add a user" surfaces the right recording without anyone needing to tag it manually.

Screen + Webcam Recording

Records your full screen, a specific browser tab, or an application window. Picture-in-picture webcam overlay is optional. Audio from your microphone is captured by default. No time limit on recordings, no watermark on the free tier.

AI Meeting Summaries (Paid)

On paid plans, Kommodo connects to Google Calendar and can join scheduled meetings to record, transcribe, and summarize. The summary includes action items and decisions extracted from the transcript. This is a different use case from SOP creation but useful for teams that want one tool for both async documentation and meeting notes.

Shareable Links

Every recording generates a link you can share with anyone — no account required for viewers. The link shows the video player, the transcript, and the SOP side by side. You can share internally via Slack, email, or embed in Notion or Confluence pages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kommodo really free?

Yes. Kommodo's free tier includes unlimited screen and webcam recordings with no watermark, no time limit per recording, AI transcription, and automatic SOP generation. Videos are stored for 60 days on the free plan. Paid plans ($9/month annual or $15/month monthly) add permanent storage, unlimited AI assistant usage, AI meeting summaries, and a searchable video library.

How is Kommodo different from Loom?

Loom is primarily async video messaging — you record, share a link, and the viewer watches. Kommodo does the same but adds AI that automatically generates a formatted step-by-step SOP with screenshots from every recording. If you are documenting a process (IT onboarding, software training, customer support playbooks), Kommodo produces both the video and the written guide simultaneously. Loom requires you to write the SOP yourself.

What is the difference between Kommodo and Scribe?

Scribe captures only screenshots and mouse clicks to build step-by-step guides — it does not record video. Kommodo records full screen video with audio and generates both the video and a step-by-step guide with screenshots. If you need video walkthroughs alongside your documentation, Kommodo is the better choice. If you only need lightweight screenshot-based guides with no video, Scribe is simpler.

Can Kommodo replace Synthesia or HeyGen for training videos?

No — they serve different purposes. HeyGen and Synthesia create polished, presenter-led avatar videos from scripts, ideal for formal training courses, compliance modules, and customer-facing content. Kommodo records your actual screen and generates SOPs from real workflows, making it best for process documentation and internal how-to guides. Most teams use both. Does Kommodo work for customer support teams?

Yes. Customer support teams record how-to walkthroughs for common issues and share the auto-generated SOP link instead of writing tickets manually. The searchable video library on paid plans lets new support reps find any procedure by keyword without manual tagging.

How much does Kommodo cost?
