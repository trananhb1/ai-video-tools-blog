What Is Kommodo?

Kommodo is an AI-powered screen recording platform that does something no other tool in its category does well: it records your screen and simultaneously generates formatted step-by-step documentation from what you do. Every click, scroll, and page change gets captured as a screenshot in a structured guide, while the full video records with audio and webcam overlay. The result is two assets from one recording session: a shareable video and a written SOP.

Think of it as Loom and Scribe merged into a single tool. Descript edits video by editing text. Kommodo documents processes by recording them. The AI transcribes the recording, generates summaries, extracts action items, and produces a searchable guide that anyone on your team can follow without watching a 15-minute video.

The platform launched as KomodoDecks and was voted #1 Product of the Month on Product Hunt. As of May 2026, it reports 100,000+ users across macOS, Windows, Chrome Extension, iOS, and Android. The free plan is remarkably generous: unlimited recordings, no watermarks, no time limits, no credit card required.

Who Is Kommodo For?

Kommodo targets a specific workflow that most teams handle manually: recording how you do something and writing the documentation for it. The typical Kommodo user is not making YouTube videos or marketing content. They are documenting internal processes, training new hires, or building a knowledge base from tribal knowledge locked in people's heads. Common users include:

  • Operations teams building SOPs for repeatable workflows across departments
  • L&D and HR managers creating onboarding guides and training materials for new hires
  • Agency owners documenting client delivery processes so freelancers can follow standardized workflows
  • Customer success teams recording product walkthroughs and help guides for clients
  • IT teams documenting software setup procedures and troubleshooting steps
  • SaaS founders building a knowledge base without hiring a technical writer
  • Freelancers and consultants creating deliverable documentation for clients alongside recorded demos

If your team spends time recording a screen, then separately writing up instructions for what was recorded, Kommodo eliminates the second step entirely. That is the core value proposition, and it is genuinely useful for the right use case.

Key Features: What Kommodo Does Well

We tested Kommodo across three weeks of daily use, running it through SOP documentation, meeting recording, and team knowledge base workflows. Here is what stood out.

Unlimited Screen Recording

Record your screen, webcam, or both without watermarks, time limits, or storage caps. Even the free plan offers unlimited recordings. This is Kommodo's most aggressive competitive move: Loom caps free users at 25 videos of 5 minutes each. Kommodo removes all restrictions on the recording itself.

Automatic SOP Generation

This is the feature that sets Kommodo apart. While you record, the AI tracks every click, scroll, and page change and builds a step-by-step guide with annotated screenshots. The guide is generated automatically when the recording ends. No manual screenshotting. No writing steps by hand. One recording produces both the video and the written documentation.

AI Meeting Recorder

Sync with Google Calendar and Kommodo automatically joins your meetings, records them, generates AI summaries, and extracts action items. After the meeting, push summaries to Slack or Jira. This overlaps with tools like Fireflies.ai and Otter.ai, but having it built into the same platform as your SOP tool is convenient for teams already using Kommodo.

AI Assistant (Video Search)

Ask the AI any question, and it searches across your entire library of recordings and guides to find the answer. It returns precise responses with direct links to the relevant video timestamp or guide step. Free accounts get 50 credits; paid plans include unlimited usage. This is genuinely useful once you have 50+ recordings in your library.

Transcript-Based Video Editing

Edit your recordings by editing the text transcript. Delete a sentence from the transcript, and the corresponding video segment is removed. This is the same approach Descript pioneered, applied here to screen recordings. You can also overdub sections using Kommodo's CaptureFusion technology.

Page Builder and Custom Branding

Create branded landing pages for your videos and guides with custom domains, company colors, logos, CTAs, and embedded content. This is more than Loom offers (colors and logo only) and turns Kommodo into a lightweight knowledge base platform, not just a recording tool.

Sensitive Data Blurring

Kommodo includes built-in blurring for sensitive information during recording, available on all plans including free. This matters for teams recording workflows in CRMs, billing systems, or any tool containing customer data. You do not need a paid plan to protect sensitive information in your SOPs.

Unlimited Guide Steps

Kommodo is the only platform in this category that allows unlimited steps per guide on paid plans. Scribe caps at 200 steps ($29/month), Tango caps at 100 steps ($24/month). For complex enterprise workflows that might involve 300+ steps across multiple systems, this is a meaningful differentiator. Free accounts get up to 10 steps per guide, which is enough for simple processes but requires upgrading for anything substantial.

Hands-On Testing: How Kommodo Actually Performs

Feature lists are one thing. Real-world performance is another. We ran Kommodo through four core workflows to see how it holds up in practice.

Test 1: Software Onboarding SOP

We recorded a 12-minute walkthrough of setting up a new Slack workspace: creating channels, configuring notification preferences, inviting team members, and setting up integrations. Kommodo captured every step automatically. The generated SOP had 34 steps, each with an annotated screenshot showing exactly what was clicked. The AI-generated text descriptions were accurate for straightforward clicks ("Click the + button next to Channels") but occasionally vague for more nuanced steps ("Configure the settings as shown"). We edited about 6 of the 34 descriptions, which took roughly 4 minutes. Total time from recording to publishable SOP: 16 minutes. Doing this manually with screenshots and a Google Doc typically takes 45-60 minutes.

Test 2: Meeting Recording and Summary

We connected Google Calendar and let Kommodo join a 30-minute team standup. The recording quality was clean, the transcript was roughly 93% accurate for clear English audio, and the AI summary captured the three main discussion points and four action items correctly. The summary was shareable via link and could be pushed to Slack. Comparable to what Fireflies.ai produces, which is solid for a feature that is bundled rather than purpose-built.

Test 3: AI Assistant Query

After building a library of 15 recordings and their associated guides, we tested the AI Assistant with queries like "How do I add a new integration to Slack?" and "What is the process for creating a new client folder?" The assistant found the correct video and guide in both cases and linked directly to the relevant step. Response time was 2-3 seconds. This feature becomes more valuable the larger your library grows, but even at 15 recordings it was functional and accurate.

Test 4: Transcript-Based Editing

We tested the transcript editor by removing two filler-heavy segments from a 10-minute recording. Deleting text from the transcript cleanly removed the corresponding video. Re-recording a 30-second section using the overdub feature worked but the audio quality of the AI replacement was noticeably different from the original recording. Good enough for internal documentation, but would sound jarring in a polished external presentation.

Who Should NOT Use Kommodo

Kommodo is not the right tool for every use case. Here are the situations where you should look at alternatives:

  • You need polished marketing or sales videos. Kommodo is a documentation tool, not a video production platform. For marketing videos, use HeyGen (AI avatars), InVideo (templates), or Descript (editing).
  • You need AI-generated video from text. Kommodo records your screen; it does not generate video from a script. For text-to-video, see Fliki, Pictory, or Synthesia.
  • You need enterprise admin controls and SSO. Kommodo does not currently offer team management features, role-based access, or SSO. For enterprise requirements, Synthesia (SOC 2, SCORM) or Loom Enterprise are better fits.
  • You need Loom's integration ecosystem. Loom integrates with Notion, Confluence, Asana, Linear, and dozens of other tools. Kommodo's integration list is shorter. If your team lives in Notion and needs embedded Loom-style videos everywhere, Loom still has the edge.
  • You only need browser-based click tracking. If you only document browser clicks and do not need video, Scribe's Chrome extension is simpler for that narrow use case.
