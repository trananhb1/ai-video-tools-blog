At a Glance: How They Differ

Both Elai and Synthesia produce AI avatar videos for corporate training. Both require Enterprise plans for SCORM export. Both offer free trials. The practical difference is this: Synthesia is a polished, studio-quality platform for linear training video at a lower entry price. Elai is a more L&D-specific platform with interactive features — branching, quizzes, Avatar Dialogs — available below Enterprise.

If your team primarily produces "talking head explains the policy" training modules and wants the best avatar quality per dollar, Synthesia wins. If your team builds scenario-based training where learners make choices inside the video, Elai is the better fit without paying Enterprise rates.

Avatar Quality

Synthesia has the stronger avatar library by most measures. The Expressive avatar line shows natural eye movement, upper-body gesture variation, and spontaneous micro-expressions that Elai's stock avatars do not replicate. Synthesia's 230+ stock avatars also cover more demographic diversity than Elai's 80+.

Elai's stock avatars are professional and credible for structured training content — the avatar delivers the script with correct lip-sync and a neutral, professional presentation. For compliance modules, onboarding narration, and process walkthroughs, Elai's quality is sufficient. For executive communications or any content where the avatar's realism reflects on the brand, Synthesia's Expressive line is noticeably better.

On custom avatars: Synthesia includes Personal Avatars (a 30-second video upload) on the Starter plan ($18/mo), which is a meaningful advantage. Elai's Selfie Avatar is available on the Team plan (~$100/mo annual). If you need a custom avatar of a real person and do not want to pay $100/month, Synthesia Starter delivers that at $18/month.

L&D-Specific Features: Where Elai Has the Edge

Elai was built for L&D practitioners, and it shows in the feature set available on standard paid plans. The clearest differentiator is Avatar Dialogs — an interactive video format where the avatar presents a scenario and learners choose a response. The video branches based on the choice. This is a genuine scenario-based learning capability that Synthesia does not offer below Enterprise.

Built-in quizzes let course creators add comprehension checks directly inside the video player. Learners answer questions without leaving the video. Synthesia has no quiz feature on paid plans below Enterprise.

PowerPoint import is Elai's strongest workflow feature. Upload a deck and Elai creates one video scene per slide, pulling narration from speaker notes. A 24-slide compliance training deck becomes a production-ready video in roughly an hour including edits. Synthesia does not have a native PowerPoint import — you manually recreate slides inside Synthesia's editor.

Screen recording overlay lets Elai combine avatar narration with screen capture for software training. The avatar appears alongside the screen recording, talking through each step. Synthesia does not include this feature.

For L&D practitioners who need to build interactive training content without paying Enterprise rates, Elai's standard plans offer meaningful capabilities that Synthesia does not match until Enterprise.

Language Support

Synthesia supports 140+ languages — roughly twice Elai's 80+ languages. For multinational organizations deploying training content across non-Western markets (East Asia, Southeast Asia, Middle East), Synthesia's wider language coverage is a practical advantage.

Elai's 80+ language translation is sufficient for most European and major global language rollouts. Elai's one-click translation is accurate in the major European languages and produces correct lip-sync in German, Spanish, French, and Portuguese. Both tools rely on text-to-speech for translated narration rather than dubbing a real voice — different from HeyGen's voice-clone translation approach.

If your organization deploys training in 10+ languages including smaller regional markets, Synthesia's 140+ is a real advantage. For English + 3-5 major languages, both tools cover you.

SCORM and LMS Integration: Enterprise on Both

This is the most misunderstood aspect of both platforms. As of May 2026, both Elai and Synthesia gate SCORM export to Enterprise plans only. Neither tool includes SCORM on any paid plan below Enterprise.

What this means in practice:

  • If your LMS supports standard MP4 video upload (most do), you can use Elai Basic or Synthesia Starter to produce video and manually upload it. SCORM packaging is not required for basic LMS delivery.
  • If your L&D program requires formal SCORM 1.2 or SCORM 2004 compliance — for progress tracking, pass/fail scores, and completion status in the LMS record — you need Enterprise on either platform.
  • Synthesia Enterprise and Elai Enterprise both support SCORM. For large organizations, pricing is negotiated; typical contracts run $20,000–$100,000+ annually depending on seat count and feature set.

Elai's advantage here is that its Avatar Dialogs and quizzes provide LMS-like tracking inside the video even on non-Enterprise plans — learners get feedback and the course creator can see completion data. This is not SCORM-native, but it fills some of the functionality gap for teams that cannot afford Enterprise.

Verdict

For a solo L&D practitioner or a small team producing straightforward talking-head training video, Synthesia Starter at $18/month is hard to beat. The avatar quality is better, the library is larger, the language coverage is broader, and the price is lower.

For teams that build scenario-based training — where learners make choices, face consequences, and get quiz feedback — Elai is the better tool without paying enterprise rates. Avatar Dialogs, quizzes, and PowerPoint import are features Synthesia reserves for Enterprise contracts that can cost $20,000+ per year. Elai gives you a meaningful subset of that capability at $23/month.

If SCORM LMS compliance is a hard requirement, budget for Enterprise on either platform — it is not available below Enterprise on either tool. Both platforms support standard MP4 upload to most LMS systems without SCORM, which covers the majority of teams that do not need formal pass/fail tracking in their LMS records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Elai better than Synthesia?

Elai is better for L&D teams who need interactive branching, quizzes, and PowerPoint import at sub-Enterprise pricing. Synthesia is better on avatar quality, language breadth, and entry price. Both gate SCORM to Enterprise. For most solo trainers, Synthesia Starter at $18/month wins on price. For teams building interactive scenarios, Elai wins.

Do Elai or Synthesia include SCORM on paid plans?

No. Both tools gate SCORM export to Enterprise plans only — this is an industry-wide standard for AI avatar platforms. Teams without a hard SCORM requirement can upload standard MP4 video to most LMS platforms without needing Enterprise on either tool.

Which is cheaper, Elai or Synthesia?

Synthesia Starter is cheaper at $18/month (annual) vs Elai Basic at $23/month. At the team/mid tier, Synthesia Creator is ~$67/month annual while Elai Team is ~$100/month annual. Synthesia is the cheaper option at both price levels, but Elai includes more L&D-specific features at the higher tier.

Can I use Synthesia without a custom avatar?

Yes. Synthesia provides 230+ stock avatars on all plans including the free tier. Synthesia Starter also includes 3 Personal Avatars (custom 30-second video upload) so you can use your own face from the first paid plan. Stock avatars are sufficient for most training content.

Does Elai have a free plan?

Elai offers a free trial with limited minutes. It does not have a permanent free tier the way Synthesia does. Synthesia's free plan provides 10 minutes/month of watermarked video indefinitely — useful for testing whether the platform fits your workflow before committing to a paid plan.

What LMS platforms work with Elai and Synthesia?

For standard MP4 upload (no SCORM): virtually all LMS platforms work with either tool — Moodle, TalentLMS, Cornerstone, Docebo, 360Learning, Workday Learning, and others all accept video file uploads. For SCORM-packaged deployment, both tools require Enterprise plans with custom pricing.
