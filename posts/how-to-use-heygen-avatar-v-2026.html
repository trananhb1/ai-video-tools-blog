This is HeyGen's standout feature, launched April 8, 2026. Avatar V creates a photorealistic digital twin of you from just 15 seconds of webcam footage.

  4. HeyGen processes your recording in 1-2 minutes

Tips for the best recording:

  • Face the camera directly, eyes at lens level

  • Don't worry about what you say — the AI extracts your appearance, not your words

Step 3: Write Your Script (3-5 Minutes)

After selecting your avatar, HeyGen opens the scene editor. Click the script panel on the right side to start writing.

How to Write a Good Avatar Script

  • Write for speaking, not reading. Short sentences. Conversational tone. Imagine you're explaining something to a colleague over coffee.
  • Aim for 130-150 words per minute. That's natural speaking pace. A 500-word script produces roughly a 3-minute video.
  • Front-load the value. Put your main point in the first 10 seconds. Viewers decide whether to keep watching in that window.
  • Break it into scenes. Each scene is one "shot." A 3-minute video typically needs 3-5 scenes (1 idea per scene).

Script length guide:

Video Length Word Count Avatar V Credits Used

1 minute 130-150 words ~20 credits
3 minutes 400-450 words ~60 credits
5 minutes 650-750 words ~100 credits

Credit usage is approximate. Avatar V uses ~20 credits/min. Creator plan includes 200 credits/mo.

Pro tip: HeyGen has a built-in AI script assistant. If you're stuck, click the AI icon in the script editor, describe what your video is about, and it generates a draft script. Edit it to match your voice, then move to the next step.

Step 4: Select Voice and Language (1-2 Minutes)

Click the voice selector in the script panel. HeyGen gives you three voice options:

  1. Stock voices (300+ in 40+ languages): Browse by language, gender, and style. Preview each voice by clicking the play button. Best for quick projects and stock avatar videos.
  2. Voice cloning (Creator plan+): Upload a 30-second audio sample of your voice, and HeyGen creates a synthetic clone. Your avatar speaks in your voice, in any language. This is the most convincing option for personal brand content.
  3. Existing audio upload: Upload your own audio recording, and HeyGen lip-syncs the avatar to it.

For multilingual content: Select the target language in the voice picker. HeyGen handles lip-sync automatically — the avatar's mouth movements match the new language. This works across all 175+ supported languages. For a full walkthrough of HeyGen's translation feature, see our HeyGen review.

Common mistake: Skipping the voice preview. Every voice sounds slightly different in tone, pacing, and emphasis. Listen to 3-4 options before committing. A mismatched voice undermines an otherwise good video.

Step 5: Customize Your Scene (2-3 Minutes)

The scene editor lets you control everything around and behind your avatar.

Background Options

  • Solid colors: Clean, professional. White or light gray works for most business videos.
  • Preset backgrounds: Office, classroom, studio, outdoor — HeyGen includes dozens of options.
  • Custom upload: Upload your own image (JPG or PNG). Use a company-branded background or a specific setting.
  • AI-generated backgrounds: Describe a setting and HeyGen creates it.

Adding Visual Elements

Step 6: Preview and Adjust (1-2 Minutes)

Before rendering, always preview your video. Click the "Preview" button in the top-right corner of the editor.

What to Check

  • Pacing: Does the avatar speak too fast or too slow? Adjust by shortening the script or adding pauses (use commas and periods to create natural breaks).
  • Pronunciation: The AI occasionally mispronounces proper nouns, technical terms, or abbreviations. Use SSML tags to correct tricky words — HeyGen's editor supports phonetic overrides.
  • Scene transitions: If you have multiple scenes, check that transitions feel smooth. Each scene cut should match a logical content break.
  • Visual alignment: Make sure text overlays, logos, and images don't overlap the avatar or sit in awkward positions.

Why this step matters: Every render consumes credits, whether you use the output or not. A 3-minute render at 20 credits/min costs 60 credits. On the Creator plan (200 credits/mo), one bad render wastes 30% of your monthly allowance. Preview first, render once.

Step 7: Render, Download, and Publish (2-5 Minutes)

  1. Click "Submit" in the top-right corner to start rendering.
  2. Rendering takes 2-5 minutes for a 3-minute video on the Creator plan. Business plan gets priority rendering (roughly 50% faster).
  3. You'll get a notification when the video is ready. Go to "My Videos" to find it.
  4. Click "Download" to export as MP4. Resolution: 720p (free), 1080p (Creator), 4K (Business).
  5. Or use direct sharing to publish straight to YouTube, LinkedIn, or TikTok from HeyGen.

Export formats: MP4 is the default and works everywhere. If you need to edit further in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or Descript, download the MP4 and import it into your editor.

After your first video: Watch it back critically. Is the avatar believable? Is the pacing right? Does the voice match the content? Your first video is a calibration run. Your second and third will be noticeably better because you'll know exactly which settings to adjust.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Rendering without previewing. Every render costs credits. A wasted render on the Creator plan ($24/mo, 200 credits) means you just threw away $2.40+ per minute of output. Always preview first.
  2. Writing scripts like blog posts. Avatar video scripts need short sentences, pauses, and a conversational tone. Long, complex sentences sound robotic when spoken by an AI voice.
  3. Ignoring pronunciation quirks. Proper nouns, acronyms, and technical terms trip up AI voices. Test these in preview and use SSML phonetic tags for corrections.
  4. Using Avatar V for videos that don't need it. Avatar V costs 20 credits/min. Standard avatars cost ~1 credit/min. For internal training or quick demos where your face isn't the selling point, stock avatars stretch your credits 20x further.
  5. Forgetting the call to action. End your video script with a clear next step — "click the link below," "book a demo," "subscribe." AI avatar videos convert best when they direct the viewer somewhere specific.
