What Is PixVerse?

PixVerse is an AI video generation platform built for short-form content. Text-to-video. Image-to-video. Video-to-video. Multiple visual styles — realistic, anime, clay, 3D. The focus is clips under 15 seconds with cinematic camera behavior, not long-form video production.

The V6 model, launched March 30, 2026, is the current flagship. It introduced 20+ cinematic lens controls, native audio generation, and multi-shot character consistency — three features that meaningfully separate PixVerse from the field of generalist AI video tools. Earlier models (V5.6, V5.5, V5, V4.5) are still available for users who prefer their output characteristics. A separate C1 model for film-style production launched in April 2026, and the R1 model handles real-time interactive generation.

On the Artificial Analysis Video Arena, PixVerse V6 ranks around #4 in image-to-video with an Elo score of approximately 1,323–1,343 as of September 2026. That puts it behind HappyHorse 1.0, Seedance 2.0, and Kling 3.0, but ahead of Runway Gen-4.5 and Pika 2.5 in image-to-video quality. The benchmarks confirm what you see in practice: PixVerse produces strong short clips with good motion coherence and visual fidelity.

PixVerse sits in a different lane than avatar-based tools like HeyGen or Synthesia. It generates entirely new video from prompts or reference images — no talking heads, no avatars, no templates. Think of it as a visual effects tool for social creators rather than a corporate video maker. If you want to generate a 10-second clip of a samurai walking through cherry blossoms with a dolly zoom and synchronized ambient audio, PixVerse is where you go.

PixVerse C1: Storyboard-to-Video and Cinematic Production

PixVerse C1 launched in April 2026 alongside V6 and targets a different audience entirely. Where V6 handles general-purpose social video, C1 is built for cinematic animation, complex VFX, and multi-shot consistency for short-form film production.

Storyboard-to-Video (Unique to C1)

This is C1’s standout feature — and nothing else on the market does it at this price. You upload a grid image of 3–9 comic panels or storyboard frames, and C1 generates a continuous multi-shot video from them. No text prompt needed. The workflow compresses what used to require per-shot generation and manual stitching into a single upload. For anime, webtoon, and short drama creators who already work with illustrated storyboards, C1 turns existing assets directly into video. The output covers comic-to-video, manga adaptation, and episodic AI film workflows.

Physically Believable VFX

C1 targets effects that generative models typically fail at: fire that casts light, lightning that illuminates surroundings, particles that follow physics rather than drifting randomly. According to PixVerse’s own testing documentation, C1 consistently outperformed V6 on physically coherent action sequences. Not independently verified by AIVP.

Specs and Pricing

C1 supports text-to-video, image-to-video, transition generation (first and last frames), and reference-to-video fusion. Maximum output is 15 seconds at 1080p with optional audio. Credits are billed per second — check the current PixVerse model pricing docs before planning a large batch, as 1080p with audio becomes expensive quickly. Start at 540p or 720p while refining your prompts. Lip sync and sound effects may add separate credit costs.

Who C1 Is For

Anime and webtoon creators. Short drama and AI film producers. Anyone running a multi-shot narrative who needs consistent characters across scenes. If you work with illustrated storyboards already, C1’s panel-to-video flow saves hours per episode.

PixVerse Earn and Affiliate Program: What the $5,500 Day Actually Means

PixVerse runs two separate monetisation programs for creators. Here is what each one actually offers.

PixVerse Affiliate Program

The affiliate program pays 30% recurring commission for 12 months on every subscription renewal from creators you refer. The 90-day cookie window means content you publish today can still convert three months later. Once your referred revenue exceeds $5,000/month, you automatically upgrade to the Super Affiliate tier at 35% — no application required. PixVerse also advertises a two-tier sub-affiliate bonus, but its current page contains conflicting figures (5% in the FAQ and 20% elsewhere), so verify the live dashboard terms before relying on that component.

PixVerse’s own blog documents a creator earning $5,500 in a single day — vendor-documented; AIVP has not independently verified the creator’s dashboard or payout. PixVerse says the creator published useful tutorial and comparison content consistently, crossed the $5,000/month referred-revenue threshold (triggering the 35% Super Affiliate rate), and then had one video go viral. At 35% on the $30/mo Pro plan, each qualifying payment produces $10.50 commission, so $5,500 is equivalent to about 524 Pro payments landing that day from some mix of new buyers and renewals. That is a peak result, not a starting point or typical-income claim.

Illustrative commission scenarios (not income projections):

  • 10 active Pro payments at the 30% rate: $90 commission per billing cycle
  • 100 active Pro payments at the 30% rate: $900 commission per billing cycle
  • 500 active Pro payments at the 35% rate: $5,250 commission per billing cycle

The 90-day cookie and 12-month commission window let useful content keep converting after publication. A referral can keep paying on qualifying renewals for up to a year if the subscriber remains active. Apply to the PixVerse affiliate program →

PixVerse Earn

Earn is a separate creator campaign program — post PixVerse-made videos to Instagram, YouTube, or X with #pixverse, join a campaign from the dashboard at earn.pixverse.ai, and receive cash or credits for approved submissions. PixVerse currently advertises $50K+ campaign pools, $100K+ in prize pools, and a 30-day payout timeline via Tremendous. It is free to join and lists no fixed follower minimum, but account status, engagement, content compliance, and each campaign’s rules are reviewed.

Important caveats: Each campaign has specific eligibility requirements; some are invitation-only. Displayed balances are subject to verification, fraud checks, and manual review corrections before withdrawal. This is not reliable passive income — treat it as upside on content you would make anyway, not a primary income source.

How much does PixVerse cost per month in 2026?

Is PixVerse better than Kling for AI video?

They excel at different things. PixVerse V6 leads on cinematic camera controls (20+ lens types), native audio generation, and multi-shot character consistency at a lower starting price ($10/month vs Kling's $5.99/month). Kling 3.0 produces longer clips (up to 3 minutes vs PixVerse's 15 seconds), higher resolution (4K on lower tiers), and has a more generous free tier (66 credits/month). For short-form social content under 15 seconds with character-locked storytelling, PixVerse is the stronger choice. For longer clips and maximum resolution, Kling wins.

What is PixVerse V6?

PixVerse V6 launched March 30, 2026 and is the latest generation model. It introduced 20+ cinematic lens controls (wide-angle, telephoto, tilt-shift, fisheye, macro, dolly zoom, rack focus, crane shots), native audio generation synced to video content, 15-second 1080p multi-shot clips with character consistency, and improved motion coherence. V6 uses a credit-per-second pricing model: 540p costs 7 credits/second without audio or 9 credits/second with audio; 1080p costs 18 credits/second without audio or 23 credits/second with audio.

What styles does PixVerse support?

PixVerse supports four visual styles: Realistic (photorealistic output), Anime (Japanese animation style), Clay (claymation/stop-motion look), and 3D (rendered 3D animation). You can also use text-to-video, image-to-video, and video-to-video workflows. The V6 model added cinematic lens controls that work across all styles, letting you combine a style preset with a specific camera behavior like dolly zoom or rack focus.

Can PixVerse generate audio with video?

Yes, PixVerse V6 introduced native audio generation that creates synchronized sound effects and ambient audio matched to the video content. This is not AI voiceover or text-to-speech — it generates environmental audio (footsteps, wind, explosions, music) that matches what is happening visually. Adding audio costs extra credits: 9 credits/second at 540p vs 7 without audio, and 23 credits/second at 1080p vs 18 without. Audio generation is available on all paid plans.
