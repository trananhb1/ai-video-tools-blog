What Is Quso AI (Formerly Vidyo.ai)?

Quso AI is an all-in-one video repurposing and social media management platform that turns long-form videos into short clips, adds captions, and lets you schedule posts directly to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Pinterest. As of May 2026, it has over 4 million users.

If the name sounds unfamiliar but the concept rings a bell, you may know it by its previous name. Vidyo.ai rebranded to Quso AI in December 2024. The rebrand was not cosmetic — it marked a strategic expansion from a video clipping tool into a full social media AI suite. The core clip detection engine from Vidyo.ai carried over, but Quso added social scheduling, AI avatars, an AI content writer, analytics dashboards, and brand kit management that the original product never had. If you had a Vidyo.ai account, your login, subscription, and projects transferred automatically. Still see Vidyo.ai branding in old bookmarks? It is the same product — our Vidyo.ai link lands on the new Quso dashboard.

The platform sits in a crowded category alongside Opus Clip, Submagic, and Descript. What separates Quso is its ambition to cover the entire creator workflow: clip → caption → schedule → analyze, all from one dashboard. Whether it actually delivers on that promise is what we tested.

Who Is Quso AI For?

Quso AI targets solo creators and small teams who repurpose long-form content into short-form social video. The typical user publishes across multiple platforms and does not want to pay for separate clipping, captioning, and scheduling tools. Common users include:

  • YouTubers repurposing long videos into Shorts, Reels, and TikToks without switching between three different apps
  • Podcasters who need to clip interview highlights and publish them as vertical video across social platforms
  • Solo content creators managing their own social media presence and looking to save time on editing and scheduling
  • Course creators and coaches who want to turn webinar recordings and training sessions into bite-sized social content
  • Social media managers at small businesses who handle content creation, scheduling, and analytics for one brand

If you run a multi-brand agency or need advanced team collaboration with approval workflows, Quso is not built for that scale. It is a solo creator and small-team tool — and that is exactly who it serves well.

Key Features: What Quso AI Does Well

We tested Quso AI across its core feature set. Here is what matters and how each feature actually works in practice.

AI Clip Detection

Upload a long video or paste a YouTube URL, and Quso's AI analyzes the transcript and audio to identify the most engaging segments. It generates multiple short clips (typically 30-90 seconds) automatically, each reframed to 9:16 vertical format. The clip detection is transcript-driven, so clear speech with minimal background noise produces the best results.

AI Captions & Subtitles

Auto-generated captions with up to 97% accuracy according to Quso. Multiple style templates let you customize font, color, size, and animation. Captions burn directly into the video export. Multi-language caption support is available, though the primary strength is English and major European languages.

Social Media Scheduling

Schedule and publish directly to 7 platforms: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Pinterest. Includes a content calendar view, bulk publishing on the Growth plan, and basic analytics. This is the feature that separates Quso from pure clipping tools like Opus Clip.

AI Avatars (80+)

Create videos with over 80 diverse AI avatars without needing a camera or crew. Useful for creating talking-head content when you do not want to appear on screen yourself. The avatar quality is functional for social content, though it trails dedicated avatar platforms like HeyGen and Synthesia in lip-sync accuracy.

AI Filler & Silence Removal

Automatically detects and removes filler words ("um," "uh," "you know") and awkward silences from clips. Available on Essential plan and above. This gives clips a polished, studio-grade feel without manual editing. The detection accuracy is solid for English content.

Video Resizing (Multi-Format)

Export clips in 16:9 (landscape), 9:16 (vertical), and 1:1 (square) formats from a single source video. Smart reframing keeps the speaker centered in vertical crops. This saves significant time compared to manually cropping each clip for different platforms.

AI Content Writer

Generates social captions, blog posts, show notes, and hashtags from your video content. Writes in your brand voice once you configure the brand kit. Available on Essential plan and above. The writing quality is serviceable for social captions, though you will want to edit anything longer than a caption.

CutMagic Scene Detection

AI-powered scene detection that identifies visual transitions and topic shifts in your video. Works alongside the transcript analysis to produce more contextually coherent clips. The B-roll library lets you add supplementary footage to cover visual gaps in talking-head content.

Brand Kit and Templates

Upload your logo, brand colors, and fonts to maintain consistent branding across all clips and social posts. Custom templates let you save your preferred caption styles, intro/outro sequences, and visual layouts for reuse. This is a time-saver for creators who publish frequently and want every clip to look on-brand without restyling each one manually. Brand kit is available on Essential and Growth plans.

Hands-On Testing: How Quso AI Performs

We tested Quso AI with three different source video types to see how the platform performs across real workflows.

Test 1: YouTube Video Repurposing

We pasted a 25-minute YouTube interview URL into Quso. The platform imported the video and generated 8 clip suggestions in about 3 minutes. Each clip was auto-reframed to 9:16 vertical with captions applied. Of the 8 clips, 5 captured genuinely engaging moments from the interview. The other 3 picked up tangential segments that would not perform well on their own. We edited two clips — trimming endpoints and swapping caption styles — in about 4 minutes total. From URL paste to export-ready clips: roughly 10 minutes for 5 usable shorts.

Test 2: Podcast Episode Clipping

We uploaded a 45-minute podcast MP4 (audio-only with a static image). Quso's clip detection relied entirely on transcript analysis here, and it performed well. It identified 12 potential clips, with the best 6 capturing standalone story beats that worked as social clips. The filler word removal cleaned up "ums" and dead air automatically. We paired two of the clips with Quso's AI avatar feature to add a visual presenter, which took about 2 minutes per clip. Total workflow for 6 clips: about 20 minutes.

Test 3: Social Scheduling Workflow

After generating clips, we tested the scheduling pipeline. Connecting TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram took about 5 minutes of OAuth setup. Scheduling a week of content (14 posts across 3 platforms) took another 10 minutes using the content calendar. The bulk publishing feature on the Growth plan would streamline this further, but we tested on the Essential plan where you schedule posts individually. The experience was straightforward — comparable to dedicated scheduling tools like Buffer, though with fewer analytics features.

Known Issues and Limitations

Quso AI is ambitious, but the all-in-one approach introduces trade-offs. Here is what to watch for.

Credit System Limits Heavy Users

Every action in Quso costs credits: generating a clip uses 5-15 credits, adding captions costs 3-5 credits, and resizing uses 2-5 credits. A single clip with captions and resizing can consume 10-25 credits. The free plan's 75 credits run out fast — you might get 3-7 fully processed clips before hitting the limit. Even the Lite plan's 100 monthly credits feel restrictive if you are repurposing content daily. The Growth plan (600-1,800 credits) is where heavy users need to land, and that costs $40/month.

Scheduling Locked to Higher Plans

Social media scheduling — arguably Quso's biggest differentiator versus Opus Clip — is not available on the free or Lite plans. You need the Essential plan at $32/month to schedule to 7 platforms, or the Growth plan at $40/month for unlimited scheduling and bulk publishing. If scheduling is why you are considering Quso, the entry price is effectively $32/month, not the $24/month Lite price shown prominently on the pricing page.

No Advanced Social Analytics

The analytics dashboard tracks basic metrics (post reach, engagement, follower growth), but it lacks the depth of dedicated social analytics tools. No competitor benchmarking, no hashtag performance analysis, no optimal posting time suggestions based on audience behavior. If analytics are a priority, you will still need a separate tool.

AI Avatar Quality Trails Specialists

The 80+ AI avatars are a nice addition, but they are not at the quality level of dedicated avatar platforms. Lip-sync accuracy, facial expressiveness, and voice naturalness all trail what you get from HeyGen ($24/month) or Synthesia ($18/month annual). The avatars are functional for quick social content where production quality is secondary to speed, but they are not suitable for brand videos, training content, or customer-facing material where avatar realism matters.

Limited Team Collaboration

No approval workflows, no role-based permissions, no shared content libraries. If you work with a team that reviews content before publishing, you will need to manage that process outside of Quso. This is a single-user or small-team tool, not an agency platform.

Best Use Cases for Quso AI

If you publish a weekly YouTube video and want to extract 5-10 shorts per episode for cross-platform distribution, Quso handles the entire pipeline. 2. Podcast Repurposing

Upload a podcast episode, let Quso detect the most quotable moments, add visual captions, and pair with an AI avatar if you want a talking-head presenter. 3. Social Media Content Calendar

For solo creators managing TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn, Quso's scheduling feature eliminates the need for a separate tool like Buffer or Later. Batch-produce clips on a weekend, schedule the entire week, and let the platform auto-publish. The content calendar view makes it easy to visualize your posting cadence across platforms.

4. Course and Webinar Clips

Record a 60-minute webinar or training session and use Quso to extract the key teaching moments as standalone clips. The clips work well for social proof, lead generation, and promoting future events. The AI avatar feature lets you create supplementary explainer clips without additional recording.

5. Camera-Free Content with AI Avatars

If you want to create talking-head content but do not want to appear on camera, Quso's 80+ AI avatars offer a functional alternative. Write a script, select an avatar, and generate a social-ready video in minutes. The avatar quality is suitable for social content — though for client-facing or training videos, dedicated avatar tools like HeyGen deliver noticeably better results.

Who Should NOT Use Quso AI

Quso is not the right tool for every video workflow. Here are the situations where you should look elsewhere:

  • You need the best possible clip quality. If clip detection accuracy and virality prediction are your top priorities, Opus Clip is the better pure clipper with its AI Virality Score and XML export for professional editors.
  • You need studio-grade animated captions. Quso's caption styles are functional but limited. Submagic offers 35+ animated caption styles with word-level highlighting that outperforms Quso on visual impact.
  • You need advanced video editing. Quso is a repurposing tool, not a full video editor. For text-based editing, voice cloning, and granular timeline control, Descript is the better choice.
  • You run a multi-brand agency. No approval workflows, no team permissions, no shared content libraries. Agency teams should look at dedicated social management platforms.
  • You need high-quality AI avatars. Quso's avatars are a secondary feature, not a core strength. For training videos, brand content, or anything where avatar realism matters, use HeyGen or Synthesia.
  • You produce primarily text-to-video content. Quso is built for video-to-video repurposing. If your workflow starts with text, blog posts, or scripts, Fliki or Pictory are better fits.
