What happened to Sora?

On March 24, 2026, OpenAI announced a two-phase shutdown of Sora. The consumer app closed April 26; the API dies September 24. Three forces killed it:

  • Unsustainable compute costs. Sora was reportedly burning roughly $1 million per day in GPU costs. OpenAI redirected that capacity to coding agents and enterprise products ahead of its planned IPO.
  • Declining users. Active users dropped from 1 million at peak to under 500,000. Lifetime in-app revenue was an estimated $2.1 million — far below infrastructure costs.
  • Copyright headwinds. Ongoing litigation over training data created legal risk OpenAI chose not to carry.

The practical impact: every creator, marketer, and developer who relied on Sora now needs a replacement. 7. Seedance 2.0 — Best motion realism (with caveats)

Seedance 2.0 by ByteDance generates cinema-quality clips with built-in sound, dynamic camera movement, and consistent characters using a unified multimodal audio-video joint generation architecture. You can combine up to 9 images, 3 video clips, 3 audio clips, and text prompts in a single generation — the most flexible multimodal input of any tool on this list.

For motion — especially dance, fitness, and complex human body movement — Seedance 2.0 is the strongest model available. Motion transfer from a 15-second reference clip lets you apply camera movements and character momentum to entirely new scenes. The international platform Dreamina provides English access.

Copyright warning: Seedance 2.0 is currently restricted in the United States due to copyright disputes with Hollywood studios. The Motion Picture Association, Disney, Warner Bros., Paramount, and Netflix sent legal letters to ByteDance after the model was used to generate videos featuring copyrighted characters like Spider-Man and Darth Vader. ByteDance paused the global rollout on March 16, 2026. As of April 2026, the model is available in 100+ countries but not the US.
  • Pricing: Free tier (100 daily credits via Dreamina, no watermarks), Pro from ~$20/mo for priority queue and extra credits. China pricing: ~$9.60/mo via Jimeng.
  • Best for: Dance, fitness, and motion-heavy content. Users outside the US who want cinema-quality clips with native audio.
  • Biggest pro: Best motion realism for complex human movement. Multimodal input (images + video + audio + text). Free tier with no watermarks.
  • Biggest con: Restricted in the US. Copyright controversy. 15-second max per shot. English documentation limited.
