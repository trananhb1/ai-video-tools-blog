  • Video Agent — give it a single prompt and get a complete, publish-ready video up to 3 minutes long. Script, scene structure, voiceover, and your Digital Twin in cinematic Seedance footage are all handled automatically.
  • AI Video Generator — direct access to Seedance 2.0 for text-to-video and image-to-video B-roll generation. Upload a product photo or location reference and Seedance builds cinematic footage around it, with first-frame and last-frame control for precise scene transitions.

Avatar V is HeyGen's fifth-generation model, launched April 8, 2026. It generates a photorealistic digital twin from just 15 seconds of webcam footage — no studio, lighting, or green screen required. Key capabilities:

  • Learned gestures — patterns captured from your real video, not generic animation overlays.
  • 0.840 face similarity score — the highest published benchmark in avatar AI as of May 2026.

Instant Highlights v2

Instant Highlights v2 turns long-form video — podcasts, webinars, keynotes, interviews, live streams — into publish-ready short clips automatically. The v2 rebuild adds several capabilities that were missing or limited in v1:

  • Prompt-based search — type "the part about fundraising mistakes" and the AI finds it in a 3-hour video. Your long-form library becomes searchable.
  • Dynamic camera follow — the virtual camera tracks speakers as they move across a stage or gesture off-centre, creating the look of a professional camera operator rather than a static centre-crop.
  • Multi-speaker handling — stacks multiple speakers in a single frame or automatically cuts between them with context-aware transitions.
  • Integrated post-production — add captions, translate and lip-sync into 175+ languages, and upscale to 4K without leaving HeyGen. No re-uploads or third-party tools required.

This positions HeyGen as a direct competitor to Opus Clip for video repurposing, with the added advantage of built-in translation and avatar features. If you already pay for HeyGen, Instant Highlights v2 may replace your short-form clip tool entirely.

Understanding HeyGen's Credit System

This is the area where HeyGen draws the most criticism, and it is worth explaining clearly. HeyGen uses a credit-based system where 1 credit equals approximately 1 minute of video. The Creator plan includes roughly 15 credits per month. That means if you produce a single 10-minute video, you have used most of your monthly allowance. For teams producing multiple videos per week, the Business plan or additional credit purchases are necessary.

The credit system can feel restrictive compared to platforms that advertise "unlimited" video creation. Multiple users on review platforms report confusion about exactly how many credits they receive and how quickly those credits are consumed by different features like translation or Interactive Avatars. Our advice: before committing to an annual plan, use the free tier to understand your actual credit consumption per video. The product earns our recommendation; the billing and support experience warrants caution.

How to Use HeyGen to Create AI Videos

You can ship a professional AI avatar video in 8 steps and under 15 minutes, from script to final export. Here is the condensed workflow on the $24/month Creator plan (free tier: 3 videos x 3 min). For the full beginner walkthrough with screenshots, credit math, and common mistakes, see our step-by-step HeyGen Avatar V tutorial.

  1. Sign up free at heygen.com — no credit card required for the free tier.
  2. Choose an avatar — pick from 300+ stock avatars or create a custom Avatar V twin from 15 seconds of webcam footage.
  3. Write or paste your script — use the built-in script editor. Keep it under 500 words for a 3-minute video. Tip: write for spoken delivery (short sentences, conversational tone).
  4. Select voice and language — choose from 300+ voices in 40+ languages, or clone your own voice (Creator plan+). For multilingual, select the target language and HeyGen handles lip-sync automatically.
  5. Customize the scene — pick a background (upload or AI-generated), add text overlays, logos, and screen recordings. Use Seedance 2.0 for cinematic scenes with your Digital Twin.
  6. Preview and adjust — play through the preview. Check pacing, pronunciation (use SSML tags for tricky words), and scene transitions.
  7. Generate the video — hit Submit. Rendering takes 2-5 minutes for a 3-minute video on Creator plan (priority rendering on Business+).
  8. Download and publish — export as MP4 (1080p on Creator, 4K on Business). Direct sharing to YouTube, LinkedIn, and TikTok available.

Pro Tips

  • Batch your scripts — write 5-10 scripts in one session, then generate back-to-back. Most efficient use of credits.
  • Use the API for scale — if you need 50+ personalized videos (sales outreach, onboarding), the API automates everything from script injection to delivery.
  • Test with free tier first — make 3 test videos before committing to Creator. You will know within 15 minutes if HeyGen fits your workflow.
  • Avoid credit waste — preview before rendering. Each render consumes credits regardless of whether you use the output.
  • Combine with Instant Highlights v2 — record a 30-minute webinar, then use Instant Highlights to auto-cut it into 10+ short clips. One recording, many outputs.
