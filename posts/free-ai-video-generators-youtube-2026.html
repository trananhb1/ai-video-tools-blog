What YouTube Actually Requires from AI Video

Before picking a tool, you need to know what YouTube enforces. As of May 2026, these are the rules that matter for AI-generated content:

  • AI disclosure label: You must toggle "Altered or synthetic content" in YouTube Studio when your video contains realistic-looking AI-generated people, places, or events a viewer could mistake for real. Obviously stylized AI content (animation, abstract visuals) does not require the label.
  • Monetization: AI-generated videos are eligible for YPP monetization as long as you add genuine human creative direction — scripting, editing, narration, or commentary. Purely auto-generated content with zero human input risks demonetization.
  • Resolution floor: YouTube has no hard resolution minimum, but videos below 720p get lower watch time and algorithmic reach. Practical floor for a monetized channel: 1080p at 24–30fps.
  • Watermarks: A tool watermark (e.g., "Made with Veo") is not a YPP violation, but it looks unprofessional and signals low production value to viewers. Avoid it on monetized content.
  • Commercial-use license: Most free tiers do NOT grant commercial-use rights. If you earn ad revenue from YouTube, you are using the output commercially. Check each tool’s terms.
  • Music and voice: AI-generated music or voice clones can trigger Content ID strikes if they resemble copyrighted audio. Use original AI voices and royalty-free AI music.

FAQ: YouTube AI Video Rules

Can I monetize AI-generated videos on YouTube in 2026?

Yes. YouTube allows monetization of AI-generated videos as of May 2026, but your content must show genuine human creative direction — scripting, editing, narration, or commentary. Purely auto-generated content with no human input risks demonetization. You must toggle the "Altered or synthetic content" label in YouTube Studio when your video contains realistic-looking AI-generated people, places, or events. You also need a commercial-use license from the AI tool — most free tiers do NOT grant this.

Which free AI video generators have no watermark for YouTube?

As of May 2026, CapCut exports at 1080p with no watermark on desktop (watermark on mobile free). Canva Free exports at 1080p without watermark but caps AI video generation at roughly 5 lifetime uses. Seedance 2.0 via MyShell exports without watermark from most access points. Google Veo 3.1 adds a "Made with Veo" watermark on Free and Pro tiers. Kling AI, InVideo, Fliki, and Vidnoz all add watermarks on their free plans.

Do I need to disclose AI-generated content on YouTube?

Yes, for realistic synthetic content. YouTube requires creators to toggle the "Altered or synthetic content" label in YouTube Studio when uploading videos that contain realistic-looking AI-generated or altered people, places, or events that a viewer could mistake for real. Stylized or obviously artificial content does not require the label. Failing to disclose can result in content removal, demonetization, or channel strikes.

What resolution does YouTube need for monetization?

YouTube does not enforce a strict resolution floor for monetization. However, videos below 720p look poor on modern screens and receive lower average view duration, which hurts algorithmic reach. For YPP eligibility, 1080p at 24–30fps is the practical floor. Most free AI video tools export at 720p — only CapCut and Canva Free reliably export at 1080p without watermark. If your tool caps at 720p, upscale to 1080p before uploading.

Will YouTube detect AI-generated videos and ban my channel?

YouTube does not ban channels for using AI-generated content. It bans channels for violating its policies — mass-produced spam, misleading deepfakes, or content with no human creative input. As long as you add genuine creative value (scripting, editing, commentary, narration) and disclose synthetic content using the YouTube Studio label, AI-generated videos are treated the same as any other content.

Can I use free AI video tools for faceless YouTube channels?

Yes. Faceless YouTube channels are one of the strongest use cases for free AI video tools. Use CapCut (free, no watermark) to assemble AI-generated clips, stock footage, and text overlays with auto-captions. Use Google Veo 3.1 (50 free daily credits) to generate B-roll clips. Add AI voiceover with ElevenLabs (10,000 free characters/month). The key for monetization: your editing, script, and narration must show genuine human creative direction.
