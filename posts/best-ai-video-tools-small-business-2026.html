What small businesses actually need from AI video

Most small businesses don't need cinematic AI video. They need a fast, consistent way to produce promos, explainers, product demos, social clips, and follow-up content without booking a videographer or spending a half-day on editing.

The tools in this guide are selected on three criteria:

  • Price: All plans under $50/month (most under $30)
  • Output quality: Usable in real marketing — no obvious template artifacts, decent voiceover, watchable pacing
  • Learning curve: A non-technical team member can produce content within an hour of signing up

Free tiers are noted where they exist, but this guide focuses on paid plans — free tiers nearly always watermark or credit-gate too heavily for business use.
