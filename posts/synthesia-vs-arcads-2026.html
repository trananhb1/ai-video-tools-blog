Synthesia vs Arcads: What They Actually Do

If you landed here trying to pick between Synthesia and Arcads, the most useful thing we can tell you up front is this: they barely compete. Both generate video from a script with an AI-driven on-screen person, and that surface similarity is where the resemblance ends. The two products solve completely different jobs.

Synthesia is an enterprise AI avatar platform built for corporate training, onboarding, compliance, and internal communications. You write a script, pick an AI avatar from 230+ stock presenters (or upload your own), choose a voice and language, and Synthesia generates a long-form video of that avatar delivering your script — with accurate lip-sync, professional posture, and consistent delivery across 10-30 minute runs. Over 50,000 companies use it including Xerox, BBC, Nike, Heineken, and Amazon. Full Synthesia review.

Arcads is a focused AI UGC ad creative tool built for performance marketers running paid social. You feed it a product brief or a script, pick from 1,000+ AI actors styled to look like real TikTok and Meta UGC creators, and Arcads generates short (15-60 second) ad-style videos that read as user-generated content rather than brand video. The actors have the natural eye contact, hand gestures, micro-expressions, verbal fillers, and product-handling spontaneity that drive direct-response conversion. Arcads vs Creatify vs MakeUGC comparison.

The 60-second take: If the video's job is to teach or inform — train a new hire, explain a policy, narrate a course module — choose Synthesia. If the video's job is to sell on a paid social feed — first 3 seconds of a Meta or TikTok ad — choose Arcads. The aesthetics, format lengths, pricing models, and feature priorities all flow from that one difference.

The Use Case Divide: Training vs Ads

Most "X vs Y" AI video comparisons argue over the same job. This one is different. Synthesia and Arcads target jobs that have almost no overlap, and trying to use one for the other ends badly.

Synthesia's Job: Internal Video at Scale

Synthesia's product, pricing, and feature set are all optimised for the L&D buyer. The 30-minute video length cap, 140+ language coverage, SCORM export, SAML SSO, quizzes, branching scenarios, PowerPoint-to-video import, and Studio Avatars (broadcast-quality custom presenters) only matter to teams producing training, onboarding, compliance, internal announcements, or customer education content.

The aesthetic is intentionally polished. The avatars stand upright, keep their hands visible, deliver lines with even pacing, and look like trained corporate spokespeople — because that is what the L&D buyer wants. A new hire watching a 12-minute compliance module needs a presenter who reads as credible authority, not someone who looks like they're filming on a phone.

That same polish becomes a liability the moment you try to use Synthesia for a paid social ad. HeyGen vs Synthesia covers the avatar-realism head-to-head in detail, but neither tool is the right choice for performance ad creative.

Arcads' Job: Ad Creative at Scale

Arcads is built for the performance marketer who needs 10-50 fresh ad variants a week to stay ahead of creative fatigue on Meta and TikTok. Everything in the product reflects that:

  • Actors styled like real UGC creators — handheld framing, kitchen and bedroom backdrops, natural lighting, casual outfits
  • Performance cues built in — eye contact with camera, hand gestures, breathing, occasional verbal fillers ("like", "honestly", "omg")
  • Batch generation — spin up 10 versions of the same script across 10 different actors in a single render queue
  • Ad-copy generation — hook + body + CTA structure, pain-point templates, proof-point angles tuned for direct response
  • Short-form output — 15-60 second videos sized for 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) and 1:1 / 4:5 (Meta feed)

Run the same script through Synthesia and Arcads side by side and the difference is immediately obvious. The Synthesia version reads as "professional training video that happens to mention a product." The Arcads version reads as "real person showing me the product." For paid social, that distinction is worth 20-50% in CTR.

The hard rule: Synthesia for content where the viewer expects authority. Arcads for content where the viewer expects authenticity. Almost never both.

Avatars vs AI Actors (The Aesthetic Difference)

Synthesia: Corporate Realism, Long-Form Stability

Synthesia's Express-2 engine drives 230+ stock avatars with reliable lip-sync across long-form video (10-30 minutes), consistent posture, and the kind of measured hand gestures that work for corporate training. The avatars hold their tone across a full learning module without the jittery micro-expressions that can creep into other tools on longer runs.

Custom avatars come in two tiers: Personal Avatars (record yourself on a webcam, generated in minutes — good enough for sales outreach and exec announcements) and Studio Avatars (in-studio professional recording, broadcast quality, around $1,000/year extra). Both are designed to scale a single human presenter across hundreds of videos a year.

Where Synthesia falls short on the aesthetic axis: the avatars are too polished for short-form social. If you used a Synthesia avatar in a Meta UGC ad, viewers would clock it as corporate within 2 seconds. For social-ad aesthetics, see HeyGen's Avatar V or move to a UGC-specific tool like Arcads.

Arcads: UGC Realism, Performance-Marketing Tuned

Arcads' actor library is the opposite end of the realism spectrum. The 1,000+ AI actors are licensed from real people who consented to having their likeness used for AI-generated ads, then tuned for the visual language of UGC: handheld framing, casual eye contact, natural hand and product-holding gestures, breathing, blink rates, micro-expressions, and verbal fillers that ad audiences read as a real creator review rather than a brand spokesperson.

The trade-off is that Arcads is much narrower in what it can do. There is no long-form mode — renders cap out around 60 seconds. There is no built-in stock footage library, no B-roll, no on-screen text editor at the depth of a Synthesia or Pictory. The product does one thing: spokesperson UGC ads. For that one thing, it is the realism leader in the category as of May 2026, narrowly ahead of Creatify on close-up lip-sync and well ahead of MakeUGC on actor naturalism.

Workflow & Editor

The editing experience tracks the use case.

Synthesia's editor is scene-based: each scene contains an avatar, a background, optional B-roll or screen recordings, and a script box. You write the script (or use the AI Copilot to generate it from a URL or document), pick the avatar and voice, choose a background, and Synthesia handles the render. The 2026 update added Video Agents (two-way real-time avatar conversations for support and training) and beefed-up PowerPoint-to-video conversion. The workflow is deliberate — built for the L&D producer who wants a finished, tightly controlled video.

Arcads' editor is built around the ad-creative loop: write or generate the script, pick one or many actors, generate, review, regenerate the losers, ship the winners. Three things stand out:

  • Batch script generation — describe the product and Arcads writes 5-10 hook/body/CTA variants you can route to different actors
  • Multi-actor variant generation — same script, 10 actors, one queue — ideal for testing which face converts
  • Direct export to ad-ready aspect ratios — 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 with subtitle burn-in

What Arcads does not have: no scene-by-scene B-roll editor, no quizzes, no branching, no Powerpoint import, no LMS export. It is intentionally narrow. If you want a deeper editor with more visual control, InVideo AI or Creatify may be closer fits.

Enterprise Features (SOC 2, SCORM, API)

Synthesia is built for enterprise procurement. Arcads is not, and does not pretend to be.

Synthesia ships SOC 2 Type II compliance, SAML SSO, SCORM export for LMS integration, role-based permissions, audit logs, dedicated account management on Enterprise, and a developer API from the Creator plan ($52/month). Interactive video features — quizzes, branching scenarios, CTA buttons embedded in the player — make it suitable for compliance training that LMS platforms can track. This is the moat that keeps Synthesia winning enterprise deals.

Arcads offers API access on its Pro tier and team seats for larger ad teams, but does not have SOC 2 certification, SAML SSO, SCORM export, or LMS integration. If your procurement process requires those, Arcads cannot pass — but procurement is also not its buyer. Performance marketing teams typically buy ad-creative tools on the marketing budget, not the IT budget, so the gap rarely matters in practice.

Procurement gate: If "we need SOC 2" or "we need SCORM" is in the buying criteria, Synthesia wins automatically — Arcads is not even in the consideration set. For L&D-specific comparisons that also include Colossyan, see HeyGen vs Colossyan vs Synthesia for training.

Final Verdict

The honest answer: Synthesia and Arcads are not really competitors. They are best-in-class tools for different jobs, and the "winner" is just whichever job you actually have.

Synthesia wins any video where authority, governance, and long-form delivery matter: corporate training, onboarding, compliance, executive announcements, sales outreach, customer education, multilingual rollouts. The combination of 230+ realistic avatars, SOC 2 + SCORM + SSO, interactive course features, AI Copilot, Video Agents, and the lowest entry price in the category ($18/month annual) makes it the most complete enterprise avatar platform in 2026.

Arcads wins any video where authenticity, batch volume, and direct-response performance matter: AI UGC ad creative for Meta, TikTok, and Shorts. The 1,000+ UGC-tuned actors, batch script-and-actor generation, ad-copy generator with hook/body/CTA structure, and ad-ready aspect ratio export make it the realism leader for paid social. At ~$11 per finished video, it replaces human UGC creators who would charge $150-$2,000 per clip.

Our practical recommendation: If you do both jobs (most marketing-led companies do), you will end up using both tools. Synthesia for the training, onboarding, internal comms, and any video where the audience expects a polished presenter. Arcads for the paid social ad creative where the audience needs to see a "real person" using the product. Trying to use one for the other almost always produces a worse outcome than picking the right tool for the job.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Synthesia or Arcads better in 2026?

Neither — they solve different problems. Synthesia wins for corporate training, onboarding, internal comms, and any L&D video where an on-screen avatar reads a script (from $18/month annual, 230+ avatars, SOC 2 + SCORM). Arcads wins for AI UGC ad creative — short, performative spokesperson videos meant to look like real users for paid social acquisition (from $110/month for 10 videos, 1,000+ AI actors). They barely overlap. Pick Synthesia if your goal is to teach or inform; pick Arcads if your goal is to sell on Meta or TikTok.

Which is cheaper, Synthesia or Arcads?

Synthesia is the cheaper monthly subscription by a wide margin — $18/month (Starter, annual billing) versus Arcads $110/month (Starter, 10 videos). But the comparison is misleading because they price for different jobs. Arcads charges per video (~$11/video on Starter and Creator), and a single converting UGC ad can pay back the entire monthly fee in one campaign. Synthesia caps minutes per year, while Arcads caps videos per month. If you only need 10 short ad videos a month, Arcads' per-video model can be cheaper than buying enough Synthesia minutes to match. There is no free tier on Arcads (7-day refund window); Synthesia gives you 3 minutes total on a permanent free plan.

Can Synthesia replace Arcads for UGC ads?

No, and you should not try. Synthesia avatars look like polished corporate presenters — professional posture, even delivery, neutral framing. That is the opposite of what works in AI UGC ads, where the unboxing-style spontaneity, raw emotion, and natural product handling drive the conversion. Arcads' actors are tuned specifically for the TikTok and Meta UGC aesthetic with breathing, eye contact, micro-expressions, and verbal fillers ("like", "honestly", "omg") that Synthesia's training-optimised avatars deliberately avoid. If you ran a Synthesia avatar as a UGC ad, the audience would clock it as corporate within 2 seconds and scroll past.

Can Arcads replace Synthesia for training videos?

No. Arcads has no support for long-form video (most outputs are 15-60 seconds), no SCORM export for LMS integration, no SOC 2 / SSO / governance for enterprise procurement, no quiz or interactivity features, no PowerPoint-to-video import, and no Studio Avatar tier for a branded corporate spokesperson. The actor library is also performance-marketing flavoured rather than corporate-presenter flavoured. For internal training that needs to be tracked in a learning management system, Arcads is not a fit. Synthesia (or Colossyan) is the right choice.

What does Arcads have that Synthesia does not?

Three things: (1) AI actors that look like real UGC creators rather than polished corporate presenters, with the natural awkwardness and product-handling gestures that perform on Meta and TikTok ads; (2) batch ad generation — spin up 10-50 script-and-actor variants in a single session for A/B testing; (3) script generation tuned for direct-response ad copy, including hook/body/CTA structure, pain-point angles, and proof-point templates. Synthesia is built for the opposite job: a single polished avatar delivering a script consistently across a long-form video. They are different products.

What does Synthesia have that Arcads does not?

A lot: 230+ stock avatars vs Arcads' 1,000+ actors but in a more polished L&D aesthetic, 140+ language voice library vs Arcads' English-first library, SOC 2 Type II + SAML SSO + SCORM export for enterprise procurement, AI Copilot to generate scripts from URLs and documents, Video Agents for real-time avatar conversations, PowerPoint-to-video import, interactive video features (quizzes, branching, CTAs), Personal and Studio custom avatars, long-form support (10-30 minute videos), a free plan, and a starter tier at $18/month. Synthesia is a full L&D platform; Arcads is a focused UGC ad creative tool.

Which has better avatar realism, Synthesia or Arcads?

It depends on what realism means for your use case. Synthesia's Express-2 avatars are best-in-class for corporate realism — accurate lip-sync across long-form video, consistent posture, professional delivery. Arcads' AI actors are best-in-class for UGC realism — the natural eye contact, hand gestures, breathing, micro-expressions, and verbal fillers that read as a real person rather than a brand spokesperson. Run them side by side and Synthesia looks more polished; Arcads looks more human. For a podium or a teleprompter, Synthesia wins. For a phone-held selfie in a kitchen, Arcads wins.
