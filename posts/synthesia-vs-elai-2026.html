Synthesia vs Elai 2026: Which AI Training Video Tool Is Better?

📅 Published: May 27, 2026 🕑 12 min read ✍️ Tom Tran

Quick Answer

Synthesia is better for non-SCORM training video at scale — 230+ avatars, 140+ languages, polished interface, from $18/month (Starter annual, 120 credits/year). Elai is better when you need SCORM export, interactive quizzes, or custom avatars without an enterprise contract — all available on the Advanced plan at $100/month. The core trade-off: Synthesia gates SCORM behind Enterprise (custom pricing); Elai includes SCORM at a fixed price. For L&D teams deploying to an LMS, Elai Advanced ($100/mo) is the smarter spend unless you need Synthesia's avatar variety at scale.

Tools at a Glance

Both Synthesia and Elai are AI avatar video platforms built for corporate training, onboarding, and L&D content. They share the same core workflow — type a script, pick an avatar, render a video — but they diverge sharply on price tiers and which features unlock at which plan.

Synthesia launched in 2017 and is the dominant name in enterprise L&D video. It has 230+ stock avatars, 140+ languages, and a polished interface trusted by companies like Zoom, Reuters, and Heineken. The catch: SCORM export and custom avatars require either Creator ($64/month annual) or Enterprise (custom) plans. Synthesia's free tier gives you 3 minutes of video per month.

Elai (formerly VideoAI.io) is a focused training video platform that packages SCORM export, interactive quizzes, branching scenarios, and LMS integration into a single Advanced plan at $100/month. It has fewer stock avatars (80+) and languages (75+) but is a more complete eLearning authoring tool. Free tier: 1 video per month (up to 1 minute).

Avatars & Customisation

Synthesia leads on avatar volume and diversity. Its 230+ stock avatars span professional, casual, diverse age groups and ethnicities. All stock avatars are available on every paid plan including the $18/month Starter. Custom avatar creation (uploading a consent video to clone your likeness) is available on Creator ($64/month) and above.

Elai offers 80+ stock avatars — fewer, but diverse and professionally shot. Custom avatar creation requires the Advanced plan ($100/month). Elai also supports custom voice cloning in 28 languages, which Synthesia does not offer at comparable price points.

If avatar variety and realism are your primary requirement and you don't need SCORM, Synthesia's Creator plan at $64/month is hard to beat. If you need SCORM alongside custom avatars, Elai Advanced at $100/month is actually the cheaper option compared to Synthesia Enterprise.

SCORM & LMS Integration

This is the clearest differentiator between the two platforms.

Synthesia offers SCORM 1.2 and SCORM 2004 export, but only on its Enterprise plan. There is no fixed Enterprise price — it's a custom quote process that typically starts at $200–300/month for small teams and scales with user volume. For most L&D teams, this means SCORM on Synthesia is out of reach unless they have a procurement budget and can commit to an annual enterprise contract.

Elai includes SCORM 1.2 export, SCORM 2004, xAPI (Tin Can), and LMS integration on its Advanced plan at $100/month. Compatible platforms include Moodle, TalentLMS, Cornerstone, Docebo, Blackboard, Canvas, and most major LMS providers. Elai also supports progress tracking, quiz scoring, and completion reporting that flows back to the LMS — features critical for compliance training.

If you need to upload training videos to an LMS with SCORM tracking, Elai wins this category outright. $100/month gives you a known, fixed budget. Synthesia's approach gates SCORM behind enterprise contracts that require negotiation and typically take weeks to finalise.

Interactive Video Features

Elai is the clear winner here. The Advanced plan includes:

  • In-video quizzes with multiple choice and true/false questions
  • Branching scenarios — viewers make decisions that lead to different video paths
  • Clickable hotspots on video frames
  • Quiz scoring that syncs back to your LMS via SCORM tracking
  • Pass/fail thresholds for compliance training completion

Synthesia does not offer interactive video features on any plan. Synthesia produces linear videos — play-through content with no learner interaction. For pure knowledge transfer where interaction isn't required (announcements, product demos, process overviews), Synthesia's linear output is perfectly adequate. But for skills assessment and compliance training where you need quiz results tracked in an LMS, Elai's interactive layer is essential.

Verdict

The decision comes down to one question: do you need SCORM?

If your training videos go straight to YouTube, a shared drive, or an intranet without LMS tracking — choose Synthesia. The $18/month Starter plan gives you a generous entry point, 230+ avatars, and 140+ languages. It's also the better tool for creating internal communications, product announcement videos, and non-eLearning content where avatar quality and variety matter more than tracking.

If your training videos need to live inside an LMS with quiz scores, completion tracking, pass/fail thresholds, and SCORM compliance — choose Elai. The $100/month Advanced plan is more expensive than Synthesia's Starter, but it's dramatically cheaper than Synthesia Enterprise. You get SCORM, xAPI, interactive quizzes, custom avatars, voice cloning in 28 languages, and unlimited videos in one package. For an L&D team with a known LMS deployment requirement, Elai Advanced is the clearer value.

The best path if you're unsure: start with Synthesia's free tier to experience avatar quality, then try Elai's free plan to test the SCORM workflow. The interactive quiz experience in Elai is immediately apparent — if you need it, it's obvious within minutes of testing.

FAQ

Is Synthesia or Elai better for corporate training?

It depends on your SCORM needs. Synthesia is better for non-SCORM training video starting at $18/month with 230+ avatars and 140+ languages. Elai is better for L&D teams that need LMS deployment — SCORM, interactive quizzes, and custom avatars all come in one Advanced plan at $100/month.

Does Synthesia support SCORM?

Yes, but only on Synthesia's Enterprise plan (custom pricing). SCORM is not available on Starter ($18/month) or Creator ($64/month). If SCORM is essential and you can't commit to an enterprise contract, Elai's fixed-price Advanced plan at $100/month is the practical alternative.

How much does Synthesia cost vs Elai?

Synthesia: $18/month (Starter annual), $64/month (Creator annual), Enterprise (custom). Elai: $23/month (Basic annual), $100/month (Advanced, includes SCORM + quizzes + custom avatars), Enterprise (custom). Elai costs more to start but includes far more L&D features at the $100/month tier than Synthesia's $64/month Creator plan.

Which tool has more avatars, Synthesia or Elai?

Synthesia has 230+ stock avatars on all plans. Elai has 80+ stock avatars. For sheer avatar variety and realism, Synthesia is the stronger option.

Can Elai create interactive training videos?

Yes. Elai Advanced ($100/month) includes in-video quizzes, branching scenarios, and clickable hotspots with LMS score reporting. Synthesia does not offer interactive video features on any plan.

Which is better for multilingual training content?

Synthesia supports 140+ languages vs Elai's 75+. For organisations that need to localise training across many countries, Synthesia's language library is larger. Elai supports the major business languages and includes voice cloning in 28 languages on the Advanced plan.
