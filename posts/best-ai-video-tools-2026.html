5. Descript — Best for Podcasters & Video Editors

Score: 8.3/10

Descript is the only tool in our top 10 that approaches professional video editing rather than just AI video generation. The core innovation: edit your video like a Google Doc. Every word in the transcript is linked to the video timeline, so deleting a sentence from the transcript cuts that segment from the video. This makes long-form editing 5–10× faster for anyone who is more comfortable typing than scrubbing a timeline.

The Overdub voice cloning feature lets you fix spoken mistakes by typing the correct words — your AI voice reads them in your tone. The Underlord AI suite adds summarization, filler word removal, and auto-chapter generation.

Pros

  • Edit video by editing transcript

  • Best filler-word removal tested
  • Podcast + video in one tool
  • Free tier for starters

Cons

  • Not a video generator — needs existing footage
