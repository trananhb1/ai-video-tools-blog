HeyGen vs Elai: Overview

HeyGen and Elai are both AI avatar video platforms that turn text into presenter-led video without a camera or actor. They overlap on surface features — avatars, voiceovers, templates — but they are optimised for different production contexts.

HeyGen is built for marketers and content creators. Avatar V delivers the highest avatar realism we have tested, with micro-expressions and natural movement that hold up next to real footage. The platform handles 175+ languages with lip-sync, making it the go-to for brands that need the same video in multiple markets. Full HeyGen review →

Elai is built for L&D teams and instructional designers. The slide-based editor imports PowerPoint decks directly and publishes SCORM packages compatible with Moodle, TalentLMS, Cornerstone, and Docebo — a workflow HeyGen does not offer at any price. Interactive quizzes and branching scenarios let learners make choices inside the video. Full Elai review →

The decision is almost always clear once you identify your primary use case. The exceptions are teams that do both marketing and training — below we map out those scenarios. Avatar Quality

Avatar quality is where the gap between these two tools is most visible.

HeyGen's Avatar V is the most expressive AI avatar available on a commercial platform as of May 2026. Natural head tilts, blinking cadence, micro-expressions between sentences, and subtle hand gestures are all present without any configuration. For a viewer watching a marketing video or a personalised sales email, the avatar reads as genuinely lifelike. This is HeyGen's most defensible competitive advantage — it is ahead of Synthesia, Elai, and most other platforms on pure realism.

Elai's avatars are professional and appropriate for corporate contexts, but they are less expressive. The emphasis is on consistency and neutrality — qualities that work well for training content where the learner focuses on the information, not the presenter. Elai recently added Selfie Avatars (photo-based) and Studio Avatars (green-screen recorded), with the Studio option giving the best output quality. Custom avatar creation requires the Advanced plan ($100/month).

For teams deciding on avatar quality alone: HeyGen is the clear choice for any external-facing content. For internal training where presenter neutrality is fine, Elai's stock avatars are adequate.

Editor and Templates

HeyGen uses a timeline-based video editor. You paste a script, choose an avatar and background, and the platform builds a video scene. Multiple scenes can be sequenced on a timeline. The editor supports text overlays, images, B-roll from a stock library, and uploaded footage. It is optimised for short-to-medium marketing and social content (under 10 minutes).

Elai uses a slide-based editor modelled on presentation software. Each slide is a video scene. You can import a PowerPoint deck and Elai will map slides to scenes automatically, with the AI generating voiceover from your slide notes. This matters enormously for L&D teams: most training content is written in PowerPoint, and Elai's import feature cuts production time significantly. There are 150+ templates for common training formats — onboarding, compliance, product training.

Both have a learning curve of roughly 20-30 minutes to produce a first decent video. HeyGen's template library is larger (300+ vs 150+), but Elai's templates are more relevant for training scenarios.

Training and LMS Features

This is where Elai is purpose-built and HeyGen is not.

Elai's training features include:

  • SCORM export (Advanced plan, $100/mo) — works with Moodle, Cornerstone, TalentLMS, Docebo
  • Interactive quizzes embedded in video — multiple choice, drag-and-drop, fill-in-the-blank
  • Branching scenarios — learners make decisions and the video path changes based on choices
  • Completion tracking and analytics — pass/fail rates, time-on-slide, drop-off points
  • Automated translation with voiceover in 75+ languages — single source video, localized automatically

HeyGen's training features are limited. You can produce a training video — the output quality is excellent — but HeyGen cannot export SCORM packages, does not support interactive quizzes, and has no native LMS integration. If your training video is a standalone watch-only asset, HeyGen works. If it needs to live inside an LMS with completion tracking, Elai is necessary.

For enterprise L&D teams comparing the SCORM landscape: Elai Advanced at $100/month offers SCORM export that Synthesia gates behind Enterprise pricing. If SCORM is the requirement, Elai is substantially more accessible than the alternatives.

Language Support

HeyGen: 175+ languages with lip-sync. When you translate a video in HeyGen, the avatar's mouth movements are recomputed to match the target language's phonetics, and the original speaker's voice is preserved via voice cloning. The result is a translated video where the avatar appears to be speaking naturally in the new language. This is the standard for international marketing campaigns.

Elai: 75+ languages via TTS, voice cloning in 28 languages. Translation produces a narrated video where an AI voice delivers the translated script. The avatar's lip movements are not updated to match the target language — they remain calibrated for the source. For training content viewed by internal teams, this level of localization is usually acceptable. For consumer-facing content, the visual mismatch is noticeable.

If you need to take a 3-minute product walkthrough from English to Spanish, French, German, and Japanese simultaneously, HeyGen's translation output is more polished. For localizing internal SOPs or onboarding videos, Elai's narration-based translation gets the job done at lower cost.

Verdict

These tools are not competing for the same customer. HeyGen and Elai happen to share an entry price point, which makes the comparison natural — but the decision is usually obvious once you define the output.

If a video will be watched by customers, appear on social media, or need to impress external audiences, use HeyGen. Avatar V is the best available, and the lip-sync translation feature has no equivalent at this price point.

If the video goes into an LMS, requires SCORM for completion tracking, or needs interactive quiz elements for a corporate learning program, use Elai. At $100/month (Advanced), Elai provides SCORM, API access, custom avatars, and 1080p output in one plan — capabilities that would cost significantly more on Synthesia Enterprise.

The only team that genuinely needs to evaluate both is one that does external marketing AND internal training at scale. In that case: run both free trials against your actual use case.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is HeyGen better than Elai?

HeyGen produces more realistic avatars and is better for marketing videos, social media content, and brand communications. Elai is better for corporate training, LMS integration, and SCORM-compliant eLearning. They are near-identical in entry price (~$23-24/month) but serve different production workflows.

Does Elai support SCORM export?

Yes. Elai supports SCORM export on its Advanced plan ($100/month), making it compatible with Moodle, TalentLMS, Cornerstone, and Docebo. HeyGen does not offer SCORM export on any plan.

Which is cheaper, HeyGen or Elai?

At entry level they are nearly identical: HeyGen Creator $24/month (annual) vs Elai Basic $23/month. At mid-tier, HeyGen Creator is $67/month vs Elai Advanced $100/month — but Elai Advanced includes SCORM and API that HeyGen doesn't offer until $149/month.

Can I create a custom avatar on both platforms?

HeyGen includes custom avatar creation on its Creator plan ($24/month). Elai requires the Advanced plan ($100/month) for custom avatars. HeyGen's entry-level custom avatar access is a meaningful cost advantage for teams that need a personal avatar without heavy training content requirements.

Which tool has better language support?

HeyGen supports 175+ languages with lip-synced translation. Elai supports 75+ languages via TTS narration with voice cloning in 28 languages. For global marketing, HeyGen's lip-sync is significantly better. For internal training localization, Elai's 75 languages are usually sufficient.

Does HeyGen have interactive video features?

HeyGen focuses on linear video production. Elai offers interactive quizzes, branching scenarios, and clickable hotspots built into the video — designed for L&D applications where learners make decisions within the viewing experience.
