What Is Elai?

Elai is an AI avatar video platform built specifically for structured training and educational content. You write a script (or import a slide deck), pick an AI avatar presenter from a library of 80+ stock options, and Elai generates a narrated video with synchronized lip movement, professional backgrounds, and text overlays. The distinguishing feature is its slide-based editor — instead of a timeline-based interface, you build videos scene-by-scene in a format that feels like PowerPoint, which dramatically lowers the learning curve for L&D teams.

Founded in 2021, Elai has grown steadily in the corporate training space. It is not as widely known as Synthesia or HeyGen, but it competes directly with Synthesia for the L&D market and undercuts it on price. Over 2,000 organizations use Elai to produce training modules, onboarding sequences, product tutorials, and compliance videos — all without a camera or production team.

The 2026 update improved custom avatar expressiveness significantly, with better hand gesture synchronization and more natural idle animations. The translation pipeline was also refined, with measurably better lip-sync accuracy across 80+ languages compared to the 2025 version.

Who Is Elai For?

Elai is purpose-built for a specific buyer: training and L&D professionals who need to produce structured, multi-scene video at scale without a production background. The typical Elai user is not a video editor. They are an instructional designer, an HR manager, a product trainer, or a content developer who has existing PowerPoint materials and needs to convert them into video quickly and cheaply.

  • Corporate L&D teams producing onboarding, compliance, and product knowledge modules for LMS platforms
  • Instructional designers who build in slides and want a direct path from slide deck to narrated video
  • HR and people ops teams creating consistent onboarding experiences at scale across locations
  • Multinational organizations that need training content in 10+ languages without hiring voice actors per region
  • SMBs and scale-ups that need professional-looking training video but cannot justify Synthesia Enterprise pricing
  • Content creators building educational YouTube channels with a consistent AI presenter

If your primary need is avatar-led training content — not marketing video, not social ads, not cinematic generation — Elai is worth serious evaluation. If you need consumer-grade avatar realism or enterprise compliance features (SOC 2 Type II, SSO), look at HeyGen or Synthesia first.

Key Features: What Elai Does Well

Slide-Based Multi-Scene Editor

Build videos scene by scene in a drag-and-drop interface that mirrors Google Slides. Each scene gets its own avatar, background, script, and overlays. For training teams who already think in slides, the learning curve is near-zero. This is Elai's single biggest competitive advantage over Synthesia's more production-style interface.

PowerPoint & Google Slides Import

Upload an existing .pptx or import a Google Slides URL. Elai automatically converts each slide into a video scene and generates an AI avatar narration from your speaker notes. What used to take days of recording and editing can be done in under an hour. We tested a 24-slide compliance deck and had a draft video in 35 minutes.

80+ Stock Avatars

Diverse library covering multiple ethnicities, genders, age ranges, professional styling, and pose options (standing, seated, half-body, full-body). Quality is consistently solid for corporate use. Not as lifelike as HeyGen's Avatar V or HourOne's Reals, but for training content where the script is the focus, the avatars are professional enough not to distract.

One-Click Translation (80+ Languages)

Write content in English, click translate, and Elai generates localized versions in 80+ languages — with translated captions, synchronized lip movement, and culturally appropriate avatar delivery. For multinational organizations deploying compliance or product training across regions, this eliminates weeks of manual localization work.

URL-to-Video

Paste a blog post or help article URL and Elai extracts the content, builds a multi-scene script, and generates a complete avatar-narrated video. Useful for converting existing knowledge base articles into video training modules without rewriting the content.

Screen Recording Integration

Combine avatar narration with screen recordings. The avatar appears alongside the screen capture, guiding viewers through each step. Ideal for software training, IT onboarding, and step-by-step process documentation. This feature closes the gap with tools like Loom for process-based training.

SCORM Export (Enterprise)

Export videos in SCORM-compliant format for direct upload to LMS platforms including Moodle, Cornerstone, TalentLMS, and Docebo. SCORM export is available on the Enterprise plan only — as it is with Synthesia and most AI avatar platforms. Teams on the Basic or Team plans can upload standard MP4 exports to most LMS platforms manually; only formal SCORM packaging requires Enterprise.

Custom Avatar Creation (Advanced)

Submit a studio recording of a real person and Elai produces a digital clone reusable across unlimited videos. The 2026 update improved expressiveness: better hand gesture sync and more natural idle animations. Requires a controlled lighting environment and a 10+ minute recording — higher bar than HeyGen's 15-second Avatar V clone, but output quality is solid for presenter-led training.

Hands-On Testing: How Elai Actually Performs

We evaluated Elai against three real-world training workflows to understand how it holds up in production use.

Test 1: PowerPoint Import Workflow

We imported a 24-slide compliance training deck — standard corporate format with bullet-point text and some diagrams. Elai processed the import in under two minutes, created 24 video scenes, and drafted narration from our speaker notes. The narration quality was high for scenes with full speaker-note text. For slides where speaker notes were sparse or missing, the AI drafted narration from the slide body text — this worked about 70% of the time and produced awkward phrasing 30% of the time (bullet points read as lists rather than natural sentences). We corrected those manually in about 20 minutes. Total time from deck to final video: 55 minutes.

Test 2: Translation into 5 Languages

We used the one-click translation to create German, Spanish, Japanese, French, and Portuguese versions of a 12-scene onboarding video. All five languages processed in under eight minutes total. Lip-sync accuracy was excellent in German, Spanish, and French. Japanese was good but occasionally slightly ahead or behind the avatar's mouth movements during longer compound words. Portuguese was accurate. We spot-checked three scenes per language for caption accuracy — translation quality was professional-grade in all five. For a multilingual compliance rollout, this workflow is genuinely impressive.

Test 3: Avatar Realism Comparison

We generated the same 90-second script using Elai's most lifelike stock avatar, HeyGen's Avatar V (from a 15-second video capture), and a Synthesia Expressive avatar. HeyGen Avatar V was clearly most lifelike — the custom digital twin captured facial expressions and micro-gestures that stock avatars cannot match. Synthesia's Expressive avatars came second with natural eye movement and better upper-body animation. Elai's stock avatar performed well for a structured presentation delivery but showed less expressiveness in emotional passages. For training content, the Elai avatar is professional and credible. For customer-facing marketing or executive communications, HeyGen remains the stronger choice.

Known Limitations

Smaller Avatar Library

Elai's 80+ stock avatars are notably fewer than Synthesia's 230+ or HeyGen's 300+. For organizations with specific diversity requirements or niche appearance needs, the library may feel constrained. Elai partially addresses this with custom avatar creation, but that requires a studio recording session — a meaningful barrier for smaller teams.

Avatar Movements Less Natural Than Competitors

Compared to HeyGen Avatar V or Synthesia's Expressive line, Elai's avatar animations are competent but occasionally stiff. The avatars do not produce the micro-expressions or spontaneous gestures that make HeyGen's output feel like real video. For most training use cases this is acceptable — the script, not the presenter, carries the content. But for high-visibility content where avatar realism is critical, this is a real limitation.

No Real-Time Streaming

Elai is a rendered video platform — you cannot use its avatars for live webinars, real-time conversation interfaces, or interactive AI agents. If you need a live streaming AI avatar, HeyGen's Streaming API or D-ID's interactive avatars are the right tools.

API Less Mature Than D-ID

Elai offers API access on the Advanced plan, but the API is less documented and less capable than D-ID's Talks API for custom integrations. If your use case requires deep API integration (embedding avatar video generation into a custom application), D-ID or HeyGen are the better technical choices.

No SOC 2 or SSO on Standard Plans

Enterprise compliance features (SOC 2 Type II certification, Single Sign-On) are not available below enterprise pricing. For organizations in regulated industries — healthcare, finance, government — that require these certifications, Synthesia is the more appropriate choice.

Who Should NOT Use Elai

  • Marketing teams producing consumer-facing video. Elai's avatar realism and motion quality are optimized for training, not for customer-facing campaigns where avatar quality directly affects brand perception. For marketing, HeyGen or Creatify are more appropriate.
  • Enterprise teams with SOC 2 / SSO requirements below enterprise tier. If your security team requires SOC 2 Type II at a standard commercial pricing tier, Synthesia includes it from Standard ($29/mo) upward.
  • Teams needing live streaming or interactive avatars. Elai is a batch video rendering tool. If you need a real-time AI presenter for live events, demos, or conversational interfaces, use HeyGen's Streaming API or D-ID's interactive avatar product.
  • Social media ad creative teams. Elai is not built for the short, high-variation ad creative workflow. For AI UGC ads, see Arcads vs Creatify vs MakeUGC.
  • Pure text-to-video without avatars. If you need voiceover-driven content repurposing without an on-screen presenter, Fliki or Pictory are simpler and cheaper.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Elai cost in 2026?

Elai's Basic/Creator plan starts at $23/month and includes all 80+ stock avatars, the slide-based editor, branching (Avatar Dialogs), quizzes, and 80+ language translation. The Team plan costs approximately $100/month on annual billing and adds collaboration seats (3 editors + guests), 4K export, 1 Selfie Avatar, and 1 Voice Clone. Enterprise pricing is custom and includes SCORM export. Annual billing saves approximately 20% on lower tiers. Updated May 2026.

Does Elai support SCORM export for LMS?

Yes, but only on the Enterprise plan — SCORM export is not included in the Basic or Team tiers (updated May 2026). This is the same as Synthesia, where SCORM is also Enterprise-only. Teams without a hard SCORM requirement can export standard MP4 video and upload it to most LMS platforms (Moodle, TalentLMS, Cornerstone, Docebo) without needing Enterprise on either platform.

Can Elai import PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. You can upload a .pptx file or import from Google Slides. Elai converts each slide into a video scene and auto-generates avatar narration from your speaker notes. For slides with sparse notes, the AI drafts narration from the slide body — useful but may require editing for bulleted content.

How does Elai compare to Synthesia?

Elai and Synthesia target the same L&D market. Synthesia has a larger avatar library (230+ vs 80+), more language coverage (140+ vs 80+), and includes SOC 2/SSO compliance from Standard tier. Elai's advantages: the slide-based editor is better for existing PowerPoint workflows, SCORM export is available at $100/month (vs Synthesia enterprise only), and it is priced lower for teams that only need standard features. For the full comparison, see our D-ID vs Elai comparison.

What languages does Elai support?

Elai supports 80+ languages with one-click translation. You can write content in English and generate localized versions with translated captions and synchronized lip-sync in any supported language. Particularly strong for European languages and major Asian languages. For 140+ language coverage, Synthesia has broader support.

Is there a free trial for Elai?

Yes. Elai offers a free trial that lets you test the editor, import slides, and generate a limited number of videos with a watermark. No credit card is required to start. The trial is enough to evaluate the workflow and test avatar options before committing to a paid plan.
