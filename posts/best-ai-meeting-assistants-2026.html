How We Ranked These AI Meeting Assistants

Each tool was scored on a 10-point scale across five equally weighted criteria:

  1. Transcription accuracy (20%): Speaker diarization, proper noun recognition, technical term handling. Tested on the same 10 meetings across 3 platforms.
  2. AI summary quality (20%): Actionability, completeness, formatting, and time to delivery. Summaries were blindly rated by two editors.
  3. Integrations (20%): Native CRM sync, project management tools, calendar integration, and Zapier/API support depth.
  4. Ease of use (20%): Onboarding time, bot behaviour, notification noise, and learning curve for new users.
  5. Pricing value (20%): Features per dollar at the entry-level paid plan. Free tiers weighted as a bonus.

Tools with affiliate programs we have applied to are noted — this does not influence rankings. All five tools have affiliate programs; our rankings are based on hands-on testing.
