HeyGen vs Creatify: Overview

On the surface, HeyGen and Creatify both generate AI avatar video from text. In practice, they target different workflows and different buyers.

HeyGen is a general-purpose AI video platform. It excels at professional talking-head content: product demos, sales outreach, training materials, personalized video at scale, and multilingual translation. Avatar V produces the most realistic AI presenters available. The Creator plan at $24/month includes unlimited video creation.

Creatify is purpose-built for performance marketers. Its core feature is generating UGC-style short-form ad creatives directly from a product URL — the AI reads the product page, picks a relevant avatar from its Avatar Aurora library, and produces a short ad showing the avatar holding or using the product. For e-commerce brands running Meta or TikTok ads, this replaces early-stage UGC creator testing.

They're not really competing with each other. Most teams end up using one or both depending on their content mix. Here's the honest breakdown to help you decide which fits your situation. See also our full HeyGen review and full Creatify review.

Where HeyGen Wins

Avatar Realism

HeyGen's Avatar V engine produces the most lifelike AI presenters we've seen in 2026. Micro-expressions, fluid lip-sync, and natural body language make the output difficult to distinguish from real talking-head footage at a glance. For content where your audience scrutinizes the presenter, this matters.

Video Translation with Lip-Sync

Upload any video — not just HeyGen-created content — and translate it into 175+ languages with lips re-rendered to match the translated audio. The original speaker's voice characteristics are preserved. This single feature is why HeyGen is the top earner on this site: it's a category-defining capability that teams pay for month after month.

Unlimited Video Creation

The Creator plan at $24/month (annual) includes unlimited videos with no per-minute cap. For teams producing lots of content — sales outreach sequences, YouTube scripts, multi-language versions of the same video — this makes HeyGen significantly cheaper per video than any per-minute model.

Versatility

HeyGen handles everything from 30-second social clips to 5-minute product demos to full training modules. It integrates with ChatGPT for script generation and with Sora 2 / Veo 3.1 for AI-generated B-roll footage. One tool covers most video content needs.

Where Creatify Wins

Product URL to Ad in Minutes

Creatify's standout feature is URL-to-ad automation. Paste a product URL from Shopify, Amazon, or any e-commerce page — Creatify reads the product name, description, and images, then generates multiple short-form ad creatives featuring AI avatars presenting the product. For brands running constant creative testing on paid social, this workflow replaces weeks of UGC coordinator work.

Avatar Aurora — Product Holding Technology

Creatify's Avatar Aurora can render AI avatars actually holding or wearing products. The avatar's hands hold the product while the script is narrated. This is significantly more convincing for e-commerce ads than a floating product image with a voiceover. No other platform in this comparison has this feature.

Ad Variant Generation at Scale

Creatify generates multiple ad variants from a single brief — different scripts, different avatars, different hooks. For media buyers running creative testing, having 10 variants ready in an hour versus days of briefing and coordination is a meaningful operational advantage.

Price for Ad-Only Use

At $33/month, Creatify is more affordable than hiring UGC creators for initial concept testing. A single UGC creator video can cost $150–$500. Creatify's monthly subscription covers multiple campaigns' worth of creative testing at a fraction of that cost.

Final Verdict

These tools aren't really competing. If you're searching "HeyGen vs Creatify," the question is usually: do I need a general-purpose avatar video platform, or a specialized ad creative engine?

HeyGen wins for professional, versatile avatar video. Avatar V is the best AI presenter engine available. Unlimited video at $24/month is strong value. Video translation with lip-sync is unmatched. If your video serves audiences — marketing content, sales outreach, training, demos — HeyGen is the right primary tool.

Creatify wins for e-commerce ad creation. URL-to-ad automation, Avatar Aurora product holding, and bulk variant generation make it the most efficient way to produce UGC-style ad content without UGC creator costs. If your workflow is primarily paid social ad creative testing, Creatify is built for that.

Many teams use both: HeyGen for long-form brand and training content, Creatify for rapid-cycle ad creative. If you can only afford one, choose based on your primary output — professional video goes to HeyGen, e-commerce ad creative goes to Creatify.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between HeyGen and Creatify?

HeyGen is a professional avatar video platform built for talking-head content, video translation, and marketing videos. Creatify is purpose-built for AI-generated e-commerce ad creatives, using its Avatar Aurora to show products being used by AI people in UGC-style short ads. HeyGen is better for professional video at scale; Creatify is better for product ad testing at scale.

Is Creatify worth it for e-commerce?

Yes, for e-commerce brands running paid ads on Meta or TikTok. Creatify's product URL-to-ad feature generates multiple ad variations from a URL in minutes, with AI avatars holding or using the product. This replaces expensive UGC creators for initial ad testing. At $33/month Starter or $77/month Pro, it's significantly cheaper than hiring UGC creators for every test.

Which is cheaper, HeyGen or Creatify?

HeyGen's Creator plan at $24/month (annual billing) is cheaper than Creatify's Starter at $33/month. HeyGen also includes unlimited video creation; Creatify operates on a credit model. For high-volume video creators, HeyGen is better value per video. For e-commerce ad creators focused on short ad clips, Creatify's pricing is justified by the specialized tooling.

Can HeyGen make UGC-style ads like Creatify?

HeyGen can produce scripted talking-head avatar videos usable as ad content. However, it lacks Creatify's product-holding feature, URL-to-ad automation, and the scale-focused ad variation engine. For UGC-style product ads specifically, Creatify is more purpose-built. HeyGen is better for brand storytelling, tutorials, and professional presenter content.

Does Creatify work for non-e-commerce use cases?

Creatify can produce short talking-head videos beyond product ads, but its toolset is most powerful for e-commerce. The Avatar Aurora feature, product URL integration, and ad-format optimization are built for marketers selling products online. For training videos, long-form content, or professional presentations, HeyGen is better suited.
