Who Each Tool Is For

HeyGen and Creatify both use AI avatars to create videos without a camera, but they are not aimed at the same buyer.

HeyGen started as a talking-head video platform for businesses. Its core product is an AI avatar that reads a script, lip-syncs across 175+ languages, and can be cloned from a 15-second video of yourself. Companies use it for training content, product demos, YouTube channels, and translating existing videos into new languages without re-recording. The Avatar V release in April 2026 brought phoneme-level lip-sync and identity-consistent output across long-form clips and camera angles — the most realistic AI avatar output available from any self-serve tool.

Creatify was built for performance marketers and e-commerce brands. Feed it a product URL and it scrapes the product page — images, title, copy — then generates a short-form UGC-style ad in minutes. The Avatar Aurora feature can show an AI presenter physically holding and demonstrating your product, which no other tool does out of the box. Batch mode lets you generate 50 variants of the same ad at once for A/B testing across Meta and TikTok. The entire workflow is pointed at one goal: reducing cost-per-click by improving ad creative.

The overlap is small. If you need an avatar to read a script, both tools work. Everything else about them is different.

Pricing Breakdown

  • Free: 3 videos/month, up to 3 minutes each, watermarked output
  • Creator — $29/month ($24/month billed annually): unlimited standard videos, 200 Premium Credits/month, Avatar V access, 1080p, no watermark
  • Pro — $99/month ($82/month annual): 2,000 Premium Credits, team seats, priority render

Premium Credits cover HeyGen's advanced features: Avatar V generation (~6 credits/min), video translation with lip-sync (~5 credits/min). The Creator plan's 200 credits gives you roughly 33 minutes of Avatar V video per month.

Creatify Pricing (2026)

  • Free: 10 credits/month, 2 video ads or 20 image ads, watermarked, 100 AI actors, 50 templates
  • Starter — $33/month (50% off annual): 100 credits (~20 videos), no watermark, 700+ avatars
  • Pro — $49/month (50% off annual): 300 credits (~60 videos), 1,500+ avatars, batch mode, ad analytics, Avatar Aurora

Creatify's annual billing discount is steeper (50%) than HeyGen's (~17%). For high-volume ad production, the Pro plan at ~$0.82 per video is hard to beat.

Avatars and Output Quality

HeyGen's Avatar V is the most realistic AI avatar available on a self-serve platform. The 15-second video you provide becomes a digital twin that speaks any of 175+ languages with phoneme-level lip-sync — meaning the mouth movements match the actual sounds of the target language, not just English phonetics mapped onto another language. Across a 10-minute video it stays consistent in identity and expression. That is hard to do, and HeyGen is the only tool doing it at this quality level.

Creatify's Avatar Aurora runs at 24fps and is built for short-form ad content. The key advantage is the product-holding capability: the AI presenter can grip, gesture toward, and interact with your product in a way that mimics authentic UGC creator videos. This matters for conversion rates on Meta and TikTok because it triggers the visual pattern users associate with real reviews. Creatify's 1,500+ avatars on the Pro plan are curated for advertising performance — diverse demographics, realistic ad-style presentation, and regional variants for different markets.

For pure avatar realism, HeyGen wins. For ad-specific avatar variety and product interaction, Creatify wins.

FAQ

Is HeyGen or Creatify better for e-commerce ads?

Creatify is better for e-commerce ads. Its URL-to-video feature pulls product images and copy directly from your product page, Avatar Aurora avatars can hold and present products, and batch mode generates up to 50 ad variants at once for A/B testing. HeyGen lacks all three of those capabilities.

How much does HeyGen cost vs Creatify in 2026?

HeyGen Creator is $29/month ($24 annual). Creatify Starter is $33/month, Pro is $49/month — both with 50% off annual billing. HeyGen is cheaper at entry level. Creatify Pro delivers more video volume for ad production.

Which tool is better for multilingual video?

HeyGen wins clearly. It supports 175+ languages with phoneme-level lip-sync and voice cloning. Creatify supports 29 languages for ad generation but lacks video translation for existing content.

Does Creatify have a free plan?

Yes. Creatify's free plan gives 10 credits/month — enough for 2 video ads or 20 image ads, with a watermark. HeyGen's free plan gives 3 watermarked videos per month, up to 3 minutes each.
