### LCP Root Cause Analysis
The 332% LCP spike (≈1,400ms baseline → 5,668ms) over Jun 28–Jul 4 has three likely causes:
1. **Render-blocking Google Fonts CSS** (fixed this cycle): 12 pages loaded `fonts.googleapis.com` as a synchronous `<link rel="stylesheet">`. Even with `display=swap`, the CSS network request itself blocks paint.
2. **Traffic doubling**: 800 visits vs ~400 prior. More real mobile users on slower connections shifts the p75 LCP upward naturally.
3. **AdSense serving larger ads**: As site traffic grows, AdSense has more signals and may serve heavier banner placements above the fold.

Fix applied: `<link rel="preload" ... as="style" onload="this.onload=null;this.rel='stylesheet'">` removes the render-blocking resource entirely. Text renders in system fonts immediately (font-display:swap was already in the Google Fonts URL).
